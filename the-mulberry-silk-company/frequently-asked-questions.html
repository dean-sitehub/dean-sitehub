Frequently Asked Questions

Answers before you order

Here are the most common questions customers ask about our mulberry silk bedding range, enquiries, pricing and support.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book or make an enquiry?

You can start on our Enquire Now page or contact us directly by email at martin@themulberrysilkco.com. This is the best route if you want help choosing between a Mulberry Silk Comforter, Linen Set, Pillow and Comforter Set, Silky Sleep Set, Cover, Flat Sheet or Silk Sleeping Bag Liner.

What is included in your product range?

Our confirmed product range includes the Mulberry Silk Comforter, Mulberry Silk Linen Set, Mulberry Silk Pillow and Comforter Set, Silky Sleep Set, Mulberry Silk Cover, Mulberry Silk Flat Sheet and Silk Sleeping Bag Liner. You will also see featured collections such as Our Featured Products, Sleep In and Most Loved Products across the brand experience.

Are prices shown online?

Yes, the current showcase includes product pricing to help you compare options quickly. Because catalogue pricing can change, the live hosted shop is the best place to confirm the latest selling price before placing an order.

How does pricing work for sets and individual items?

Some products are sold as complete coordinated sets, while others are available as individual bedding pieces. This gives you the flexibility to build a full silk sleep setup or add one specific item to your bed or travel collection.

How long does it take to hear back after I enquire?

Response times can vary, but the enquiry page and direct email channel are set up so customers can start the conversation quickly. If your question is about choosing the right product, it helps to mention the item name and what kind of sleep setup you want.

What if I am not sure which product suits me?

If you are unsure where to begin, start with what you want to improve: full-bed comfort, a coordinated bedding refresh, pillow-and-comforter pairing, or something lighter for travel. We can point you toward the product that best matches that goal.

Do you offer support after I browse the shop?

Yes. If you need clarification before you buy, you can still reach out by email or use the enquiry page. That is especially helpful if you are comparing a set against an individual product or want to understand the difference between featured options.

Where do I go for live ordering and checkout?

The static website is designed to guide you, but live cart, checkout, order handling and payment are managed through the hosted shop. Visit the Shop page to browse the product snapshot and continue to the live shopping flow.
