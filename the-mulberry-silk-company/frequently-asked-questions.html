Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about mulberry silk bedding, enquiries, pricing, ordering and support.

How do I book or enquire?

You can start on the Enquire Now page or email martin@themulberrysilkco.com. The enquiry route is useful if you want help choosing between a Mulberry Silk Comforter, Linen Set, Pillow and Comforter Set, Silky Sleep Set or other sleep essentials.

What products are available?

The available range includes the Mulberry Silk Comforter, Mulberry Silk Linen Set, Mulberry Silk Pillow and Comforter Set, Silky Sleep Set, Mulberry Silk Cover, Mulberry Silk Flat Sheet and Silk Sleeping Bag Liner. The website also highlights featured and most loved products to help you start with the core range.

What is included in a set?

The wording of each product matters. A set product, such as the Mulberry Silk Linen Set or Mulberry Silk Pillow and Comforter Set, is intended as a coordinated combination rather than a single standalone item, while products like the Flat Sheet or Cover are individual pieces.

How does pricing work?

Pricing is shown by product, so customers can compare individual items and larger coordinated sets clearly. Because some products are single pieces and others are bundled combinations, prices vary according to the item selected.

How long does turnaround take?

The website evidence confirms direct-to-door ordering, but does not publish a fixed turnaround window on this preview site. For the most accurate delivery timing on a specific item, send an enquiry before placing your order.

How do I get support after visiting the site?

Email support is the clearest contact path for product guidance, order-related questions and general help. If you are unsure what to choose, mention the products you are comparing so the response can be more useful and specific.
