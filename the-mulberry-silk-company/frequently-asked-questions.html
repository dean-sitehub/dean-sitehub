Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about shopping the mulberry silk range, placing enquiries and knowing what to expect before you order.

How do I book or make an enquiry?

You can start on the Enquire Now page if you would like help before purchasing. If you are ready to browse the live catalogue, head to the Shop page and continue through to the hosted shop.

What products are included in the range?

The current product focus includes the Mulberry Silk Comforter, Mulberry Silk Linen Set, Mulberry Silk Pillow and Comforter Set, Silky Sleep Set, Mulberry Silk Cover, Mulberry Silk Flat Sheet and Silk Sleeping Bag Liner. You may also see highlighted collections such as Our Featured Products, Sleep In and Most Loved Products.

How is pricing shown?

Pricing is displayed per product so you can compare options clearly. Snapshot prices currently shown on the site include items such as the Mulberry Silk Comforter at 5489.00 and the Mulberry Silk Linen Set at 16317.00, but the hosted shop should be treated as the best place to confirm current live pricing before purchase.

How long does ordering take?

This website is designed to help you understand the range and move quickly into the hosted shop. Product and order timing can vary, so if you need clarity before buying, send an email enquiry and mention the exact item you are interested in.

Can I get support choosing the right product?

Yes. If you are comparing a complete set with a single item, or deciding between bedroom and travel options such as the Silk Sleeping Bag Liner, you can email martin@themulberrysilkco.com for guidance.

Where do I go for checkout and payment?

The live cart, checkout and payment process takes place in the hosted shop environment linked from this website. That keeps this site focused on brand information, product guidance and direct customer enquiries.
