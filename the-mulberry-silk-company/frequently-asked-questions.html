Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers about enquiries, products, pricing visibility, ordering steps and support.

How do I book or enquire?

You can start by visiting our Enquire Now page or emailing martin@themulberrysilkco.com. If you already know which product interests you, include that in your message so the reply can be more specific.

What products do you offer?

Our range includes the Mulberry Silk Comforter, Mulberry Silk Linen Set, Mulberry Silk Pillow and Comforter Set, Silky Sleep Set, Mulberry Silk Cover, Mulberry Silk Flat Sheet and Silk Sleeping Bag Liner. We also highlight featured and most-loved products to make browsing easier.

Is pricing available online?

Yes, selected catalogue pricing is visible on the shop showcase and hosted shop. Because product pricing can change, the latest live shopping information should always be checked through the shop links before you place an order.

What is included in a set?

The exact combination depends on the product name. For example, the Mulberry Silk Pillow and Comforter Set groups matching sleep essentials together, while the Silky Sleep Set is positioned as a compact silk-focused sleep bundle. If you want clarity before ordering, send an enquiry for guidance.

How long does turnaround take?

Specific turnaround times were not supplied in the available business information, so the best option is to enquire directly for current ordering and delivery guidance. We recommend emailing before purchase if timing is important to your order.

How do I get support after browsing?

Email remains the clearest support channel for product questions, order direction and help choosing between options. You can also browse the Shop page to review the collection and then contact us with your shortlist.

Need more help?

If your question is about choosing the right silk bedding combination, featured products or what to order first, get in touch and we will point you in the right direction.
