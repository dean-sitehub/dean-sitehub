Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions customers most often ask before choosing mulberry silk bedding and sleep products.

How do I book or make an enquiry?

You can start by visiting the Enquire Now page or emailing martin@themulberrysilkco.com. This is the best route if you want guidance before purchasing a product or if you need help comparing items in the range.

What products are included in the range?

The current source-backed range includes the Mulberry Silk Comforter, Mulberry Silk Linen Set, Mulberry Silk Pillow and Comforter Set, Silky Sleep Set, Mulberry Silk Cover, Mulberry Silk Flat Sheet, and Silk Sleeping Bag Liner. You may also see featured products and highlighted favourites presented as part of the main catalogue.

How is pricing shown?

Product pricing is displayed per item in the shop snapshot and live hosted shop. Because pricing can change over time, it is worth checking the latest live product page before placing an order or contacting the business if you want confirmation on a specific set.

What happens after I enquire?

After you get in touch, your enquiry can be used to narrow down the right product, bedding combination, or sleep set for your needs. Once you are ready, you can move through to the hosted shop for the live ordering and checkout flow.

What is included in a set?

Each set is named to help customers understand the product grouping, such as the Mulberry Silk Pillow and Comforter Set or the Silky Sleep Set. If you want clarification on what a set covers before ordering, an enquiry is the simplest way to get direct guidance.

How long does delivery take?

The supplied business information confirms direct-to-door delivery, but it does not include fixed turnaround times. For the most accurate timing on your intended order, it is best to contact the business directly before purchase.

Can I get help choosing between products?

Yes. This is one of the most useful reasons to enquire, especially if you are deciding between a single-item purchase, a coordinated bedding set, or a travel-oriented item such as the Silk Sleeping Bag Liner.

How do I get support after visiting the site?

You can reach support through email or by using the enquiry page. The website is designed to guide you toward the correct product category first, then into direct contact if you need help before moving ahead.
