About Us

A closer look at the thinking behind The Mulberry Silk Company and why the range stays focused on the experience of sleeping better at home.

The Mulberry Silk Company exists to make sleep comfort feel more intentional. Rather than trying to be everything to everyone, the business stays centred on mulberry silk bedding and sleep accessories that customers can understand at a glance. That focus gives shoppers a clean, direct path to products such as the Mulberry Silk Comforter, Mulberry Silk Linen Set and Mulberry Silk Flat Sheet without having to wade through unrelated categories.

The story of the company is closely tied to a long-running commitment to direct-to-door ordering and a specialist silk range. Over time, the catalogue has stayed rooted in the idea that good sleep products should feel elegant, practical and easy to shop online. Whether a customer is buying a full Pillow and Comforter Set, trying the Silky Sleep Set or looking for a Silk Sleeping Bag Liner for travel, the experience is meant to feel clear rather than complicated.

What makes The Mulberry Silk Company worth choosing is its disciplined point of view. The business does not dilute its identity with random homeware; it stays close to bedding, softness and coordinated silk sleep essentials. That means customers arrive knowing they are browsing a store built around one idea done properly: helping people bring mulberry silk comfort into daily life.

What drives the business

The driving idea is simple: help people create a softer sleep environment with a specialist silk range instead of overwhelming them with endless options. Product choice, page structure and customer guidance all support that direct approach.

What we focus on

The range remains centred on bedding and sleep accessories such as comforters, covers, sheets, coordinated sets and travel-ready silk liners. That focus gives the business a clear identity and helps customers compare products with confidence.

What customers can expect

Customers can expect a product-led experience, direct online ordering and a strong emphasis on comfort delivered to the door. The promise is not clutter or noise, but a more refined route to shopping for silk sleep essentials.
