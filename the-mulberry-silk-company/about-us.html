About Us

The Mulberry Silk Company is built around the idea that better sleep begins with the feel of the bedding you live with every night.

The Mulberry Silk Company focuses on mulberry silk bedding and sleep products for customers who want a softer, more refined rest experience at home. Its collection brings together comforters, linen sets, pillow and comforter sets, covers, flat sheets, sleep sets and a silk sleeping bag liner, so customers can shop individual items or build a coordinated sleep setup. The brand serves people who care about the details of sleep comfort and want products that feel purposefully selected rather than generic.

The story of the company is rooted in long-term attention to sleep comfort and direct delivery. The supplied brand message shows a business that has been shipping sleep comfort direct to customers for over 20 years, with a product range that stays closely tied to what the name promises: mulberry silk essentials. That consistency gives the business a clear identity and makes it easier for customers to understand what it stands for as soon as they arrive.

What makes The Mulberry Silk Company worth choosing is its specialised focus. The brand does not spread itself across unrelated categories; instead, it centres on mulberry silk bedding and sleep accessories that work together naturally. Customers can browse most loved and featured products, ask product questions directly, and shop with the confidence that the range has been built around one simple idea: sleep success in your hands.
