About The Mulberry Silk Company

The Mulberry Silk Company is built around one clear idea: better sleep starts with better bedding. Our range centres on mulberry silk comforters, coordinated linen sets, pillow combinations, covers and lightweight sleep accessories for customers who want softness, breathability and a more refined sleep experience at home. We serve shoppers who know the difference fabric can make and want that comfort delivered straight to their door.

Our approach is product-led and uncomplicated. Instead of trying to be everything to everyone, we focus on a specialised collection that keeps silk bedding easy to understand, easy to compare and easy to order online. That means customers can browse signature pieces like the Mulberry Silk Comforter, Silky Sleep Set or Silk Sleeping Bag Liner and quickly find the option that suits their room, routine or travel plans.

What drives us is the promise of sleep comfort made practical. We value clean presentation, straightforward service and products that feel calm, luxurious and genuinely useful night after night. From your first browse to your final order enquiry, we aim to make premium mulberry silk bedding feel welcoming, clear and worth coming back for.
