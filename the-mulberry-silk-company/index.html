About The Mulberry Silk Company

The Mulberry Silk Company is dedicated to sleep comfort through a focused range of mulberry silk bedding and sleep accessories. Customers visit the brand when they want a bedroom that feels calmer, softer and more considered, whether they are replacing a single item or refreshing an entire sleep setup. From silk comforters to flat sheets and coordinated sets, the collection is designed to make each layer of the bed feel more inviting.

The business has been shipping sleep comfort direct to customers for over 20 years, and that long-standing focus shows in the way the range stays centred on what people actually use night after night. Instead of trying to be everything to everyone, The Mulberry Silk Company keeps its attention on silk sleep products that are easy to understand and easy to shop. That straightforward approach helps customers compare products clearly and choose the combination that suits their room, routine and comfort preferences.

What drives the company is simple: thoughtful products, direct delivery and a shopping experience that keeps comfort at the centre. The promise behind the brand is not about noise or trends, but about helping customers create a softer place to rest with products they can recognise and trust. Every order begins with the same goal: to put sleep success in your hands with mulberry silk essentials that feel special from the first touch.
