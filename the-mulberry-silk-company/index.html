About The Mulberry Silk Company

The Mulberry Silk Company is built for customers who want their bedroom essentials to feel considered, soft and easy to live with. The range centres on mulberry silk bedding and sleep products, including comforters, linen sets, pillow and comforter sets, flat sheets, covers and a silk sleeping bag liner. Every product on the site is presented clearly so shoppers can understand what they are buying and choose sleep comfort that suits their home or travel routine.

Our approach is simple and product-led: make mulberry silk sleep options easy to browse and order online, then send that comfort directly to your door. Instead of filling the site with distractions, we keep the focus on the pieces people actually want to compare, from a full Mulberry Silk Linen Set to a smaller Silky Sleep Set. That straightforward shopping experience is part of what has helped the business remain closely associated with sleep comfort for over two decades.

What matters most to us is that customers feel confident about what they are choosing for their bed, guest room or travel bag. We value softness, practical luxury and a product range that works together, whether you are starting with a comforter or building out a coordinated set. The promise behind The Mulberry Silk Company is clear: thoughtful mulberry silk products, direct ordering and a calmer path to better rest.

Our Products

Explore our mulberry silk bedding collection, from complete sleep sets to individual silk essentials designed for smoother texture, lighter layering and a more refined sleep experience.

Mulberry Silk Comforter

The Mulberry Silk Comforter is designed for customers who want a cleaner, lighter-feeling bedding layer with a premium finish. It brings the natural appeal of silk into the bedroom in a format that feels substantial without looking bulky. This is the product to choose when the goal is a calm, polished bed that still feels inviting at the end of the day.

Mulberry Silk Linen Set

The Mulberry Silk Linen Set is ideal for shoppers who want a more complete bedroom refresh in one coordinated purchase. It keeps the look of the bed consistent while bringing silk into more of the sleep surface. If you prefer a finished, put-together presentation rather than mixing separate pieces, this set makes that choice easy.

Mulberry Silk Pillow and Comforter Set

This set combines two core sleep essentials for customers who want their bed to feel cohesive from the first touch. Pairing the pillow and comforter together helps create a more complete silk sleep experience without needing to build a combination from scratch. It is a practical option for gift buying, main bedrooms or guest spaces that deserve a more luxurious finish.

Silky Sleep Set

The Silky Sleep Set offers an accessible entry point into the range for customers who want to try silk comfort in a smaller format. It is a smart pick for personal use, a thoughtful present or anyone starting to explore silk bedding before investing in a full set. Mulberry Silk Cover

The Mulberry Silk Cover helps finish the bed with a smooth outer layer that feels refined and visually clean. Mulberry Silk Flat Sheet

The Mulberry Silk Flat Sheet is for shoppers who appreciate a neat, layered bed and want the finish of silk against the skin. Silk Sleeping Bag Liner
