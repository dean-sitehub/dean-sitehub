About The Mulberry Silk Company

The Mulberry Silk Company focuses on one thing that matters every single night: helping customers create a more comfortable place to sleep. Our collection centres on mulberry silk bedding and sleep accessories, from comforters and linen sets to pillows, flat sheets and travel-ready liners. We serve shoppers who want their bedroom essentials to feel gentle, refined and easy to order from home.

Our approach is simple and product-led. We make it easy to understand the range, compare coordinated sets, and choose pieces that suit your sleeping style, whether you are refreshing a full bed or adding one key layer of silk comfort. Every part of the site is built around direct-to-door convenience, so customers can browse, enquire and shop without unnecessary steps.

What keeps people coming back is the clarity of the offer and the comfort behind it. We believe premium bedding should feel inviting rather than complicated, and that good sleep starts with materials chosen for softness and everyday ease. From a full mulberry silk linen set to a silky sleep set or sleeping bag liner, our promise is straightforward: elegant sleep comfort, presented clearly and delivered with care.
