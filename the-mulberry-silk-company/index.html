About The Mulberry Silk Company

The Mulberry Silk Company is focused on one simple idea: helping customers create a calmer, softer place to sleep with a specialised range of mulberry silk bedding and sleep essentials. From comforters and linen sets to flat sheets, covers, and coordinated sleep sets, the business is built around products that make the bedroom feel more considered and more comfortable. It speaks directly to shoppers who know what they want from their bedding and want it delivered without unnecessary complication.

Its product range reflects a practical sleep-first approach rather than a broad general catalogue. The collection keeps the choice clear, with signature pieces like the Mulberry Silk Comforter, Mulberry Silk Linen Set, Mulberry Silk Pillow and Comforter Set, Silky Sleep Set, Mulberry Silk Cover, Mulberry Silk Flat Sheet, and Silk Sleeping Bag Liner all easy to understand at a glance. That clarity makes it easier for customers to choose the right combination for everyday sleep, guest rooms, or travel.

What makes the brand memorable is the way it pairs luxury textile appeal with straightforward service. The message is not about excess or trend; it is about sleep comfort shipped direct to your door and a long-running focus on this specialist product space. Customers come here for the feel of mulberry silk, the convenience of ordering online, and a product selection designed to turn bedtime into something softer and more restorative.
