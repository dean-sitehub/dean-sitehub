Privacy Notice

This notice explains how customer information may be collected and used when you interact with The Mulberry Silk Company website. It is practical guidance and not legal advice.

What information may be collected

We may collect information you choose to share through contact forms, email and normal website use. This can include your name, email address, phone number if supplied, the content of your enquiry and the products you are interested in.

How information is used

Your information may be used to respond to enquiries, help with product selection, provide customer support, maintain website functionality and understand general site use. It may also be used to improve how product information is presented to customers.

Analytics and technical data

Like most websites, this site may collect routine technical information such as browser type, device data, pages visited and general usage patterns. This information is usually used in aggregate form to understand website performance and visitor behaviour.

How long information may be retained

Enquiry and support information may be retained for a reasonable period so that follow-up communication, customer service and normal business administration can be handled properly. Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the enquiry and any applicable legal or operational requirements.

When information may be shared

Information may be shared with trusted service providers that help operate the website, hosted forms, email or ecommerce-related systems, where this is necessary for normal business operation. We do not describe any sharing beyond what is reasonably required to run those services.

Your choices and assistance

If you need help with your information, want to correct details you have sent, or want to ask a privacy-related question, contact martin@themulberrysilkco.com. We may update this notice by publishing a revised version on this page.
