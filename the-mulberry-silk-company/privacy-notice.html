Privacy Notice

This notice explains the practical ways customer information may be collected and used through this website. It is provided as general guidance and is not legal advice.

Information that may be collected

If you contact the business through a form, email or linked service, information such as your name, email address, phone number and message details may be collected. Basic website usage data may also be collected through normal analytics, cookies or server logs when you browse the site.

How information may be used

Customer information may be used to respond to enquiries, provide product guidance, improve the website experience and support ordering-related communication. It may also be used to understand which products or pages attract interest so the site can remain clear and useful.

Email, forms and linked services

When you send an email to martin@themulberrysilkco.com or use a form linked on this site, your message is processed through the relevant email or form service provider. Those services may store information on their systems in order to deliver and manage communications.

Analytics and normal site use

Like most websites, this site may collect standard technical information such as browser type, device type, pages viewed and approximate usage patterns. This information is generally used to understand website performance and improve navigation, content and customer journeys.

How long information may be kept

Enquiry records and related communication may be kept for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain customer service records, manage website operations or comply with legal and business requirements. Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the request and whether further communication is needed.

When information may be shared

Information may be shared with trusted service providers that help operate the website, contact forms, analytics tools, email systems or hosted shop environment. Information is not shared casually; it is only disclosed where reasonably necessary to run the website, respond to customers or meet legal obligations.

Your choices and support

If you would like help with your information, want to update contact details or have a privacy-related question, email martin@themulberrysilkco.com. The business will aim to assist with reasonable requests relating to access, correction or clarification.
