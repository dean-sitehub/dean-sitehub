Privacy Notice

This notice explains, in practical terms, how website and enquiry information may be collected and used by The Mulberry Silk Company. It is provided for general guidance and is not legal advice.

Information that may be collected

If you contact the business through email, forms or linked platforms, information such as your name, email address, phone number and message details may be collected. Standard website usage information, such as browser type, pages visited and general analytics data, may also be collected through normal site operation.

How information may be used

Customer information may be used to answer enquiries, provide product guidance, respond to support questions, improve website performance and help customers complete their intended journey from browsing to ordering. Information may also be used for routine administration and record-keeping.

Email, forms and communications

If you email the business or submit a form, your message and contact details may be stored so the business can respond and keep track of the conversation. Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your enquiry.

Analytics and normal site use

Like most websites, this site may use basic analytics or technical logs to understand traffic patterns, page usage and general performance. This helps identify which pages are useful and where improvements may be needed.

How long information may be kept

Enquiry and communication records may be kept for as long as reasonably necessary to answer your request, maintain customer support records, or meet operational and legal requirements. Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the interaction.

Sharing with service providers

Information may be processed by trusted service providers used for website hosting, forms, email handling, analytics, maps or ecommerce infrastructure. Those providers should only receive the information needed to perform their service functions.

Your choices and requests

If you would like help accessing, correcting or updating your contact information, or if you have a privacy question, you can email martin@themulberrysilkco.com. Reasonable steps can then be taken to assist you.
