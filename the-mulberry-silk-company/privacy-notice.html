Privacy Notice

This privacy notice explains the type of information that may be collected through this website and how it may be used. It is practical website guidance and not legal advice.

What information may be collected

The website may collect information you choose to submit through enquiry forms, email messages, or other direct contact methods. This can include your name, email address, phone number, product interest, message content, and any order-related questions you include.

Information collected through normal website use

Like most websites, normal technical information may also be collected when you browse the site. This can include device type, browser type, pages visited, approximate usage patterns, and basic analytics data used to understand website performance.

How information may be used

Customer information may be used to respond to enquiries, help with product selection, support shopping decisions, improve website performance, and maintain normal website operations. It may also be used to follow up on a request you have made about mulberry silk bedding products or support queries.

How long information may be kept

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry, maintain records of communications, support customer service, or meet operational and legal requirements. Retention periods may differ depending on the nature of the enquiry or transaction.

When information may be shared

Information may be shared with service providers that help operate the website or support related functions, such as hosting, form handling, analytics, ecommerce systems, or communication tools. Information should only be shared where reasonably necessary for those services to function.

Third-party services

This website may use third-party tools such as embedded forms, maps, social links, and a hosted shop experience. Those providers may process information according to their own privacy terms, so customers should review third-party notices where relevant.

Your choices and requests

If you want help with your personal information, would like to update details you have shared, or want to ask how your information has been used in relation to a website enquiry, you can contact the business directly. Requests can be sent to martin@themulberrysilkco.com.

South African context

This page is written as a practical notice for a South African business website. It is intended to help customers understand normal website information handling in plain language.
