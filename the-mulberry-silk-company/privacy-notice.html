How customer information may be collected and used

This notice explains the practical ways information may be handled when you use this website or contact us.

Privacy Notice

Please note: this page is provided for general information and is not legal advice.

1. Information we may collect

When you use contact forms, send an email, browse the site or interact with hosted tools, we may receive information such as your name, email address, message content, device data, browser information and normal website usage data. If you contact us about products such as a Mulberry Silk Comforter, Linen Set or Sleep Set, your enquiry details may also be stored for customer service purposes.

2. How we use information

We may use customer information to respond to enquiries, provide product guidance, improve website performance, maintain records of communication and support the operation of the website and hosted shop experience. Information may also be used to understand which pages and product categories are most useful to visitors.

3. Forms, analytics and normal site use

This website may use embedded forms, analytics tools, maps and hosted shop links supplied by service providers. Those tools may collect technical or behavioural data such as page views, interaction patterns, referral sources and approximate device information in the ordinary course of website operation.

4. How long information may be kept

We may retain enquiry emails, contact form submissions and related records for as long as reasonably necessary to answer questions, manage customer support, maintain business records and comply with legal or operational requirements. Retention periods can vary depending on the type of information and purpose involved.

5. When information may be shared

Information may be shared with trusted service providers who help operate the website, forms, hosting, analytics, ecommerce flow or communication systems. We do not describe those providers as using your information for their own unrelated marketing on this page, but they may process data on our behalf as needed to deliver the relevant service.

6. Your choices and requests

If you want help with your personal information, want to correct submitted details or have a privacy-related question, you can contact us at martin@themulberrysilkco.com. We will do our best to respond appropriately within the scope of the systems and information available to us.

7. Updates to this notice

We may update this privacy notice from time to time to reflect operational, technical or legal changes. The latest version published on this page will apply to your use of the website from that point forward.
