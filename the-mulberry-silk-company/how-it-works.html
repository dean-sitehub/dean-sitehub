How It Works

From browsing to better sleep in a few clear steps

We keep the process straightforward so you can focus on choosing the right mulberry silk product for your room, routine or travel plans.

How It Works

1. Explore the range

Start by browsing the core product collection, including the Mulberry Silk Comforter, Mulberry Silk Linen Set, Mulberry Silk Pillow and Comforter Set, Silky Sleep Set, Mulberry Silk Cover, Mulberry Silk Flat Sheet and Silk Sleeping Bag Liner. If you want a quicker entry point, the featured products section is a useful shortcut.

2. Ask questions if needed

If you are unsure which option fits your bed, sleep style or gifting plans, use the enquiry page or email us directly. This is the best stage to ask about how sets compare with individual items and which product makes the strongest starting point.

3. Continue to the live shop

Once you know what you want, move to the hosted shop for live product pages, ordering and checkout. The static website helps you understand the range, while the hosted shop handles the active ecommerce flow.

4. Follow up for support

If you still need guidance after browsing, you can continue the conversation by email. Support is especially helpful when you are deciding between a full silk bedding refresh and a smaller, focused purchase.

What to expect

Expect a product-led experience with simple navigation, clear category names and direct ways to get in touch. We do not overcomplicate the process with unnecessary steps or cluttered product messaging. The goal is to help you understand the collection quickly and move toward the right purchase with confidence.

Because the brand is centred on direct-to-door ordering, the enquiry process and the hosted shop work together. One helps with questions, the other handles live shopping. That makes it easier to get both guidance and convenience in the same journey.
