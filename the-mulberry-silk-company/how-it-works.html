How It Works

A simple path from browsing to enquiry, so you can choose the right mulberry silk product with confidence.

1

Browse the range

Start by exploring the main products, including the Mulberry Silk Comforter, Linen Set, Pillow and Comforter Set, Silky Sleep Set, Cover, Flat Sheet and Silk Sleeping Bag Liner. If you already know what you need, the shop snapshot is the fastest route to the relevant product page.

2

Send an enquiry if needed

If you want help comparing products or understanding which item is right for your room or travel needs, use the enquiry page or email directly. This is especially helpful when choosing between a single silk item and a coordinated set.

3

Get product guidance

Your enquiry can be answered with product-focused guidance based on the items you mention. The more specific you are about what you are considering, the easier it is to point you toward the most suitable mulberry silk option.

4

Move to ordering

When you are ready, continue through the hosted shop for live ordering and checkout handling. That keeps the main website clean and informative while directing customers to the dedicated buying flow for current product pages.

5

Receive direct-to-door delivery

The business positioning centres on shipping sleep comfort direct to your door. Once you have chosen the right product, the process moves from selection to delivery with a focus on making premium sleep comfort easy to access online.

6

Ask for follow-up support

If you still have questions after browsing, return to the enquiry page or email the business for support. Product-led follow-up keeps the process straightforward and centred on helping you shop the silk range with clarity.
