How It Works

From first browse to final order, here is the simplest way to shop The Mulberry Silk Company range and get support when you need it.

1

Browse the range

Start with the home page or go straight to the shop showcase to compare the main categories. You can look through complete bedding combinations such as the Mulberry Silk Linen Set, or focus on single items like the Flat Sheet or Cover.

2

Send an enquiry if needed

If you want clarity before purchase, use the enquiry page or email directly. This is useful when you are deciding between a comforter-only option, a coordinated pillow and comforter set, or a smaller entry product such as the Silky Sleep Set.

3

Move to the hosted shop

When you are ready to continue, follow the links into the hosted shop. That environment is where you will find live product pages, current pricing and the full checkout flow.

4

Complete your order

Once inside the hosted shop, select your item and continue through the order process. This website does not simulate a cart or payment step, which keeps the journey cleaner and ensures buyers land in the correct live store environment.

5

Get follow-up support

If you need assistance before or after browsing, contact the business by email. Including the exact product name in your message makes it easier to answer your question quickly and clearly.
