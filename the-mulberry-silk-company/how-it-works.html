How It Works

A straightforward path from browsing mulberry silk products to making an enquiry and completing your order.

1

Browse the range

Start on the home page or shop page to review the visible product range. You can compare options such as the Mulberry Silk Comforter, Linen Set, Pillow and Comforter Set, Flat Sheet, Cover, Silky Sleep Set, and Silk Sleeping Bag Liner.

2

Shortlist what suits you

Once you know whether you want a single bedding piece, a coordinated set, or a travel-friendly silk item, narrow your options. The product-led layout is meant to make this choice easier without forcing you through unrelated categories.

3

Send an enquiry if needed

If you want help before buying, use the enquiry page or email contact. This is useful if you need clarity on how products fit together or which item best matches your intended sleep setup.

4

Move to the live shop

When you are ready to buy, continue to the hosted shop experience. That is where the live product links, purchase flow, and final ordering steps are handled.

5

Receive direct-to-door delivery

The business positioning centres around shipping sleep comfort direct to your door. If you need the latest delivery timing for a specific order, the best route is to ask before finalising your purchase.

6

Come back when you want to add more

Because the range includes both standout products and coordinated sets, customers can return later to build out a fuller mulberry silk bedding setup. The store structure supports that gradual, product-by-product approach.

Next Step

If you are unsure where to begin, start with an enquiry and mention the product you are considering. You will have a clearer route into the collection and a better sense of which option suits your sleep needs.
