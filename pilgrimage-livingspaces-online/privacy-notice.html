Privacy Notice

This notice explains what information may be collected through this website and how Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store may use and protect it.

If you contact the business through a form, email or phone enquiry, you may provide your name, contact details and the information you choose to include about your order or request. This may include product interests relating to handcrafted furniture, decor, homeware or textiles and soft furnishings.

How information may be used

Your information may be used to respond to enquiries, assist with orders, provide support, and communicate about products or delivery-related questions. Information may also be used to improve customer service and understand what kinds of products visitors are most interested in.

Analytics and website data

Like many websites, this site may collect limited technical information such as browser type, device information and general visit activity through standard analytics tools. This helps maintain and improve the site experience, but does not mean the business sells your personal data.

How information is protected

Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store aims to handle personal information carefully and only keep access to it where reasonably necessary for business use. No website can promise absolute security, but reasonable steps may be taken to reduce misuse, unauthorised access or disclosure.

Requests for updates or deletion

If you would like to update your information, ask what information is held, or request deletion where appropriate, please contact the business directly. Requests can be sent to Deankann@gmail.com.
