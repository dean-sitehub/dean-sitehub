Privacy Notice

How website and enquiry information is handled

This notice explains the types of visitor and customer information Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store may receive and how it is used.

Privacy Notice

Information you may provide

When you contact Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store, you may share your name, phone number, email address, delivery area and details about the products you are interested in. If you place an order through the hosted shop, further purchase and delivery information may also be collected as part of fulfilling that order.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, assist with product selection, communicate about orders, discuss delivery questions and provide customer support. It may also be used to improve how the website and buying experience are presented to future visitors.

Website analytics and technical data

Like many websites, this site may collect general technical information such as device type, browser data, pages visited and approximate usage patterns. This kind of information helps maintain website performance and understand which content is most useful, but is not intended to create an unnecessary personal profile of visitors.

How information is protected

Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store aims to handle personal information with reasonable care and to use trusted service providers where forms, hosting or shop tools are involved. No website can promise absolute security, but practical measures are taken to reduce misuse, unauthorised access and avoidable exposure.

Sharing of information

Customer information is used for normal business operations such as communication, website hosting, forms and order handling. It is not shared more widely than necessary for those purposes unless required by law or requested by the customer as part of service delivery.

Your choices

You may request an update or correction if your contact information changes or if submitted details are inaccurate. You may also ask for deletion of information that is no longer needed, subject to any legal or operational requirements that may require limited record keeping.

How to make a request

For privacy questions or data requests, contact Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store at Deankann@gmail.com or call 078 532 0831. Include enough detail for the business to identify your request and respond accurately.
