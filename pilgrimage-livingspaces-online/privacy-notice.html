Privacy Notice

A straightforward explanation of what information may be collected and how it is used.

Your Information and How It Is Used

What may be collected

When you contact Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store, the business may collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, delivery details and the contents of your enquiry or support request. If you place an order, additional purchase-related information may also be needed to process the request.

How the information is used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, assist with orders, confirm delivery details, provide customer support and improve communication around handcrafted furniture, decor and textile purchases. Basic website analytics may also be used to understand how visitors interact with the site.

How information is protected

Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store aims to handle customer information with care and only use it for relevant business purposes. Reasonable steps are taken to reduce misuse, unauthorised access or unnecessary sharing of information.

When information may be shared

Information may be shared only when necessary to support the business process, such as order handling, website forms, analytics tools or delivery coordination. It is not intended to be sold as a general mailing list or unrelated third-party contact database.

Your choices

If your contact details change, or if you want to request access, correction or deletion of information previously shared through this website, you can contact the business directly. Requests will be reviewed and handled as reasonably as possible.
