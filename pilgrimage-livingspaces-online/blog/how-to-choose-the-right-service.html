How to choose the right service

Good shopping decisions often begin with knowing whether you should browse, enquire or move straight to the online store.

Enquire Now

How to choose the right service depends on how clear your needs already are. If you know the category you want, such as handcrafted furniture, decor and homeware, or textiles and soft furnishings, the online shop is a useful place to begin browsing. If you are still shaping the room and want confidence before buying, sending an enquiry may be the better choice.

When to browse the shop

The online shop works well when you want to move through the available categories at your own pace. It is especially helpful for customers who already understand the mood they want and are ready to compare pieces or continue toward purchase. Browsing first can also help you narrow down which category speaks most clearly to your space.

When to send an enquiry

An enquiry is useful when your question is more specific than a standard listing can answer. You may want guidance on how a certain type of piece fits into a room, whether to begin with furniture or decor, or how to balance textured textiles with existing items at home. In these situations, direct contact creates more clarity and often leads to a better decision.

When to use information pages

The information pages are helpful for understanding the store's categories and common shopping questions before you commit. They are a practical middle step for customers who want more context but are not yet ready to enquire directly.

Practical takeaway

If you know what you want, shop by category. If you know the feeling you want but not the exact piece, enquire first and let the selection become more focused from there.
