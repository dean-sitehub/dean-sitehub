How to choose the right service

When you are improving a room, it is easy to jump straight into buying without deciding what the space actually needs. Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store works across handcrafted furniture, decor and homeware, and textiles and soft furnishings, so the most useful starting point is identifying which layer is missing. Some rooms need an anchor piece, some need atmosphere, and some need softness and cohesion.

Start with furniture when the room lacks structure

If the room feels unfinished or visually weak, begin with handcrafted furniture. A strong furniture piece gives the interior shape and direction, making later choices easier because you are building around something with substance and character.

Choose decor and homeware when the room feels flat

A room can be functional and still feel incomplete. Decor and homeware are the categories to explore when surfaces look bare or when the space needs visual interest, contrast and a more expressive sense of personality.

Use textiles when the room needs warmth

Textiles and soft furnishings are often the final step that makes a home feel welcoming. They add comfort, soften hard edges and help tie furniture and decorative objects together into one cohesive look.

Practical takeaway

Before you buy, ask yourself one question: does this room need structure, story or softness? Your answer will tell you whether to begin with furniture, decor or textiles.
