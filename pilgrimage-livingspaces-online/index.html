About Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store

Pilgrimage Livingspaces is dedicated to offering unique, handcrafted furniture pieces that are ethically sourced from artisans in India and Indonesia. With a focus on sustainability and heritage, we provide furniture that tells a story and adds warmth to your home.

Our journey began with a passion for authentic craftsmanship and the desire to bring soulful pieces to Cape Town. We collaborate closely with talented artisans, ensuring that every item is made with care and attention to detail. Our collection includes a variety of styles that cater to diverse tastes and preferences.

We believe in fair trade practices and are committed to supporting the communities we work with. Our promise to our customers is to deliver quality furniture that not only enhances your space but also contributes positively to the world.
