How to enquire

If you have seen something you love, want help choosing between categories or need more detail before ordering, this is the best place to start. Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store focuses on curated living pieces, so enquiries are welcomed when you want a clearer sense of which items will work best in your home.

You can contact the store by phone or email for product questions, availability guidance and general shopping support. If you prefer, you can also use the contact form below to send a more detailed message about the room, mood or product type you have in mind.

For direct browsing, you can also visit the Online Shop or read more on the Products & Services page before making contact.

What you can ask about

Furniture guidance

Ask about handcrafted furniture pieces that can anchor a room with presence and warmth. This is especially useful if you are planning a living space around a statement item and want a cohesive starting point.

Decor selection

Decor and homeware enquiries can help you refine finishing touches and layered accents. If you are styling shelves, tables or a complete room, guidance can make the curation feel more intentional.

Textile choices

Textiles and soft furnishings often shape the comfort and mood of a room. Enquire if you are looking to soften an interior or add tactile detail that works with your existing furniture and decor.
