Enquire Now

If you would like help choosing handcrafted furniture, decor or textiles, Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store welcomes direct enquiries by phone, email or the contact form below. This page is designed for product questions, styling discussions and delivery-related enquiries rather than live calendar bookings. The team can guide you toward pieces that suit your room, your material preferences and the atmosphere you want to create at home.

What to ask about

You can enquire about handcrafted furniture, decor and homeware, or textiles and soft furnishings from the current range. It is also a good place to ask for help if you are trying to tie several categories together into one cohesive living space. Clear questions about style, room use and visual mood usually make the conversation more productive.

Delivery and practical details

If you are shopping from outside Cape Town or need confidence around packaging and transport, mention your location when you enquire. That helps the store respond with the most useful guidance first. Practical details such as access, room dimensions and timing can also be discussed before you commit to a purchase.
