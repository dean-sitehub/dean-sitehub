Shopping & Store FAQs

This page answers common customer questions about shopping with Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store. Because the collection centres on handcrafted furniture, decor and textiles, buyers often want clarity on ordering, delivery and how to ask about specific pieces before they commit. The guidance below is designed to help you shop with more confidence and know the best way to get in touch.

Can I order online?

Yes. Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store has an online shop showcase and a hosted shop link for live ordering. If you want extra guidance before purchasing, you can also use the enquiry page, phone number or email address to ask about a product category first.

Do you offer delivery?

Customers can enquire directly about delivery arrangements, especially if they are ordering from outside Cape Town. Sharing your location and the type of item you are interested in will help the store respond with the most useful practical information. Handcrafted furniture and delicate decor often benefit from clear delivery communication before checkout.

What is the return policy?

No detailed return policy was supplied in the available business information, so the best approach is to contact Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store directly before buying if you need clarity on returns or exchange conditions. This is especially important for larger furniture pieces or carefully selected decor items where expectations around finish, scale and placement matter.

Do you stock a specific brand or product?

The business is positioned around handcrafted artisan work and curated living spaces rather than around a published list of named brands. If you are searching for a particular type of furniture, decor piece or textile, send an enquiry describing what you need. The store can then advise whether the current range suits your request.

Can I reserve an item?

No formal reservation system is listed in the supplied information. If you have found something you love, contact the store promptly by phone or email and ask about the best next step. A direct enquiry is the most reliable way to check current availability and discuss how to proceed.

What kinds of products do you specialise in?

Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store specialises in handcrafted furniture, decor and homeware, and textiles and soft furnishings. The common thread across all categories is craftsmanship, texture and a sense of story. The range is best suited to customers who want rooms to feel curated rather than mass-produced.

Still need help?

If your question is about a room project, an existing piece in your home, or a category you are trying to narrow down, it is worth getting in touch directly. A short message explaining your space, colour palette or practical needs can save time and lead to a better recommendation. Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store is built around thoughtful selection, so questions are part of the process.
