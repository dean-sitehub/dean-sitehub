Shopping & Store FAQs

Helpful answers for customers shopping Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store for handcrafted furniture, decor and homeware, and textiles and soft furnishings.

Can I order online?

Yes. Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store has an online shop pathway, and this website includes a static shop snapshot that directs you to the hosted SiteHub shop for live ordering. If you would like guidance before buying, you can enquire first and then continue to the shop once you know which category suits your space.

Do you offer delivery?

Customers can contact the store directly to ask about delivery arrangements for specific items or destinations. Because handcrafted furniture, homeware and textiles can vary in size and handling needs, it is sensible to confirm delivery details when you enquire or before completing your purchase.

What is the return policy?

If you need clarity on returns, the best route is to contact Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store directly before purchase so you understand the process that applies to your order. This is especially useful when buying handcrafted pieces, where finish, scale and texture are part of the buying decision.

Do you stock a specific brand or product?

The store is positioned around curated artisan work rather than a mass-brand catalogue. The strongest confirmed categories are handcrafted furniture, decor and homeware, and textiles and soft furnishings. If you are looking for a particular type of piece within those categories, send an enquiry with the kind of item you need.

Can I reserve an item?

If there is a category or piece you do not want to miss, contact the store directly to ask about the best next step. Enquiring first is the most reliable way to confirm how to proceed when you are making a considered purchase for your home.

Still need help?

Reach out if you want advice on ordering, category selection or how to shop the hosted store with more confidence.
