Shopping & Store FAQs

Can I order online?

Yes. Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store has an online shop showcase and hosted shop experience that allows you to explore the collection digitally. If you prefer guidance before buying, you can also enquire directly through the contact options on this site.

Do you offer delivery?

Delivery enquiries are welcome, especially for customers shopping from outside Cape Town. Because furniture, decor and textiles can vary in size and handling needs, it is best to confirm delivery details directly with the store before finalising your purchase.

What is the return policy?

A detailed return arrangement has not been published in the supplied business data. If you have questions about returns, exchanges or product suitability, contact Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store before ordering so expectations are clear for your specific item.

Do you stock a specific brand or product?

The store is positioned around handcrafted furniture, decor and homeware, and textiles and soft furnishings rather than around a published list of brands. If you are looking for a particular style, category or room solution, send an enquiry and describe what you need.

Can I reserve an item?

No formal reservation policy has been confirmed in the supplied information. If you have seen a piece you love, the best next step is to contact the store promptly and ask about availability and the most suitable way to proceed.

What kind of customer is the store best for?

Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store is ideal for people seeking characterful pieces with craft, provenance and a strong sense of place. It suits customers who want more than utility and are building rooms with warmth, tactile detail and story-led styling.
