Shopping & Store FAQs

Helpful answers for customers browsing handcrafted furniture, decor and textiles.

Common Questions

Can I order online?

Yes. Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store offers an online shopping path through the shop page on this website. If you would like clarification on a product category before buying, you can also send an enquiry.

Do you offer delivery?

Delivery enquiries are welcomed, and source information indicates that purchases have been packaged and delivered to customers outside Cape Town. Because furniture, decor and textiles can vary in size and handling needs, it is best to confirm delivery details directly for your order.

What is the return policy?

Return terms can depend on the type of item and the condition in which it is received. If you have questions about returns, contact the business before purchase so expectations are clear for the specific furniture, homeware or textile item you are considering.

Do you stock a specific brand or product?

The focus of Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store is on handcrafted artisan work and curated living spaces, rather than promoting a generic catalogue. If you are searching for a certain style, type of furniture or textile feel, the best route is to enquire directly and describe what you need.

Can I reserve an item?

If you see something you are interested in, contact the business as soon as possible to discuss availability. Reservation arrangements may depend on the item and current demand, so direct communication is the most reliable option.

How do I know which category is right for my space?

Start by deciding what your room needs most: structure, softness or finishing detail. Handcrafted furniture is best for anchoring a room, decor and homeware are ideal for styling and visual balance, and textiles help add comfort and texture.

Still unsure? Reach out for guidance on the right piece for your home.
