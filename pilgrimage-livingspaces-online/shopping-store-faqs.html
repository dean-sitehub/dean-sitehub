Shopping & Store FAQs

Answers to common shopping questions about Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store, from online browsing to deliveries and product enquiries.

Can I order online?

Yes. Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store has an online shop showcase that directs customers to the hosted shop experience for browsing the current catalogue. If you would like help before purchasing, you can also enquire directly by phone or email.

Do you offer delivery?

The business does handle customer orders beyond Cape Town, and review evidence indicates items have been carefully packaged for delivery to Johannesburg. For current delivery arrangements, timing and costs, it is best to contact Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store directly before placing your order.

No detailed return policy has been confirmed in the supplied business information. Because handcrafted furniture, decor and textile pieces can vary in nature, customers should contact the store before purchase for clarification on returns, exchanges and any item-specific conditions.

The store is positioned around curated artisan work rather than a mass-brand catalogue. If you are looking for a particular type of handcrafted furniture, decor item or textile piece, send an enquiry and describe what you need so the team can advise on the closest match.

Can I reserve an item?

No confirmed reservation policy has been supplied for this website. If you are interested in a specific piece, contact Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store directly as soon as possible to ask about current availability and the best next step.

What makes the range different?

Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store focuses on pieces with soul and a story. The collection brings together handcrafted furniture, decor and homeware, and textiles and soft furnishings to help customers create living spaces that feel layered, tactile and personal.
