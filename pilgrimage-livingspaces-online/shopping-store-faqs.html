Shopping & Store FAQs

These answers are designed to help customers shop with more confidence while staying faithful to the information currently available for Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store.

Can I order online?

Yes. Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store has an online shop pathway, and the website includes a shop showcase page that sends customers to the hosted checkout experience for live ordering. If you prefer to ask questions first, you can also enquire directly before making a purchase.

Do you offer delivery?

Delivery support is clearly part of the customer experience, but detailed delivery zones, timelines and pricing were not supplied in the source brief. If delivery is important to your order, it is best to contact the store directly by phone or email before checkout so you can confirm what applies to your location.

What is the return policy?

No source-backed returns policy details were supplied for this site build, so the website does not invent one. The most reliable next step is to contact Pilgrimage Livingspaces online store and ask about returns or exchange conditions for the specific item or category you are considering.

Do you stock a specific brand or product?

The available evidence positions the store around handcrafted furniture, decor and homeware, and textiles and soft furnishings rather than named external brands. If you are searching for a particular style, room type or product direction, send an enquiry describing what you need and the team can advise from the current range.

Can I reserve an item?

No reserve-and-hold policy was provided in the source material, so the website does not promise that service. The best approach is to enquire directly as soon as you find something you like, especially if you are making a considered furniture purchase.

What kinds of items do you sell?

The store focuses on handcrafted furniture, decor and homeware, and textiles and soft furnishings. These categories are brought together as curated living spaces, which means the selection is intended to help customers create rooms with a coherent look and a sense of story.

How do I know where to start?

If you are furnishing a room, begin with the larger furniture pieces and then build with decor and textiles. If you already have the essentials, browse homeware and soft furnishings to add warmth, contrast and finishing detail. You can also use the enquiry page if you want guidance before choosing.
