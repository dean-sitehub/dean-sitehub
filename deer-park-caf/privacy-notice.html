Privacy Notice

This notice explains how information may be collected and used through the Deer Park Café website. It is written as practical guidance for website visitors and is not legal advice.

1. Information that may be collected

If you contact Deer Park Café through a form, email or phone call, the business may receive your name, email address, phone number and the details of your message. Basic technical information such as browser type, device data, page visits and general usage information may also be collected through normal website operation or analytics tools.

2. How your information may be used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, help with customer support, improve website performance, review visitor behaviour in a general way and manage normal business communications. Deer Park Café may also use contact details to answer questions about café visits or service-related enquiries you have sent directly.

3. Contact forms and hosted tools

This website uses a hosted contact form tool to receive website enquiries. When you submit information through that form, your details may be processed by the relevant service provider for delivery, storage and administration of the enquiry.

4. Email and phone communications

If you email Deer Park Café or call the business, the information you provide may be kept in normal communication records so the café can assist you, follow up where necessary and maintain accurate contact history. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive personal information through general contact channels.

5. Retention

Personal information may be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for communication, record-keeping, support, security, legal compliance or operational follow-up. Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the enquiry and any applicable obligations.

6. Sharing with service providers

Information may be shared with trusted service providers involved in website hosting, analytics, form processing, email delivery or related technical support where this is reasonably necessary to operate the website. Deer Park Café does not state that it sells visitor personal information through this website.

7. Cookies and analytics

The website may use standard cookies or analytics technologies to understand how visitors use the site and to help improve functionality. These tools generally focus on technical or aggregated usage data rather than personalised marketing claims on this site.

8. Your choices and requests

If you would like help with information you have submitted, or want to request correction or removal where appropriate, contact Deer Park Café at deerparkcafe@gmail.com or 066 199 9785. The business will review reasonable requests in line with its operational responsibilities and applicable South African requirements.
