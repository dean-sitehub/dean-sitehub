Privacy Notice

How information may be collected and used

This notice explains the practical ways Deer Park Café may handle personal information collected through normal website use. It is general information, not legal advice.

1. Information that may be collected

If you contact Deer Park Café through a form, email, phone call or booking enquiry, the business may receive information such as your name, contact details, message contents and any visit details you choose to provide. Basic website analytics and technical information, such as browser type, approximate device information or page usage data, may also be collected through normal site operation.

2. How the information may be used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, manage booking requests, provide customer support, improve site performance and maintain normal business communication. Deer Park Café may also use the information to understand which pages or services are most useful to visitors.

3. Contact forms, email and phone

When you submit a form or contact the café directly, your details may be stored in email systems, form tools or communication records so that the enquiry can be answered and followed up where needed. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your request.

4. Sharing with service providers

Website hosting providers, analytics providers, form platforms, map services and shop platforms may process limited information as part of operating this website. These service providers handle their own systems and may have separate privacy terms that apply to your use of their tools.

5. Retention

Personal information may be kept for as long as reasonably necessary to reply to your enquiry, manage follow-up communication, maintain records, or meet legitimate business and legal needs. When information is no longer required, it should be deleted or no longer actively used where reasonably practical.

6. Your choices and requests

If you would like to ask what information may have been recorded, request an update, or ask for help with a privacy concern, you can contact Deer Park Café directly. Requests can be sent to deerparkcafe@gmail.com or discussed by phone on 066 199 9785.
