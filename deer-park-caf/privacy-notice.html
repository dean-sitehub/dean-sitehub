Privacy Notice

This notice explains how information may be collected and used when you visit the Deer Park Café website. It is practical guidance, not legal advice.

1. Information that may be collected

If you contact Deer Park Café through the website form, email, phone, or social links, the business may receive details such as your name, contact information, and the content of your message. Basic technical information may also be collected automatically through normal website use, such as browser type, device information, and usage data.

2. How your information may be used

Information may be used to reply to enquiries, provide customer support, respond to visit-related questions, improve the website experience, and manage normal business communications. Deer Park Café does not state that it sells personal information through this website.

3. Contact forms, email, and phone

If you choose to contact the café, the details you provide may be stored in email systems, form tools, or normal business records so that the team can respond. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your enquiry.

4. Analytics and routine website data

Like many websites, this site may use basic analytics or hosting data to understand traffic patterns, page visits, and general site performance. This helps improve content, design, and usability rather than identify visitors personally in most routine cases.

5. Sharing with service providers

Some information may be processed by third-party service providers that support the website, such as form platforms, map providers, hosting services, analytics providers, and the hosted shop platform. These providers only receive the information needed for their part of the service.

6. Retention

Enquiry details and communication records may be kept for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, provide support, maintain records, or manage follow-up communication. Technical website data may be retained according to the settings of the relevant hosting or analytics tools.

7. Your choices and requests

If you want help with your information, would like to correct something you submitted, or would prefer the business to delete an enquiry where appropriate, you can contact Deer Park Café directly. Requests can be sent to deerparkcafe@gmail.com or raised by phone on 066 199 9785.

8. Contact the business

For privacy-related questions about this website, contact Deer Park Café at deerparkcafe@gmail.com. The business address listed on this website is 2 Deerpark Dr W, Vredehoek, Cape Town, 8001, South Africa.
