Privacy Notice

This notice explains what information may be collected through the Deer Park Café website and how it may be used.

Information We May Collect

If you contact Deer Park Café through the website form, by email or by phone, the business may receive information such as your name, contact details and the contents of your message. Normal website use may also involve basic technical information such as browser type, device data, page visits and similar analytics information.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, manage communication, improve the website experience and understand how visitors use the site. Where a hosted form, shop link or analytics tool is used, limited information may also be processed by trusted service providers supporting those functions.

Retention

Enquiry information may be kept for as long as reasonably needed to respond, follow up or maintain business records. Technical and analytics data may be stored for shorter or longer periods depending on the service provider involved and the purpose of collection.

Sharing

Deer Park Café does not state that it sells personal information. Information may, however, be shared with service providers who help operate forms, hosting, analytics, maps or the shop platform, but only to the extent needed for those services to function.

Cookies and Analytics

This website may use standard analytics or similar tools that collect general usage information. These tools help the site understand visits, page performance and basic behaviour patterns rather than building a public profile about individual visitors.

Your Choices

You may contact the business to ask about the personal information you have shared, request corrections or ask for help related to your enquiries. If you do not wish to share information through the website form, you may also contact the café directly by phone.

Contact for Privacy Questions

For privacy-related questions or requests, email deerparkcafe@gmail.com or call 066 199 9785. This notice is practical website guidance for a South African business and is not legal advice.
