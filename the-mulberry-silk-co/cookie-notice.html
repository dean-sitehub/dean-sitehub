Cookie Notice

This page explains the kinds of cookies or similar technologies that may be used when you browse The Mulberry Silk Co website and connected services.

Strictly necessary cookies

Some cookies or similar technical tools may be required for the website to function properly. These can help with basic page loading, security, form delivery, and routing visitors to connected services such as the hosted shop. Without these essential functions, parts of the website may not work as expected.

Analytics and measurement

The website may use standard analytics or measurement tools to understand how visitors move through pages, which content is most useful, and whether technical improvements are needed. This type of usage is intended to support website performance and customer experience rather than intrusive profiling. If analytics is enabled through hosting or connected services, those providers may place their own cookies according to their settings.

Embedded or third-party content

This website includes third-party features such as an embedded map, contact form, social links, and links to a hosted shop. Those services may use cookies or related technologies when you interact with them, even if you do not complete a purchase or submit a form. Their cookie practices are controlled by those third-party providers, not solely by The Mulberry Silk Co.

How to manage cookies

Most browsers allow you to review, block, or delete cookies through browser settings. You can usually choose to disable some or all cookies, clear existing cookies, or receive alerts before a cookie is stored. Please note that turning cookies off may affect site functionality, form behaviour, embedded content, or your experience on connected ecommerce pages.

No claim of a consent platform

This website notice is provided for transparency, but it does not claim that a dedicated cookie consent platform or granular cookie dashboard is currently in place. Visitors who want stronger control should use their browser tools, privacy settings, and device controls. If you have a question about website cookies, you can contact the business directly.
