About The Mulberry Silk Co.

The Mulberry Silk Co. focuses on sleep products that keep the experience of rest simple and comfortable. The range is centred on mulberry silk essentials, including comforters, linen sets, covers, flat sheets, pillows, and travel-ready pieces for customers who want bedding products delivered directly to their door. Whether someone is refreshing a bedroom or choosing a lighter layer for travel, the collection is built to make the options easy to understand and easy to shop.

The business has been serving customers for more than two decades with a product-led approach that stays close to what people actually need at bedtime. Instead of trying to be everything, The Mulberry Silk Co. keeps its attention on a clear sleep range and presents it in a direct, accessible way. That long-running focus gives customers a straightforward place to browse comforters, sets, sheets, and sleep accessories without the clutter that often comes with broader retail stores.

What guides the brand is a simple promise: ship sleep comfort direct to your door and keep the experience practical from first click to final delivery. The tone is warm, the selection is clear, and the emphasis remains on helping customers choose the right silk sleep item for their home or travel routine. From a full mulberry silk linen set to a silk sleeping bag liner, every item is presented as part of a sleep-focused collection made for everyday use.
