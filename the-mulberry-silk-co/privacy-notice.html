How customer information may be handled

Privacy Notice

This privacy notice explains, in practical terms, what information may be collected through this website and how The Mulberry Silk Co may use it. It is provided for general guidance and is not legal advice.

1. Information that may be collected

If you contact The Mulberry Silk Co through a form, email link, social platform, or other normal website interaction, the business may receive information such as your name, email address, phone number, and the details of your message. If you enquire about products like the Mulberry Silk Comforter or Silky Sleep Set, your enquiry content may also be stored so the business can respond accurately. Standard technical information, such as browser type, device data, and basic site usage patterns, may also be collected through website analytics or hosting logs.

2. How information may be used

Customer information may be used to answer questions, respond to support requests, provide product guidance, follow up on website enquiries, and improve the way the site functions. If you ask about an order or product range, the details you provide may be used to help identify the relevant request. Information may also be reviewed in aggregate to understand which pages or products attract the most interest.

3. Contact forms, email, and direct communication

When you use a contact form or send an email to martin@themulberrysilkco.com, your message is sent through normal communication tools and may be stored in inboxes, website form systems, or customer service records. This helps the business reply to you, keep track of ongoing support, and refer back to previous correspondence where necessary. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive personal information through web forms or email.

4. Analytics and routine website data

Like most websites, this site may use routine analytics, cookies, or server logs to understand visits, page performance, and general user behaviour. This information is normally used to improve the site experience rather than identify you personally. Analytics providers or hosting tools may process this information on behalf of the website operator.

5. How long information may be kept

Enquiry records, support emails, and related communication may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond to your message, manage follow-up requests, maintain business records, or meet legal and operational requirements. Website analytics and technical logs may be retained for shorter or system-defined periods depending on the service provider involved. The business may remove or anonymise information when it is no longer reasonably needed.

6. When information may be shared

Customer information may be shared with trusted service providers only where necessary to run the website, host forms, manage email, support analytics, or process the hosted shop experience. These providers should only use the information for the services they perform. Information may also be disclosed where required by law, to protect legitimate business interests, or to address misuse of the website.

7. Your choices and requests

If you want help with the information you have submitted, or if you need to correct or update an enquiry, you can contact The Mulberry Silk Co directly. The business will review reasonable requests relating to access, correction, or deletion, subject to legal, security, and record-keeping needs. The easiest contact point for privacy-related help is the published email address.

8. Contact for privacy questions

If you have a privacy question or need help relating to your personal information, email martin@themulberrysilkco.com. Including the words “Privacy Request” in the subject line can help your message reach the right attention more quickly.
