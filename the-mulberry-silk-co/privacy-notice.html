Privacy Notice

This notice explains what information may be collected through this website and related customer communication channels, and how The Mulberry Silk Co may use that information in practice.

What information may be collected

If you contact The Mulberry Silk Co through the website form, email, or linked channels, the business may receive details such as your name, email address, phone number, and the contents of your message. Normal website use may also result in technical information being collected, including browser type, device type, IP-related data, and basic page activity. If you continue to a hosted shop or third-party platform, those services may collect additional checkout or account information under their own privacy rules.

How your information may be used

Customer information may be used to respond to enquiries, assist with product selection, process requests, improve website performance, and maintain normal business communication. The business may also use messages and browsing patterns to understand which products attract the most interest and where customers need clearer guidance. Personal information is not collected for unrelated purposes that a normal customer would not reasonably expect.

Email, phone, and contact forms

When you send a message by email or through the embedded contact form, your details may be stored in email systems, form systems, or internal communication records so the business can reply and keep track of the conversation. If you request product help, the business may refer back to your previous messages to provide better support. Please avoid sending sensitive financial or confidential information through open website forms unless specifically requested through a secure channel.

Analytics and normal site use

This website may use standard measurement tools or hosting logs to understand traffic patterns, page performance, and visitor behaviour in an aggregated way. This kind of information helps improve the shopping journey, identify broken pages, and understand which content is useful to visitors. Analytics information is generally used for website management rather than direct personal profiling.

How long information may be kept

Enquiry records, email threads, and customer support messages may be kept for as long as reasonably necessary to answer your request, manage follow-up communication, resolve disputes, and maintain routine business records. Technical website logs may be retained for shorter operational periods, depending on the hosting or platform setup. Information that is no longer needed may be deleted, anonymised, or removed from active systems when practical.

When information may be shared

The Mulberry Silk Co may use service providers to support website hosting, analytics, forms, ecommerce, email, or social communication. In those cases, relevant information may be processed on the business's behalf so those services can function properly. Information may also be disclosed where required by law, to protect legitimate business interests, or to address fraud, misuse, or security concerns.

Your choices and requests

If you would like help accessing, updating, or correcting the information you have shared, you can contact the business directly. You may also ask questions about how your details are being used or request that certain communication be limited where reasonable. To make a privacy-related request, email martin@themulberrysilkco.com.

This notice is general website information and is not legal advice.
