Our Services

Connected vehicle technology, OEM integration support and operational tools tailored to automotive and fleet environments.

Get In Touch

Solutions built for real vehicle operations

3Gen products serves automotive programs, fleet operators and organisations that need robust technology with practical integration value. Each solution is designed to improve visibility, usability, safety or compatibility across vehicles and mobile assets.

e.things Cloud Platform

The e.things platform is for customers who need a central environment for connected vehicle and asset data. It turns fragmented information into dashboards, analytics and integration-ready outputs so teams can act on operational insight rather than chase separate systems.

Telematics + IoT Devices

These OEM-certified devices are designed for businesses that require real-time fleet visibility, CAN bus access and predictive diagnostics. They help uncover vehicle behaviour, monitor performance and support maintenance decisions before problems become costly disruptions.

Multimedia + Infotainment Systems

Infotainment systems are available in 7-inch, 8-inch and 10-inch formats with navigation powered by HERE Maps, Bluetooth and app support. They are a strong fit for OEM programs and vehicle ranges that need a polished in-cabin experience with integrated camera display capability.

Advanced Vehicle Cameras

Connected camera technology suits fleets that want clearer incident records and stronger driver safety oversight. By capturing collisions, duress moments and distraction-related events, these systems help operators respond faster and review patterns that affect risk management.

Asset Tracking Solutions

Tracking options include battery-powered, solar, hardwired and rugged IP67-rated devices for trailers, containers and high-value equipment. This is ideal for businesses that need accurate movement data, better asset accountability and stronger recovery support when items leave planned routes.

Vehicle Accessories

Accessories such as wireless charge mats, intelligent talking alarms and reverse camera systems add practical functionality to modern vehicles. They are suited to customers looking for enhancements that improve convenience while supporting safer, more informed driving.

Safety and Personal Security Devices

TEQ-Secure Safety Pendants and duress buttons are designed for lone workers and mobile teams exposed to personal risk. Real-time GPS tracking and automatic fall detection provide a clearer safety response path when a worker needs urgent assistance.

OEM Integration & Sourcing

This service is for customers who need accessory and visual systems to fit OEM branded programs correctly. 3Gen products supports sourcing, compatibility alignment and the broader supply process so deployments are easier to manage from specification through after-sales support.

Distribution Solutions

Distribution support includes models such as line fit, port fit and direct-to-dealer supply where required by the program. This helps customers move from approved product selection to structured delivery channels that match the automotive supply environment.

Warranty & Service Management

Warranty handling, reverse logistics and technical documentation form part of the broader support value around the supplied technology. For customers, that means a more complete ownership experience rather than being left to manage product issues without a support framework.

Who these services are for

3Gen products is especially relevant to vehicle brands, fleet managers, dealer networks and asset-focused operations that need technology to function reliably within structured automotive programs. The emphasis is not only on product capability, but on how the system fits into installation, compatibility, support and operational reporting.

Customers typically need one of three outcomes: improved visibility over vehicles and assets, stronger safety oversight, or better integrated in-vehicle technology. Because the solutions span hardware, software and support, the offering works well for buyers who want a coordinated technology stack instead of isolated components.

Whether the requirement is smarter fleet diagnostics, a more capable multimedia environment, or a safety device that protects mobile staff, the goal is practical performance. Every service is chosen to help customers run cleaner, more connected and better-informed operations.
