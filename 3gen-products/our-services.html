Our Services

OEM-grade connected vehicle technology for fleet operators, dealers, automotive programs, and businesses that need dependable integration and support.

Technology categories built for real vehicle environments

3Gen products focuses on systems that improve visibility, safety, driver experience, and control across connected vehicles and mobile assets. The range spans software, hardware, accessories, and integration support so customers can build a solution set that fits their operational model rather than forcing unrelated products together.

e.things Cloud Platform

This platform is for customers who want one place to unify telematics and device data from across their operations. It turns raw information into dashboards, alerts, analytics, and integrations that support decision-making, reporting, and future expansion.

Telematics + IoT Devices

These devices are suited to fleets and automotive programs that need accurate data direct from vehicle systems. The outcome is stronger oversight of vehicle health, usage, and performance, with CAN bus-aware hardware that supports more meaningful diagnostics.

Multimedia + Infotainment Systems

For customers focused on in-cabin experience, these integrated systems combine navigation, connectivity, Bluetooth, app support, and camera display functionality. They are intended for vehicles where usability and OEM-style integration both matter.

Advanced Vehicle Cameras

Connected camera systems help businesses record and review safety events quickly and with more context. They are especially useful where driver behaviour, incident investigation, and duty-of-care processes need a stronger evidence trail.

Asset Tracking Solutions

Asset tracking products are designed for trailers, containers, and other valuable mobile assets that need reliable monitoring beyond the cab. Customers benefit from better recovery visibility, more accurate asset utilisation, and tighter control across dispersed operations.

Vehicle Accessories

This category covers practical add-ons that improve convenience and safety, including wireless charge mats, intelligent alarms, and reverse camera systems. It is ideal for customers enhancing vehicle usability without sacrificing fitment quality or compatibility.

Safety and Personal Security Devices

These solutions are aimed at lone-worker safety and emergency response scenarios. GPS-enabled pendants and duress devices support faster awareness and more confident protection of people operating away from direct supervision.

OEM Integration & Sourcing

3Gen products also supports customers who need technology and accessory systems matched to OEM branded programs. That includes sourcing, compatibility alignment, and the structured support that makes rollouts and after-sales processes easier to manage.

Distribution Solutions

Distribution support helps customers align supply with the way their vehicles move through the channel, including line fit, port fit, and direct-to-dealer models mentioned in the business profile. The value is a more organised deployment path that reduces friction between sourcing and fitment.

Warranty & Service Management

Technology supply is supported by practical operational services such as documentation, warranty handling, and reverse logistics coordination. This gives customers a clearer route for ongoing support once systems are installed and in use.

Who these services are for

3Gen products is relevant to fleet operators who need better safety and operational visibility, dealers looking for integrated accessory and multimedia solutions, and automotive programs that require OEM-aligned sourcing. It also suits organisations managing high-value assets, remote workers, or mixed vehicle environments where tracking, cameras, and connected data all need to work together.
