Our Services

Vehicle technology systems built for visibility, integration and practical field use

3Gen products brings together OEM-grade connected vehicle technologies for fleet, dealer and automotive applications where reliability and integration matter.

Get In Touch

Connected solutions across the vehicle lifecycle

Each solution area is designed to solve a different operational need, from obtaining clean telematics data to improving driver safety, in-cabin functionality and asset visibility.

e.things Cloud Platform

This platform is for businesses that need more than raw location pings or disconnected reports from multiple devices. It centralises vehicle and IoT data, supports secure API integrations and helps customers move from fragmented information to a clearer operational picture with analytics and reporting that can actually be used.

It is especially relevant for fleet managers, OEM-aligned programs and service providers that want data to flow into wider business systems. The outcome is better decision-making, more usable insights and a more scalable connected vehicle environment.

Telematics + IoT Devices

These OEM-certified devices are designed for vehicle environments where CAN bus integration and dependable telemetry matter. Rather than treating the vehicle as a simple black box, they pull better quality operational information for diagnostics, status visibility and fleet oversight.

This category is suited to fleets, automotive programs and installations where visibility into usage, health and movement supports maintenance and operational control. Customers gain a more informed view of each connected vehicle and a stronger technical base for fleet reporting.

Multimedia + Infotainment Systems

These systems combine modern driver-facing features with integration-focused design. Available in 7 inch, 8 inch and 10 inch formats, they support navigation powered by HERE Maps, Bluetooth, app connectivity, CAN interfacing and multi-camera display support.

They are a strong fit for OEM programs, branded dealer offerings and vehicle upgrades that require a neater in-vehicle experience than universal aftermarket screens typically provide. The outcome is a more polished cabin interface with better compatibility and user convenience.

Advanced Vehicle Cameras

Connected camera systems are designed to capture and upload important events automatically, including collisions, duress incidents and driver distraction or behaviour triggers. This makes them useful in environments where safety review, accountability and rapid incident understanding are essential.

Fleet operators and safety-conscious vehicle programs benefit from quicker access to event footage and more context around what happened on the road. The result is stronger incident management and better visibility into driving behaviour patterns.

Asset Tracking Solutions

Not every valuable item is permanently wired into a vehicle, which is why this category includes battery-powered, solar, hardwired and IP67-rated trackers for trailers, containers and other high-value assets. The emphasis is on reliable monitoring in different environments and use cases.

These solutions suit logistics, transport and operational teams that need dependable asset location data beyond the prime mover itself. Customers can monitor mobile assets more accurately and reduce blind spots across their broader operation.

Vehicle Accessories

The accessory range includes practical enhancement products such as wireless charge mats, intelligent talking alarms and reverse camera systems. These are selected to improve safety, convenience and everyday usability in the vehicle environment.

This category works well for OEM accessory programs, dealer fitment options and customers who want vehicle additions that feel purposeful rather than gimmicky. The value is a better-equipped vehicle with accessories that contribute to real-world use.

Safety and Personal Security Devices

Products such as the TEQ-Secure Safety Pendant and duress buttons are built to support lone-worker safety through live GPS awareness and automatic fall detection. They help organisations respond faster when a person may be in distress or unable to communicate fully.

These devices are useful for field staff, mobile teams and workplace safety programs where location and emergency signalling are important. The outcome is improved personal safety coverage and a clearer escalation path during incidents.

OEM Integration & Sourcing

3Gen products also supports the sourcing and delivery side of connected vehicle technology, helping customers align visual systems and accessories with OEM branded programs. This is important where compatibility, presentation and product fitment standards cannot be treated casually.

It suits automotive brands, dealer groups and structured distribution programs looking for solutions that fit a wider commercial and technical framework. Customers benefit from a more coherent path from sourcing to installation and support.
