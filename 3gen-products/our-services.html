Our Services

From cloud platforms to in-vehicle hardware, 3Gen products supplies automotive technology that is designed to integrate cleanly, support operations and stay useful after deployment.

Get In Touch

Solutions that connect vehicles, drivers and operations

Each service is aimed at a different part of the connected vehicle ecosystem, whether you need data visibility, driver safety, multimedia integration, asset monitoring or OEM-aligned sourcing support.

e.things Cloud Platform

This cloud platform is built for organisations that need vehicle and device data in one place rather than spread across disconnected tools. It helps turn raw information into dashboards, alerts, analytics and third-party integrations that are easier for fleet, technical and management teams to use in practice.

Telematics + IoT Devices

These devices are designed for customers who need live fleet visibility, CAN bus data and stronger diagnostic insight from the vehicle itself. They support more informed maintenance planning, clearer operational oversight and faster awareness when something changes on the road.

Multimedia + Infotainment Systems

3Gen products supplies integrated infotainment systems in multiple sizes for customers who want a more capable in-vehicle interface without losing compatibility. The result is a cleaner user experience with navigation, Bluetooth, app connectivity and support for camera views within a single platform.

Advanced Vehicle Cameras

These connected camera systems are for operators who need clear evidence when incidents happen and better insight into driver behaviour over time. They help surface collisions, duress events and safety risks quickly so teams can respond, investigate and coach with more confidence.

Asset Tracking Solutions

Asset tracking is suited to trailers, containers and high-value equipment that must stay visible across different operating conditions. With battery-powered, solar and hardwired options, customers can choose a tracking approach that matches how each asset is actually used.

Vehicle Accessories

Accessory solutions add practical usability and safety inside and around the vehicle. Whether the requirement is charging convenience, warning systems or reverse camera capability, these products are selected to complement broader vehicle technology programs rather than sit apart from them.

Safety and Personal Security Devices

These devices are intended for people working alone or in higher-risk situations where immediate visibility matters. GPS tracking, duress alerting and fall detection support faster awareness and a stronger duty-of-care response for mobile staff and field teams.

OEM Integration & Sourcing

This service is for businesses that need hardware and visual systems to fit OEM-branded programs and established distribution routes. 3Gen products helps align sourcing, compatibility, documentation and after-sales support so the final rollout feels intentional rather than pieced together.

Distribution Solutions

Distribution support can include models such as line fit, port fit and direct-to-dealer supply depending on the program requirement. That gives customers a practical path to deployment when technology must move through established automotive channels.

Warranty & Service Management

After implementation, customers still need documentation, warranty handling and service continuity. This layer of support helps reduce disruption, gives stakeholders clearer reference material and keeps the technology useful over its working life.
