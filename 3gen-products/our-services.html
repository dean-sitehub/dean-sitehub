Our Services

From cloud-connected telematics to OEM-aligned accessory sourcing, 3Gen products supplies automotive technology that is designed to integrate cleanly and perform in the field.

Solutions built around real vehicle use

3Gen products serves automotive and fleet buyers who need more than standalone hardware. Each offering is intended to support integration, visibility, compliance alignment or operational control in real vehicle environments.

e.things Cloud Platform

The platform is designed to unify fragmented data from vehicles, devices and connected systems into one operational layer. It is suitable for businesses that want analytics, event intelligence and API-driven integration rather than disconnected reporting tools.

For customers, the main value is clarity. Instead of chasing data across multiple systems, teams get a cleaner view of fleet activity, asset behaviour and actionable trends that can support planning and response.

Telematics + IoT Devices

These OEM-certified devices are intended for vehicle programs that need CAN bus integration, diagnostics and live operational visibility. They are useful for fleets, automotive channels and technical buyers who need data-rich hardware that can support long-term deployment.

The outcome is better awareness of vehicle condition and movement. Customers can use this information to improve maintenance timing, identify issues earlier and manage vehicles with greater confidence.

Multimedia + Infotainment Systems

Available in multiple display sizes, these systems combine navigation powered by HERE Maps, Bluetooth connectivity, app integrations and multi-camera support. They are aimed at buyers who want a more capable in-vehicle interface without compromising OEM-oriented integration.

The customer benefit is a better cabin experience with functional display control, navigation and camera viewing in one environment. This is especially useful where driver usability and branded fitment both matter.

Advanced Vehicle Cameras

Connected cameras automatically capture safety events such as collisions, duress incidents and risky driving behaviour. They are well suited to operations where visual verification, incident response and driver coaching all play a role.

The value is not just recording footage. Customers gain quicker access to critical event evidence and a stronger basis for understanding patterns across a fleet.

Asset Tracking Solutions

Battery-powered, solar, hardwired and IP67-rated trackers give customers options for trailers, containers and other mobile assets that require reliable visibility. This service is intended for operations where location certainty and asset oversight affect planning and risk.

The practical outcome is more dependable asset monitoring with less blind time. That can support utilisation planning, security awareness and operational accountability.

Vehicle Accessories

The accessory category includes reverse camera systems, wireless charge mats and intelligent alarms that add safety and convenience to vehicle programs. These products are intended for customers who want practical enhancements rather than cosmetic add-ons.

Each accessory is selected for utility in everyday vehicle use. The result is a more functional cabin or safer vehicle environment with cleaner integration potential.

Safety and Personal Security Devices

Devices such as the TEQ-Secure Safety Pendant and duress buttons are designed for lone-worker and personal safety scenarios. They offer GPS tracking and automatic fall detection, extending visibility beyond the vehicle itself.

This gives organisations a more direct way to support personnel in mobile or isolated environments. The focus is on fast awareness when assistance may be needed.

OEM Integration & Sourcing

3Gen products supports sourcing and delivery of systems intended to fit within OEM branded programs and vehicle requirements. This includes compatibility thinking, brand alignment and the kind of integration support that helps avoid mismatched components.

For customers, this means a more structured route from requirement to deployment. It is especially relevant where distribution models, technical documentation, warranty processes and after-sales support all need to align.

Additional operational support

Distribution Solutions

Where required, 3Gen products can support different distribution pathways, including line fit, port fit and direct-to-dealer models. This matters for automotive programs that need a supply route aligned to how vehicles are prepared and delivered.

Warranty & Service Management

Support does not end at supply. The business also aligns with documentation, warranty handling, reverse logistics and service-related processes that help customers manage products once they are in operation.
