Our Services

Connected vehicle systems, in-cabin technology, safety devices and integration support selected for automotive programmes, fleets and dealer channels.

Solutions built for real vehicle operations

3Gen products supplies a focused range of automotive technology solutions backed by OEM-grade thinking. Rather than selling isolated devices, the business helps customers select systems that fit vehicle platforms, operational goals and support requirements.

Multimedia + Infotainment Systems

These integrated systems are designed for vehicles that need modern in-cabin functionality with a factory-aligned feel. Customers can expect navigation powered by HERE Maps, Bluetooth, app integrations, multi-camera display support and CAN interfacing that helps the system sit properly within the vehicle environment.

Advanced Vehicle Cameras

Connected camera solutions are intended for operators who need faster incident review and stronger driver safety oversight. The value is practical: critical events such as collisions or duress situations can be captured and uploaded automatically, giving managers a clearer view of what happened and when to respond.

Vehicle Accessories

This category includes practical add-ons such as wireless charge mats, intelligent talking alarms and reverse camera systems. It suits customers who want to improve vehicle usability and safety with accessories that complement rather than complicate the driving experience.

Safety and Personal Security Devices

These devices are for businesses and teams that need lone-worker protection and emergency alert capability. Products such as safety pendants and duress buttons provide GPS visibility and automatic fall detection, helping organisations create a faster, more accountable response path for people in the field.

Core product and support categories

e.things Cloud Platform

This cloud platform is built for customers who need one place to manage and interpret vehicle data. It brings separate data sources together and supports actionable reporting, predictive insight and secure API integration with external systems.

Telematics + IoT Devices

These devices are suitable for fleets and automotive users who need real-time visibility, diagnostics and CAN bus-based vehicle data. The expected outcome is better operational awareness, more informed maintenance planning and a cleaner route to connected fleet reporting.

Asset Tracking Solutions

Asset tracking products are designed for trailers, containers and other high-value mobile assets that need dependable monitoring. Different power and enclosure options make it possible to match the tracker to the job, whether that means battery operation, solar assistance or a hardwired installation.

OEM Integration & Sourcing

This service is for customers who require more than product supply alone. 3Gen products helps source visual and accessory systems that align with OEM branded programmes, while also supporting compatibility, rollout planning and a more complete supply relationship.

Distribution Solutions

Distribution support can be structured around line fit, port fit or direct-to-dealer requirements. That makes the service useful for programmes that need products delivered through an organised automotive channel rather than an ad hoc retail process.

Warranty & Service Management

Support does not stop at delivery. 3Gen products also addresses documentation, warranty processes, reverse logistics and after-sales service expectations, helping customers protect system uptime and keep support responsibilities clear.
