Our Services

From cloud visibility to in-vehicle hardware, 3Gen products helps automotive and fleet customers source, integrate and support technology that fits operational reality.

Get In Touch

Solutions Built Around Vehicle Data, Safety and Integration

3Gen products focuses on connected vehicle systems that need to perform well in fleets, dealer channels and OEM-related programs. Each service combines product capability with practical implementation thinking, so customers can evaluate not only what the hardware does, but how it will work in a broader vehicle, compliance or support environment.

e.things Cloud Platform

This platform is for organisations that need a single place to interpret vehicle and device data. It helps turn multiple feeds into actionable insight, supports predictive analytics and offers secure API connections for customers who need third-party integration rather than isolated dashboards.

Telematics + IoT Devices

These devices are suited to fleet operators, mobility providers and vehicle programs that need live operational visibility. CAN bus integration and diagnostic capability help customers understand more than simple location data, giving them a stronger foundation for vehicle oversight and maintenance decisions.

Multimedia + Infotainment Systems

Integrated infotainment systems are designed for customers who need navigation, Bluetooth, app capability and camera display support in one interface. The value is a cleaner in-cabin experience with features that are intended to align with modern vehicle expectations and OEM-style integration standards.

Advanced Vehicle Cameras

Camera systems are aimed at operations where incident evidence, driver coaching and safety monitoring matter. Automatic capture and upload of key events can shorten investigation time, support risk management and create a clearer picture of what is happening across a fleet.

Asset Tracking Solutions

Tracking products are available for assets that move independently of a powered vehicle, including trailers and containers. With battery, solar and hardwired options, customers can choose a tracking method that suits deployment conditions while improving asset recovery and utilisation visibility.

Vehicle Accessories

Accessories such as wireless charge mats, talking alarms and reverse camera systems support vehicle convenience and safety without treating these upgrades as afterthoughts. This service is best for businesses that want practical enhancements backed by a more considered supply and fitment strategy.

Safety and Personal Security Devices

These devices are intended for lone workers and teams operating away from immediate supervision. Real-time GPS visibility and fall detection support faster awareness when something goes wrong, helping employers add another layer of protection to field-based operations.

OEM Integration & Sourcing

This service helps customers source systems that fit OEM-branded programs and compatibility requirements. It is particularly useful where documentation, compliance alignment, branded program consistency and long-term support are as important as the hardware itself.

Operational Support Around the Product

Beyond the core product categories, 3Gen products supports customers with distribution solutions, warranty and service management, reverse logistics and technical documentation coordination. That means the conversation can extend from selection into rollout, channel delivery and ongoing product support. For customers managing scale, multiple stakeholders or structured vehicle programs, this extra layer helps reduce friction after the initial purchase decision.
