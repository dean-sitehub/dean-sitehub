Connected vehicle systems

Our Services

From cloud visibility and telematics hardware to infotainment, asset tracking and personal safety devices, 3Gen products covers the connected technology layers that modern automotive operations depend on.

Get In Touch

Solutions built around real deployment needs

3Gen products is structured for customers who need more than a box of parts. The service mix is aimed at organisations that want connected vehicle systems, OEM-aligned accessories, cloud visibility and after-sales support to work together with fewer gaps between sourcing, integration and ongoing operation.

e.things Cloud Platform

This platform is for teams that need one place to unify vehicle, camera, telemetry and asset information. It helps fleet operators and automotive programs convert dispersed data into reporting, alerts, integrations and operational insights that are easier to act on.

Telematics + IoT Devices

These devices suit fleets and vehicle programs that require OEM-grade hardware with CAN bus integration support. Customers gain deeper vehicle visibility, diagnostic context and real-time operating data that can support maintenance planning, utilisation tracking and driver oversight.

Multimedia + Infotainment Systems

Designed for branded vehicle environments and integrated cabin upgrades, these systems combine navigation, Bluetooth, app functions and multi-camera display capability. The benefit is a cleaner in-vehicle experience that feels properly installed and aligned with the vehicle ecosystem.

Advanced Vehicle Cameras

Connected camera solutions are intended for fleets that need faster incident understanding and better safety event capture. They automatically record and upload key events, giving operations teams clearer evidence for response, review and driver coaching.

Asset Tracking Solutions

These tracking systems fit trailers, containers and other mobile assets where power conditions and operating environments vary. Customers can choose from battery, solar, hardwired and ruggedised options to maintain more dependable visibility across the asset estate.

Vehicle Accessories

Accessory offerings include reverse camera systems, wireless charge mats and intelligent talking alarms for customers improving safety and convenience inside the vehicle. They are useful for dealer programs, accessory catalogues and vehicle enhancement projects where finish and compatibility matter.

Safety and Personal Security Devices

Devices such as safety pendants and duress buttons are for businesses with mobile, remote or lone-working staff. Real-time GPS tracking and automatic fall detection support a more immediate response path when a worker needs assistance.

OEM Integration & Sourcing

This service is for vehicle brands, distributors and partners that need accessories and visual systems integrated into OEM-aligned programs. The value comes from sourcing guidance, compatibility attention and support for distribution, documentation and post-sale requirements.

Distribution Solutions

3Gen products supports line fit, port fit and direct-to-dealer style distribution needs where deployment routes influence delivery and installation planning. That helps customers choose a supply model that matches program complexity and operational timing.

Warranty & Service Management

Support does not end once hardware is supplied. Warranty handling, technical documentation and reverse-logistics support help customers maintain control over the practical issues that follow implementation.
