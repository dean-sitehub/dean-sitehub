Our Services

OEM-grade vehicle technology solutions built for automotive, fleet and asset visibility requirements

3Gen products combines connected hardware, software and sourcing support so customers can deploy solutions that work inside real vehicle programs and operating environments.

e.things Cloud Platform

This platform is for businesses that need one secure place to work with vehicle and IoT data instead of juggling disconnected systems. It helps users convert raw information into actionable dashboards, predictive analysis, and integration-ready outputs through secure APIs. The result is better visibility across vehicles, assets, and connected workflows.

Telematics + IoT Devices

These devices are suited to fleets and automotive programs that need real-time visibility, cleaner diagnostics, and CAN bus-connected insights. They support data capture from the vehicle itself, allowing customers to monitor usage, understand faults earlier, and improve maintenance planning. For operators, that means fewer blind spots in day-to-day decision-making.

Multimedia + Infotainment Systems

3Gen products supplies fully integrated infotainment systems with navigation, Bluetooth, app connectivity, CAN interfacing, and multi-camera display support. They are designed for customers who want in-cabin digital systems that feel factory-aligned rather than aftermarket improvised. The value is a more seamless driver and passenger experience with functional integration already considered.

Advanced Vehicle Cameras

These connected camera systems automatically capture key events such as collisions, duress triggers, and distraction-related incidents. They are meant for fleets that need faster incident review and stronger behavioural insight without relying purely on manual reporting. The outcome is a clearer safety record and better evidence when something goes wrong.

Asset Tracking Solutions

Asset tracking options include battery-powered, solar, hardwired and IP67-rated devices for trailers, containers and valuable mobile assets. These solutions are for businesses that cannot afford uncertainty around asset location or movement. They improve accountability and help teams manage risk across distributed equipment.

Vehicle Accessories

The accessory range covers products such as wireless charge mats, intelligent talking alarms and reverse camera systems. These are for customers who want practical usability and safety enhancements that fit naturally into the vehicle environment. The benefit is added functionality without losing focus on integration quality.

Safety and Personal Security Devices

The TEQ-Secure Safety Pendant and duress button solutions are intended for lone workers and mobile personnel who may need immediate location-aware assistance. With GPS tracking and automatic fall detection, these devices support faster escalation in vulnerable situations. They help businesses extend duty-of-care beyond the vehicle itself.

OEM Integration & Sourcing

This service is for automotive programs that need visual and accessory systems aligned with OEM requirements. 3Gen products helps source compatible solutions and support delivery models such as line fit, port fit and direct-to-dealer distribution. Customers benefit from a more structured path to implementation, documentation, and after-sales support.

Support beyond supply

3Gen products does more than list products. The business supports distribution, compliance requirements, technical documentation, warranty handling, reverse logistics and service management so customers can evaluate a complete delivery path rather than a box of disconnected components.
