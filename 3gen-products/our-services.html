Connected vehicle product range

Our Services

From cloud-based visibility to in-vehicle hardware, 3Gen products helps automotive and fleet buyers match the right connected technology to the right operating need.

Get In Touch

Solutions built around real vehicle use

3Gen products supplies OEM-grade technologies for businesses that need compatibility, integration support and dependable performance across modern automotive environments. The range is relevant for fleet operators, dealerships, OEM-linked programs and asset-intensive businesses that need visibility, safety, convenience and stronger data flows. Each solution is designed to solve a specific operational problem rather than add technology for its own sake.

e.things Cloud Platform

This platform is for organisations that need one place to interpret data coming from multiple vehicles, devices or operating systems. It converts disconnected streams into a unified view with analytics, event visibility and secure API pathways for broader business integration. The main outcome is better decision-making based on usable data rather than isolated dashboards.

Telematics + IoT Devices

These devices are suited to fleets and automotive programs that need live vehicle information, CAN bus access and deeper diagnostics than basic trackers provide. They support more accurate visibility into vehicle behaviour and system health, which helps with oversight, maintenance planning and operational response. For customers, that means fewer blind spots across the fleet.

Multimedia + Infotainment Systems

Integrated infotainment systems are ideal for programs that want a modern cabin experience with navigation, Bluetooth, app connectivity and camera display support in one interface. Available in multiple screen sizes, they are designed to work with CAN interfacing for a more seamless vehicle fit. Customers benefit from a cleaner, more factory-aligned user experience.

Advanced Vehicle Cameras

Connected camera technology helps operators capture safety-related events quickly and preserve useful footage when incidents occur. It is particularly useful for fleets needing stronger evidence, better incident review and improved understanding of risky driving behaviour. The value is faster insight when something goes wrong and more informed coaching afterward.

Asset Tracking Solutions

Asset tracking products are intended for trailers, containers and other mobile assets that cannot always rely on traditional vehicle-based systems. With options including IP67-rated, battery, solar and hardwired units, businesses can choose the setup that matches how the asset moves and where it is used. The benefit is clearer location intelligence and less uncertainty around high-value equipment.

Vehicle Accessories

This category covers useful in-vehicle upgrades such as wireless charge mats, reverse camera systems and intelligent talking alarms. These products are for buyers who want added convenience and safety without compromising the overall vehicle experience. The result is a more functional vehicle environment for drivers and end users.

Safety and Personal Security Devices

Safety devices such as the TEQ-Secure Safety Pendant and duress buttons are aimed at lone-worker and mobile personnel scenarios where location and alert capability matter. Real-time GPS tracking and automatic fall detection can improve response speed and give organisations better visibility during emergencies. Customers gain an added layer of personal protection beyond the vehicle itself.

OEM Integration & Sourcing

This service supports businesses that need visual systems and accessories sourced to align with OEM-branded programs and technical requirements. It is especially useful where compatibility, presentation and channel consistency are important. The value lies in reducing friction between sourcing, integration and delivery across the program lifecycle.

Distribution Solutions

3Gen products supports distribution models such as line fit, port fit and direct-to-dealer delivery, helping automotive programs move products through the right channels. This matters when rollout consistency and supply coordination are just as important as the product itself. Customers benefit from a more structured path from specification to deployment.

Warranty & Service Management

Warranty handling, reverse logistics and technical documentation support help keep connected products usable long after first delivery. This is relevant for organisations that need clear after-sales processes rather than informal support arrangements. The outcome is stronger continuity, easier issue handling and a more manageable ownership experience.
