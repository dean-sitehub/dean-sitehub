How It Works

3Gen products supports customers from first enquiry through solution selection, rollout planning and follow-up support, with attention to OEM fit and operational use.

A straightforward process for technical buying decisions

1. Share the requirement

Start by outlining the vehicles, assets or workforce environment involved and the outcome you need. This may be fleet visibility, in-cab multimedia, connected camera evidence, lone-worker safety or an OEM accessory requirement.

2. Define the fit

Once the use case is clear, the conversation can narrow to the most suitable technology category or combination of categories. This stage is important because telematics, cameras, tracking and infotainment all solve different problems even when they sit in the same vehicle environment.

3. Align integration needs

If the project involves vehicle interfacing, OEM accessory alignment or broader deployment planning, those technical and operational requirements are considered early. That helps avoid selecting hardware that appears suitable on paper but creates friction during rollout.

4. Confirm sourcing and supply route

For some customers, the next step is straightforward product supply. For others, distribution routes such as line fit, port fit or direct-to-dealer planning may need to be considered so the solution arrives through the channel that best supports the vehicle program.

5. Deploy the solution

Deployment may involve platform access, hardware installation planning, accessory integration or connected camera and tracking setup. The emphasis is on creating a system that is usable in the field rather than a disconnected group of products.

6. Support after rollout

After implementation, support considerations remain important. Warranty handling, documentation and service-related follow-up help customers manage the technology over time instead of treating the project as complete the moment the hardware is delivered.

What to expect as a customer

Expect a technical conversation that starts with use case and fit rather than generic sales talk. If the need is specific, 3Gen products can focus on one product family such as asset tracking or safety devices; if the requirement spans multiple systems, the process can expand to include cloud platforms, accessories, integration considerations and support planning.

This approach is particularly useful for organisations that have to think beyond the initial purchase. Vehicle compatibility, distribution practicalities, documentation and after-sales support all influence whether a technology decision remains workable once it reaches the field.
