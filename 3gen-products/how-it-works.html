How It Works

A straightforward process for specifying the right vehicle technology

3Gen products is built around matching the right connected solution to the real operating environment, then supporting the technical and commercial path that follows.

How the process typically works

1. Start with your requirement

The process begins with an enquiry by email or contact form. The most useful first step is to explain the vehicle type, fleet context or program need, along with the category you are interested in, such as telematics, infotainment, cameras, tracking or safety devices.

2. Clarify application and fit

From there, the discussion moves into practical detail. This can include integration requirements, CAN bus considerations, branded program alignment, accessory expectations and the operational outcome you want from the technology rather than just the product name on a list.

3. Match the right solution category

Once the application is clear, the relevant solution path can be identified. Some customers need a data platform with telematics devices, others need in-cabin multimedia, connected safety cameras, asset trackers or security devices, and some need a broader package that spans more than one category.

4. Confirm sourcing and delivery pathway

For structured automotive environments, the delivery path matters. 3Gen products supports sourcing and integration-aware supply models, including scenarios that align with OEM programs, dealer channels and broader distribution requirements rather than one-size-fits-all product supply.

5. Prepare for rollout and support

After the solution path is agreed, the focus shifts to what supports successful deployment. Depending on the requirement, this may include documentation, warranty handling, reverse logistics considerations and technical guidance that helps the customer manage the solution after supply.

6. Ongoing operational follow-up

Connected vehicle technology should remain useful after installation or delivery. The support position around after-sales service helps customers deal with the practical realities of operating, maintaining and scaling the chosen solution over time.

What to include in your enquiry

To make the first discussion more productive, include the vehicle or asset type involved, whether the requirement is for an OEM, dealer or fleet setting, and the main outcome you need. It also helps to mention if you need branding alignment, camera coverage, live data visibility, navigation, lone-worker safety or asset monitoring.

What happens next

Once your requirement is understood, the next step is usually a more focused discussion around solution fit, technical compatibility and supply considerations. That keeps the process consultative and useful, especially where connected systems need to integrate into broader automotive operations.
