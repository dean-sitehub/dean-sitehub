How It Works

A straightforward process for matching connected vehicle technology to the right automotive or fleet application.

From first enquiry to ongoing support

01

Tell us what you need

Start by sharing the vehicle type, intended use and product category you are considering. This helps 3Gen products understand whether the request is best suited to telematics, infotainment, cameras, tracking, accessories, personal safety devices or a broader integration requirement.

02

Review the technical fit

Once the requirement is clear, the next step is matching the right solution to the vehicle and operating environment. This is where compatibility, CAN bus considerations, programme alignment and deployment model become important.

03

Define the supply and rollout path

Depending on the application, the solution may be aligned with line fit, port fit, direct-to-dealer supply or another distribution structure. At this stage, customers can also clarify documentation, sourcing and support expectations.

04

Receive product and platform guidance

For cloud-based, tracking, camera and connected fleet technologies, the business can guide customers toward the right mix of hardware and platform capability. The aim is to make sure the technology selected supports the operational outcome, not just the specification sheet.

05

Support after delivery

After supply, 3Gen products continues to support customers through after-sales service areas such as warranty management, technical documentation and reverse logistics where applicable. This keeps the relationship focused on performance over time rather than a once-off transaction.

What to prepare before contacting us

Vehicle or asset context

Be ready to explain whether the solution is for passenger vehicles, commercial vehicles, a fleet, trailers, containers or individual personnel in the field.

Technology goal

Clarify whether the priority is visibility, safety, multimedia, security, asset monitoring or OEM programme alignment. This helps narrow the most relevant product path quickly.

Operational requirements

If there are documentation, compliance, rollout or after-sales expectations, include them early. These details can shape the best-fit sourcing and distribution approach.
