How It Works

A straightforward process for evaluating and deploying connected vehicle solutions

From the first enquiry to after-sales support, 3Gen products helps customers move through a structured technology selection process.

01

Share your requirement

The process starts with your use case. You explain whether you need telematics, infotainment, connected cameras, tracking, accessories, lone-worker safety devices, or a broader OEM-linked requirement. The more detail you provide about vehicles, assets, users and operational goals, the more useful the guidance will be.

02

Define the technical fit

3Gen products then considers the solution fit, including the role of CAN bus integration, cloud visibility, in-cabin systems, event capture, tracking method, and branded program requirements. This step is about narrowing the options to what will actually suit the environment instead of forcing a generic product match.

03

Confirm sourcing and delivery pathway

Once the right direction is identified, the next step is to establish how the solution will be supplied and supported. That may include OEM integration, distribution planning, documentation, and any relevant service or warranty considerations. Customers get a clearer view of what implementation involves before moving forward.

04

Deploy with support in mind

Implementation is approached with long-term usability in view. Rather than ending the engagement at supply, 3Gen products positions support around after-sales service, warranty processes and technical guidance so customers can continue to work with the solution more confidently.

05

Review and expand where needed

Many customers start with one category and then identify adjacent needs such as cameras, accessories, tracking or cloud visibility. Because the offering spans multiple connected vehicle areas, follow-up conversations can build on the original deployment instead of restarting the selection process from zero.
