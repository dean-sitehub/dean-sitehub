How It Works

A straightforward process for customers who need connected vehicle technology, OEM-aligned accessories, and informed support from first enquiry to follow-up.

From enquiry to supported deployment

1

Share your requirement

Start by outlining the vehicles, assets, or use case involved. This could be a telematics rollout, infotainment requirement, asset tracking need, camera solution, personal safety use case, or an OEM-branded accessory program.

2

Review the right solution mix

3Gen products assesses which technologies are relevant to the requirement and where integration, sourcing, or support considerations may shape the recommendation. This helps avoid overspecifying or combining products that do not fit the intended environment.

3

Align on compatibility and scope

At this stage, the conversation focuses on details such as vehicle context, branded program needs, cloud visibility goals, accessory fitment, and any documentation or compliance considerations. The objective is to define a cleaner and more workable scope before supply decisions are finalised.

4

Source and coordinate delivery

Once the requirement is clear, sourcing and distribution planning can be aligned to the customer’s preferred channel model. Depending on the program, that may involve arrangements suited to line fit, port fit, direct-to-dealer distribution, or related support logistics.

5

Support beyond supply

After supply, the value continues through warranty and service management considerations, documentation, and other after-sales support needs. This is particularly important for businesses deploying technology across vehicles or assets where supportability matters over time.

What to expect as a customer

You can expect a technical, solutions-focused conversation rather than a generic sales pitch. 3Gen products is positioned for buyers who need practical answers around compatibility, operational value, and downstream support. That makes the process better suited to fleet, dealer, and automotive program decisions where the right fit matters more than speed alone.
