How It Works

A straightforward path from first enquiry to solution supply, integration planning and ongoing support.

1. Start the conversation

The process begins with an enquiry about the product area or operational challenge you need to address. You might be looking for telematics, infotainment, asset tracking, camera systems, accessories or a broader OEM-aligned solution. Clear upfront information helps shape the right recommendation.

2. Define the requirement

Once contact is made, the next step is to understand the vehicle environment, intended use, technical expectations and any channel or rollout constraints. This may involve considering CAN bus needs, asset type, fleet visibility requirements or branding alignment for an OEM program. The aim is to avoid mismatched products and unnecessary rework.

3. Match products and sourcing

With the requirement clarified, 3Gen products can align the appropriate platform, device or accessory set to the project. This could be a cloud-led telematics setup, a cabin infotainment package, asset trackers for mobile equipment or safety devices for lone workers. Where needed, sourcing and OEM integration considerations form part of the solution path.

4. Plan distribution and deployment

Some projects require more than supply alone, especially where line fit, port fit or direct-to-dealer distribution needs to be considered. This step is about making sure the route to deployment matches the customer’s operating model. Good planning here helps keep the solution consistent from specification to delivery.

5. Support the operational phase

After delivery, the focus shifts to documentation, warranty handling and service continuity where applicable. That matters in connected vehicle technology because support processes often determine whether a solution remains practical over time. The goal is to keep the customer supported after the initial supply decision has been made.

What to expect as a customer

You can expect a solution-led conversation rather than a generic product list dropped into your inbox. The most useful projects begin with a clear understanding of the operating environment, then move into product alignment, sourcing logic and support planning. That approach is especially important in automotive technology, where compatibility and after-sales structure matter as much as the product itself.
