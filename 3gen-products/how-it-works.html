How It Works

A straightforward path from enquiry to supply, integration planning and ongoing support.

The engagement process

1. Initial enquiry

The process starts when you share your requirement by email or through the contact form. Useful details include vehicle type, fleet size, asset category, safety concern or whether the requirement must align with an OEM or dealer-related program.

2. Requirement matching

3Gen products reviews the operational need and identifies the most suitable technology category, whether that is telematics, infotainment, cameras, asset tracking, accessories or personal security devices. This stage helps avoid buying hardware that does not fit the intended outcome or integration environment.

3. Scope and supply planning

Once the right solution path is clear, the supply and support scope can be outlined. That may include sourcing considerations, compatibility expectations, distribution pathway planning and any documentation or after-sales factors relevant to the project.

4. Delivery pathway

Depending on the program, delivery may align with structured models such as line fit, port fit or direct-to-dealer distribution. This is especially important where the technology forms part of a broader automotive supply or branded rollout environment.

5. Integration and operational use

For many customers, value comes from how well the product works within the vehicle or fleet environment once deployed. CAN bus interfacing, cloud connectivity, camera event capture, infotainment integration or lone-worker safety functions all depend on choosing a solution that suits the real operating context.

6. Ongoing support

After supply, the support picture can include warranty coordination, reverse logistics and technical documentation according to the project and product category. That ongoing layer helps customers maintain continuity and confidence as the technology remains in service.

What to expect as a customer

You can expect a conversation centered on your actual operating needs rather than a generic product push. Because the portfolio spans multiple connected vehicle categories, the aim is to recommend technology that supports a clear use case, whether that means fleet visibility, safer driving insight, improved in-cabin functionality or stronger asset control.

You should also expect practical discussion around integration and support. In this sector, the right decision often depends on compatibility, sourcing path, rollout model and after-sales handling just as much as the feature list. That is where 3Gen products adds value beyond simply listing available devices.

If you are unsure where to begin, start with the operational problem you want to solve. That makes it easier to map the requirement to the right product family and define what a successful implementation should look like.

Ready to begin?
