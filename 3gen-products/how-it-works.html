How It Works

From first enquiry to sourcing and ongoing support, here is what customers can expect when working with 3Gen products.

The Process

01

Share Your Requirement

Start by outlining what you need to solve. This could involve fleet visibility, in-vehicle infotainment, camera systems, asset tracking, personal safety devices or broader OEM program support. The more context you provide about the vehicle type, operating environment and desired outcome, the more targeted the next step can be.

02

Review Fit and Compatibility

Once the requirement is understood, the discussion can move toward product fit, integration needs and compatibility considerations. This is where questions around CAN bus interfacing, installation environment, accessory alignment or cloud connectivity become important, helping narrow the most suitable path.

03

Define the Solution Scope

After identifying the right product category, the scope can be shaped more clearly. That may include the devices or systems needed, whether OEM integration or sourcing support is required, and how the solution should be delivered through line fit, port fit, direct-to-dealer or another structured model where relevant.

04

Coordinate Supply and Deployment

With the scope in place, supply and rollout planning can move forward. Depending on the project, this stage may involve documentation, technical coordination, distribution considerations and practical support around getting the solution into the intended vehicle or asset environment.

05

Support After Delivery

The relationship does not need to end when a product is supplied. 3Gen products also supports customers through warranty and service management, reverse logistics and after-sales coordination, helping create a steadier support path once systems are in use.

What Customers Can Expect

Customers can expect a practical, solutions-focused conversation rather than a generic product pitch. Because the portfolio spans cloud platforms, telematics hardware, multimedia systems, cameras, tracking and safety devices, the best outcomes come from matching the solution to the operational use case. The engagement is intended to help customers make clearer decisions around technology fit, rollout requirements and ongoing support needs.
