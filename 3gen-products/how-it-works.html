How It Works

3Gen products follows a practical process: understand the requirement, match the right technology, support fitment or sourcing decisions, and stay available when the system moves into use.

From enquiry to operational use

01

Send your requirement

Start by sharing what you need to achieve, whether that is fleet visibility, an infotainment upgrade, asset tracking, a safety device or OEM-aligned sourcing. The clearer the vehicle type, use case and deployment context, the easier it is to identify a suitable path.

02

Review the application

3Gen products looks at the requirement through a technical and operational lens. That may include considering CAN bus integration, product compatibility, branding needs, support expectations and how the solution will be used once deployed.

03

Define the right solution set

Once the need is understood, the next step is aligning the right category or combination of products. This could involve cloud platform access, hardware selection, accessory fitment planning or a wider OEM integration and sourcing discussion.

04

Confirm scope and supply route

For some customers this is a straightforward supply transaction. For others, it may involve discussion around distribution models, documentation, support structure or how the solution fits into a broader vehicle program.

05

Deploy and integrate

With the scope agreed, products can move into implementation or operational use. The focus at this stage is on ensuring the selected technology aligns with the customer environment and delivers the intended practical outcome.

06

Ongoing support

Support can continue beyond supply, particularly where warranty, service handling, reverse logistics or technical documentation matter. This helps customers manage the long-term reality of connected vehicle systems rather than only the initial purchase.

What to expect when you make contact

Expect the conversation to focus on application rather than generic sales language. The goal is to understand what must work inside the vehicle, across the fleet or within the safety environment, then recommend technology that makes sense for that context.

If your requirement involves multiple components, OEM branding considerations or support obligations after delivery, that can be addressed early in the process. This approach helps reduce mismatches and keeps the conversation centered on integration and operational value.
