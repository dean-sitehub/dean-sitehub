About 3Gen products

3Gen products supplies connected vehicle technologies and accessories to South Africa's automotive market, with a focus on OEM-grade systems that are designed to integrate cleanly into modern vehicles and fleet environments. The business is the authorized reseller of Directed Technologies, Australia's leading designer and manufacturer of connected vehicle solutions used by major automakers and fleet programs globally. That gives customers access to a portfolio that covers telematics, infotainment, vehicle cameras, tracking, safety devices and supporting cloud tools through a single specialist source.

The approach is practical and technical rather than off-the-shelf. 3Gen products helps customers evaluate the right hardware, understand integration requirements, align with OEM branded programs and support the operational details that make deployment work in the real world. That includes sourcing, documentation, compliance support, distribution pathways and service considerations that matter to OEM teams, dealers and fleet operators who need technology that fits into existing workflows.

What sets the business apart is its focus on systems that do more than simply install into a vehicle. The aim is to deliver solutions that improve visibility, safety, convenience and data quality across the full ownership lifecycle, from vehicle handover to fleet operation and after-sales support. For customers looking for connected vehicle technology with a stronger engineering foundation, 3Gen products offers a more considered route than generic accessories alone.

Our Services

3Gen products delivers connected vehicle systems for automotive brands, dealers and fleet operators that need dependable hardware, clean integration and useful data from every installed solution.

e.things Cloud Platform

This OEM-grade cloud platform brings fragmented vehicle and device data into one connected environment. It is built for businesses that need secure APIs, usable fleet insight and a way to turn raw telemetry into reporting, predictive analytics and operational decisions.

Telematics + IoT Devices

These devices are designed for real-time fleet visibility with OEM-certified hardware and CAN bus integration support. They help operators monitor vehicle health, diagnostics and movement data in a way that is better suited to connected fleet management than basic tracker-only setups.

Multimedia + Infotainment Systems

3Gen products supplies integrated infotainment systems in multiple screen sizes with navigation powered by HERE Maps, Bluetooth, app support and multi-camera display capability. They are suited to OEM programs and vehicle applications that require a more polished in-cabin experience without losing integration quality.

Advanced Vehicle Cameras

Connected camera systems help fleets capture and upload critical safety events such as collisions, duress situations and driver behaviour incidents. The value lies in faster incident review, clearer operational oversight and stronger evidence when safety performance needs to be understood properly.

Asset Tracking Solutions

These tracking options cover battery-powered, solar, hardwired and IP67-rated units for trailers, containers and high-value mobile assets. They are useful for businesses that need reliable asset visibility beyond standard vehicle installations, especially where environmental durability matters.

Vehicle Accessories

The accessories range includes products such as wireless charge mats, intelligent talking alarms and reverse camera systems. Each item is aimed at improving daily usability and vehicle safety with accessories that complement modern connected mobility expectations.

Safety and Personal Security Devices

Safety products such as the TEQ-Secure Safety Pendant and duress buttons support lone-worker protection with real-time GPS tracking and automatic fall detection. They are suited to operations where staff safety depends on fast location awareness and direct emergency signalling.

OEM Integration & Sourcing

3Gen products sources visual and accessory systems designed to align with OEM branded programs and vehicle integration standards. This helps automotive businesses reduce mismatch between product, brand requirements and installation expectations while keeping compatibility front of mind.
