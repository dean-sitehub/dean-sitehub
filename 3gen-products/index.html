About 3Gen products

3Gen products supplies connected vehicle technologies and accessories for the South African automotive market, with a focus on OEM-grade systems that need to perform reliably in real operational environments. The business serves automotive programs, fleet operators and buyers who need more than off-the-shelf hardware. From telematics and infotainment to safety devices and asset tracking, the emphasis is on systems that integrate cleanly and deliver usable information.

As the authorized reseller of Directed Technologies in South Africa, 3Gen products brings locally available access to a product portfolio developed for modern vehicles and demanding fleet use cases. That includes the e.things Cloud Platform, telematics and IoT devices, advanced cameras, multimedia systems and security products, alongside sourcing and integration support. The approach is practical and technical: understand the vehicle environment, align with OEM requirements where needed, and supply technology that supports long-term deployment rather than short-term fixes.

Customers choose 3Gen products when they need connected vehicle solutions backed by careful compatibility thinking, documentation, and ongoing support. The business is built around quality-first sourcing, dependable integration pathways and products designed to work within real automotive programs. Whether the requirement is in-cabin visibility, smarter asset tracking or a branded OEM accessory solution, the promise is a clear, engineered path from product selection to operational use.

Our Services

3Gen products delivers connected vehicle technology, fleet visibility tools, infotainment systems, tracking devices and OEM-aligned accessories for automotive and operational environments that demand dependable integration.

e.things Cloud Platform

This OEM-grade cloud platform turns fragmented vehicle and device data into a usable operational view. It supports analytics, event monitoring and secure third-party integrations, helping teams move from isolated data points to informed decisions.

Telematics + IoT Devices

These devices are built for connected vehicle environments where live diagnostics, CAN bus integration and fleet visibility matter. Customers use them to monitor vehicle status, improve maintenance planning and capture the data needed for smarter operations.

Multimedia + Infotainment Systems

Integrated infotainment platforms in multiple screen sizes provide navigation, Bluetooth, app connectivity and multi-camera display support. They are suited to programs that want a cleaner in-vehicle experience without sacrificing OEM compatibility.

Advanced Vehicle Cameras

Connected camera solutions help operators capture collisions, duress events and driving behaviour with faster access to visual evidence. That means stronger incident visibility, improved safety oversight and better information when events need investigation.

Asset Tracking Solutions

Tracking options include battery-powered, solar, hardwired and IP67-rated devices for trailers, containers and other valuable mobile assets. The value is straightforward: better location accuracy, more reliable monitoring and less guesswork across dispersed assets.

Vehicle Accessories

The accessory range includes reverse camera systems, wireless charging mats and intelligent talking alarms designed to improve convenience and driver awareness. These products are suited to buyers who want practical upgrades with a cleaner vehicle fit-out.

Safety and Personal Security Devices

Personal safety devices such as pendants and duress buttons support lone-worker protection through real-time GPS visibility and automatic fall detection. They help organisations extend safety coverage beyond the vehicle and into mobile field operations.

OEM Integration & Sourcing

3Gen products helps source and deliver visual and accessory systems designed to align with OEM programs and branded requirements. This is especially valuable where compatibility, documentation, distribution models and after-sales support all need to work together.
