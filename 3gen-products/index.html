About 3Gen products

3Gen products serves South Africa's automotive, fleet, and mobility sectors with connected vehicle technology designed for real operational use. As the authorized reseller of Directed Technologies, the business focuses on OEM-grade solutions that help customers capture data, improve safety, simplify integration, and make better decisions from vehicles and mobile assets. Its offering spans cloud software, telematics hardware, multimedia systems, connected cameras, tracking tools, accessories, and personal security devices.

The company approach is practical and technically grounded. Rather than presenting disconnected products in isolation, 3Gen products helps customers understand how cloud platforms, CAN-integrated devices, infotainment systems, and safety technologies work together inside an OEM program, dealer channel, or fleet deployment. That means conversations are shaped around compatibility, compliance, distribution models, documentation, service support, and long-term usability.

Customers choose 3Gen products when they need technology that fits automotive standards and operational realities. The emphasis is on dependable sourcing, clean integration, and support that continues after delivery, whether the requirement is asset tracking, in-cabin media, event-based camera capture, reverse camera systems, lone-worker protection, or a broader connected vehicle program. Every recommendation is intended to give clients a clearer view of vehicle technology options without unnecessary complexity.

Our Services

3Gen products supplies connected vehicle technologies and supporting integration services for OEM, dealer, fleet, and asset management requirements across South Africa.

e.things Cloud Platform

This OEM-grade IoT and telematics platform brings fragmented vehicle and asset data into one environment that is easier to interpret and act on. It is suited to customers who need operational visibility, predictive insight, and secure API-driven integration with third-party systems.

Telematics + IoT Devices

These OEM-certified devices support CAN bus integration, predictive diagnostics, and real-time fleet visibility. They are designed for operators who want cleaner vehicle data, more accurate monitoring, and better maintenance and usage insight from connected hardware.

Multimedia + Infotainment Systems

Integrated infotainment platforms in 7, 8, and 10 inch formats provide navigation powered by HERE Maps, Bluetooth, app connectivity, CAN interfacing, and multi-camera display support. These systems are ideal for OEM-branded vehicle programs and customers who need a polished in-cabin digital experience.

Advanced Vehicle Cameras

Connected camera solutions are built to capture and upload safety events such as collisions, duress incidents, and driver distraction triggers. They help fleet operators investigate incidents faster, improve behavioural oversight, and strengthen road safety management.

Asset Tracking Solutions

Battery-powered, solar, hardwired, and IP67-rated tracking devices provide dependable monitoring for trailers, containers, and high-value assets. They are suited to businesses that need location accuracy, reduced blind spots, and stronger control over mobile equipment.

Vehicle Accessories

The accessory range includes wireless charge mats, intelligent talking alarms, and reverse camera systems designed to improve convenience and vehicle safety. These products suit customers who want practical upgrades that integrate cleanly into automotive environments.

Safety and Personal Security Devices

Solutions such as the TEQ-Secure Safety Pendant and duress buttons support lone-worker safety with GPS tracking and automatic fall detection. They are valuable for mobile teams, field personnel, and businesses that need a faster emergency response pathway.

OEM Integration & Sourcing

3Gen products sources and delivers visual and accessory systems built to align with OEM-branded programs. This service helps customers achieve compatibility, documentation readiness, and brand-consistent implementation across line fit, port fit, or dealer distribution models.
