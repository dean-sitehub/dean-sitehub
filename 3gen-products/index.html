About 3Gen products

3Gen products supplies connected vehicle technology and vehicle accessory solutions for the South African automotive market. The business is positioned around OEM-grade systems, serving organisations that need dependable telematics, infotainment, tracking, camera and safety technology that works cleanly within modern vehicle environments. Its offer is especially relevant to automotive programmes, fleet operators, dealer channels and buyers who need integration, sourcing and support rather than disconnected off-the-shelf hardware.

As the authorized reseller of Directed Technologies in South Africa, 3Gen products brings a portfolio designed for real operational use. That includes the e.things Cloud Platform, telematics and IoT devices, multimedia systems, connected cameras, asset tracking, personal security devices and integrated accessories. The approach is practical and technical: understand the vehicle application, match the right hardware and platform, and support the rollout with documentation, compliance considerations and after-sales service.

Customers choose 3Gen products when they need clarity across the full connected vehicle picture, from data visibility and driver safety through to OEM programme alignment. The business emphasises quality-first sourcing, reliable system compatibility and solutions that can support line fit, port fit or direct-to-dealer distribution requirements. Instead of generic gadget retail, 3Gen products focuses on technology that is meant to integrate well, perform consistently and support long-term vehicle operations.

Our Services

3Gen products offers connected vehicle technologies and automotive accessories built around fleet visibility, OEM integration, driver safety and in-vehicle experience. Each solution is selected to deliver clearer data, better compatibility and stronger operational support for South African automotive and fleet environments.

e.things Cloud Platform

This OEM-grade IoT and telematics platform brings fragmented vehicle information into one usable view. It helps customers turn raw vehicle and asset data into reporting, predictive insight and secure third-party integrations through APIs that support broader business systems.

Telematics + IoT Devices

These devices are built for real vehicle integration, including CAN bus connectivity and live operational monitoring. They support fleet visibility, diagnostics and data capture that can improve maintenance planning, driver oversight and day-to-day decision making.

Multimedia + Infotainment Systems

3Gen products supplies integrated infotainment platforms in multiple screen sizes with navigation, Bluetooth, app connectivity and multi-camera display capability. These systems are designed for vehicles that require polished in-cabin functionality without compromising compatibility with OEM interfaces.

Advanced Vehicle Cameras

Connected camera systems help customers review incidents quickly and identify behavioural patterns across fleets. Solutions are designed to automatically capture and upload safety events such as collisions, duress situations and driver-related incidents for stronger oversight and response.

Asset Tracking Solutions

Asset trackers are available in battery-powered, solar, hardwired and IP67-rated configurations for different operational conditions. They support reliable monitoring of trailers, containers and high-value mobile assets where location accuracy and durability matter.

Vehicle Accessories

The accessories range includes solutions such as wireless charge mats, intelligent talking alarms and reverse camera systems. These additions are chosen to improve safety, convenience and day-to-day usability inside working vehicles.

Safety and Personal Security Devices

Safety devices such as the TEQ-Secure Safety Pendant and duress buttons are intended for lone-worker and personal security applications. With features like real-time GPS tracking and automatic fall detection, they help organisations respond faster when a person needs assistance.

OEM Integration & Sourcing

3Gen products sources and delivers visual and accessory systems designed to align with OEM branded programmes. This service is valuable for customers who need seamless compatibility, clean brand alignment and practical support around documentation, rollout and after-sales management.
