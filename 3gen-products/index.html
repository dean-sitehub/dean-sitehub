About 3Gen products

3Gen products is the South African authorized reseller of Directed Technologies, bringing OEM-grade connected vehicle systems to automotive businesses, fleet operators and organisations that need reliable in-vehicle technology. The business focuses on solutions that help customers see more, know more and manage more across vehicles, drivers and mobile assets. From telematics devices and cloud platforms to cameras, infotainment and personal safety products, every offering is chosen for practical use in demanding operating environments.

Our approach is built around integration rather than one-off hardware sales. Many customers need systems that work properly with OEM programs, CAN bus data, compliance requirements and after-sales support expectations, so 3Gen products helps bridge the gap between advanced technology and real-world rollout. That includes sourcing, documentation, distribution support and the kind of product guidance that matters when technology must perform consistently across a fleet or branded vehicle program.

What drives the business is a clear focus on quality-first automotive technology that is dependable, compatible and fit for purpose. Customers come to 3Gen products when they need more than a catalogue—they need solutions that improve visibility, safety, driver experience and operational control. The promise is straightforward: supply connected vehicle technology with the professionalism, technical clarity and support standards expected from an OEM-grade environment.

Our Services

3Gen products supplies connected vehicle technologies and support services for automotive, fleet and asset-focused operations, with solutions spanning telematics, infotainment, camera systems, tracking and OEM integration.

e.things Cloud Platform

This OEM-grade IoT and telematics platform brings fragmented vehicle and asset data into one operational view. It is designed for businesses that need actionable insights, predictive analytics and secure API-based integrations without juggling disconnected systems.

Telematics + IoT Devices

These devices support CAN bus integration, real-time tracking and predictive diagnostics for fleets that need more than basic location data. Customers use them to improve visibility, identify issues earlier and build a clearer picture of vehicle performance in day-to-day operations.

Multimedia + Infotainment Systems

Integrated infotainment platforms are available in multiple screen sizes with navigation, Bluetooth, app connectivity and multi-camera display support. They suit OEM-aligned vehicle programs and buyers who want a better in-cabin user experience without compromising integration quality.

Advanced Vehicle Cameras

Connected camera solutions help capture and upload critical safety events such as collisions, duress incidents and driver distraction. They are well suited to fleet operations that need incident context quickly and want to support safer driving behaviour through recorded evidence.

Asset Tracking Solutions

Battery-powered, solar, hardwired and IP67-rated trackers provide dependable monitoring for trailers, containers and valuable mobile assets. The outcome is tighter control over asset movement, improved recovery visibility and better oversight across dispersed operations.

Vehicle Accessories

The accessory range includes wireless charge mats, intelligent alarms and reverse camera systems that improve convenience and day-to-day vehicle usability. These products are selected for customers who want practical upgrades that complement modern vehicle technology and safety expectations.

Safety and Personal Security Devices

Products such as the TEQ-Secure Safety Pendant and duress buttons support lone-worker safety with live GPS tracking and automatic fall detection. They help organisations protect mobile teams and respond faster when personal safety incidents occur away from fixed sites.

OEM Integration & Sourcing

3Gen products sources and delivers visual and accessory systems built to align with OEM branded programs. This service is for customers who need compatibility, brand consistency and a smoother path from specification through supply, support and after-sales management.
