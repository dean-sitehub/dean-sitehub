About 3Gen products

3Gen products supplies connected vehicle technology to the South African automotive market with a focus on systems that need to work reliably in real operating conditions. The business serves fleet operators, OEM-linked programs, dealer networks and organisations that need dependable telematics, infotainment, tracking, camera and safety solutions. Its offering is grounded in OEM-grade standards, practical compatibility and clear technical support rather than off-the-shelf gadgets that create integration problems later.

As the authorized reseller of Directed Technologies in South Africa, 3Gen products gives customers access to a portfolio developed for modern connected vehicles and fleet environments. That includes the e.things Cloud Platform, telematics and IoT devices, multimedia systems, advanced cameras, asset tracking and personal safety devices, along with sourcing and integration support. The approach is to connect hardware, software and operational requirements into one workable solution so customers can manage data, compliance, driver safety and in-vehicle experience with less friction.

Customers choose 3Gen products when they need more than a box of hardware. The business places strong emphasis on OEM alignment, CAN BUS support, documentation, warranty handling, reverse logistics and after-sales service so the solution stays usable long after installation. That practical, solutions-led mindset helps customers move from fragmented systems to connected technology that supports visibility, safety, brand consistency and everyday operational control.

Our Services

3Gen products delivers OEM-grade connected vehicle systems for fleets, dealers and automotive programs that need measurable visibility, safer operations and seamless in-vehicle integration.

e.things Cloud Platform

This platform brings fragmented vehicle and asset data into a single secure environment where teams can view live information, trends and exceptions more clearly. It is designed for customers who need actionable dashboards, predictive insight and API-ready integrations instead of isolated data sources that are hard to use.

Telematics + IoT Devices

These OEM-certified devices support vehicle visibility with CAN bus integration, real-time status reporting and predictive diagnostics. They suit operators who want dependable fleet intelligence, earlier fault awareness and a stronger basis for operational decisions.

Multimedia + Infotainment Systems

Integrated infotainment platforms are available in multiple screen sizes and are designed to work with vehicle systems through CAN interfacing. Customers benefit from navigation powered by HERE Maps, app integration, Bluetooth connectivity and support for multi-camera display environments inside the cabin.

Advanced Vehicle Cameras

Connected camera solutions help capture and upload critical safety events such as collisions, duress incidents and risky driver behaviour. This gives fleet teams faster event review, stronger incident visibility and a better foundation for safety coaching and accountability.

Asset Tracking Solutions

Battery-powered, solar, IP67-rated and hardwired trackers are available for trailers, containers and valuable mobile assets. They are intended for businesses that need consistent location awareness and monitoring across assets that do not always fit a single power or deployment model.

Vehicle Accessories

3Gen products supplies practical accessory systems including wireless charge mats, intelligent talking alarms and reverse camera solutions. These additions improve convenience, safety and usability while fitting into a broader connected vehicle strategy.

Safety and Personal Security Devices

Personal safety devices such as the TEQ-Secure Safety Pendant and duress buttons support lone-worker protection with GPS tracking and automatic fall detection. They are well suited to teams that need rapid visibility when a worker is under stress, immobile or in a vulnerable environment.

OEM Integration & Sourcing

Beyond supply, 3Gen products helps source and deliver systems that align with OEM-branded programs and distribution models. Customers gain a more seamless path through compatibility, brand alignment, technical documentation, warranty processes and ongoing operational support.
