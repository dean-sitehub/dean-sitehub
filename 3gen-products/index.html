About 3Gen products

3Gen products supplies connected vehicle technologies and accessory systems for customers who need reliable, OEM-grade solutions rather than disconnected aftermarket add-ons. The business serves South Africa’s automotive sector with telematics, infotainment, advanced cameras, asset tracking, safety devices and accessory programs that are designed to fit real operational demands. Whether the requirement is fleet visibility, in-cabin technology, compliance support or integrated vehicle hardware, the goal is to help customers deploy systems that work together cleanly.

As the authorized reseller of Directed Technologies in South Africa, 3Gen products brings locally available access to technology developed by Australia’s leading designer and manufacturer of OEM-grade connected vehicle platforms and devices. The approach is not limited to product supply alone; it also includes OEM integration and sourcing, documentation, distribution support, warranty handling and practical after-sales coordination. That makes the business especially relevant for fleet operators, dealer programs and automotive stakeholders who need a supplier that understands both the technology and the implementation pathway.

The promise behind 3Gen products is built around product quality, technical fit and dependable support. Instead of overselling broad claims, the business focuses on matching the right platform or device to the customer’s use case, from cloud visibility and predictive diagnostics to vehicle safety, lone-worker protection and multimedia integration. Every conversation is shaped by compatibility, compliance, usability and long-term support, so customers can move forward with greater clarity and fewer surprises.

Our Services

Explore connected vehicle technology, telematics hardware, fleet safety systems and OEM integration services designed for automotive programs, fleet operators and mobility-focused businesses.

e.things Cloud Platform

The e.things Cloud Platform brings fragmented vehicle and device data into one OEM-grade environment that is easier to interpret and act on. It is built for customers who need secure APIs, predictive analytics and a practical way to connect telematics information with wider business systems and reporting workflows.

Telematics + IoT Devices

These OEM-certified devices are designed for reliable vehicle visibility with CAN bus integration, live diagnostics and real-time operational insight. They suit fleet managers and automotive programs that need dependable data capture, better oversight of vehicle behaviour and stronger support for maintenance planning.

Multimedia + Infotainment Systems

3Gen products supplies integrated infotainment systems in multiple screen sizes with navigation powered by HERE Maps, Bluetooth, app support and multi-camera display capability. These systems are aimed at OEM-aligned vehicle programs and drivers who need modern in-cabin functionality without compromising integration quality.

Advanced Vehicle Cameras

Connected camera solutions help capture and upload critical safety events such as collisions, duress incidents and risky driving behaviour. They are especially valuable for fleet operations that want faster incident review, stronger driver coaching and clearer evidence when something goes wrong on the road.

Asset Tracking Solutions

Asset tracking options include IP67-rated, battery-powered, solar and hardwired units for trailers, containers and high-value mobile assets. The outcome is more precise monitoring, improved asset accountability and better visibility across equipment that does not stay in one place.

Vehicle Accessories

The accessories range includes wireless charge mats, intelligent talking alarms and reverse camera systems selected to improve convenience and day-to-day vehicle safety. These products fit customers looking for useful upgrades that integrate cleanly into broader vehicle programs.

Safety and Personal Security Devices

Safety devices such as the TEQ-Secure Safety Pendant and duress buttons support lone-worker protection through real-time GPS tracking and automatic fall detection. They are suited to teams operating in the field where personal safety, response visibility and location awareness matter.

OEM Integration & Sourcing

3Gen products also supports the sourcing and supply of visual and accessory systems designed to align with OEM-branded programs. This helps customers achieve cleaner compatibility, better documentation support and a more controlled rollout across line fit, port fit or dealer distribution models.
