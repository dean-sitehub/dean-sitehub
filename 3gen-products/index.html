About 3Gen products

3Gen products brings connected vehicle technology to automotive businesses that need dependable systems rather than pieced-together aftermarket fixes. As the authorised reseller of Directed Technologies in South Africa, the company focuses on OEM-grade telematics, infotainment, tracking, camera and safety solutions that fit the operational realities of fleets, dealers and branded vehicle programs. That means customers can evaluate one partner for in-vehicle hardware, cloud visibility, accessory systems and support pathways instead of sourcing each layer separately.

The business approach is practical and integration-led. 3Gen products helps customers connect devices to vehicle systems, align accessory programs with OEM requirements, and select hardware that supports compliance, serviceability and long-term deployment. Its offering extends beyond the initial product supply to include sourcing, documentation, distribution models, warranty handling and reverse-logistics support where needed.

What sets 3Gen products apart is its focus on clarity and fit. Whether a customer needs richer fleet data from the e.things Cloud Platform, a multimedia head unit that communicates cleanly with vehicle systems, or lone-worker protection through GPS-enabled safety devices, the goal is to deliver technology that works properly in the field and is backed by informed support. For buyers responsible for uptime, safety and brand consistency, that level of integration matters.

Our Services

3Gen products supplies connected vehicle technologies and automotive accessories for OEM programs, fleet operators, dealers and mobility teams that need visibility, integration and dependable support across the full vehicle environment.

e.things Cloud Platform

This OEM-grade cloud platform brings fragmented vehicle and asset data into one secure environment so teams can work from a single operational view. It supports actionable reporting, predictive analytics and third-party integrations through APIs, helping customers turn raw movement and event data into decisions.

Telematics + IoT Devices

3Gen products supplies OEM-certified telematics hardware designed for real-time fleet visibility and predictive diagnostics. With CAN bus integration support, these devices help operators monitor vehicle condition, driver activity and operational exceptions with more useful context than standalone GPS units.

Multimedia + Infotainment Systems

Integrated infotainment systems are available in multiple screen sizes with navigation powered by HERE Maps, Bluetooth, app integration and multi-camera display support. These systems are intended for customers who need in-vehicle multimedia that looks and behaves like part of the vehicle rather than an add-on screen.

Advanced Vehicle Cameras

Connected camera solutions are built to capture and upload key safety events such as collisions, duress triggers and driver-related incidents. They help fleet managers move quickly from uncertainty to evidence, making it easier to investigate events, coach drivers and strengthen safety processes.

Asset Tracking Solutions

Asset tracking options include battery-powered, solar, hardwired and IP67-rated devices for trailers, containers and other high-value mobile assets. The outcome is more reliable location intelligence across equipment that may not always have constant power or regular site access.

Vehicle Accessories

The accessory range includes reverse camera systems, intelligent talking alarms and wireless charge mats designed to improve everyday safety and driver convenience. These products suit OEM accessory programs and vehicle upgrades where finish, compatibility and user experience all matter.

Safety and Personal Security Devices

Solutions such as the TEQ-Secure Safety Pendant and duress buttons support lone-worker protection with real-time GPS tracking and automatic fall detection. They are especially valuable where staff operate away from fixed sites and need a fast, trackable route to assistance.

OEM Integration & Sourcing

3Gen products sources and delivers visual and accessory systems that align with OEM-branded programs and vehicle architectures. Customers benefit from a more consistent rollout supported by compatibility guidance, distribution options and attention to the after-sales requirements that follow deployment.
