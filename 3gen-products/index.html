About 3Gen products

3Gen products serves South Africa’s automotive and fleet market with OEM-grade connected vehicle technologies designed for practical, day-to-day use. As the authorized reseller of Directed Technologies, the business brings together telematics, infotainment, camera systems, asset tracking, safety devices, and vehicle accessories in one focused offering. This makes it easier for vehicle programs, dealers, and fleet operators to source systems that are built for integration rather than patchwork deployment.

The company’s approach is centred on supplying technology that works cleanly with modern vehicle environments and real operational demands. That includes solutions with CAN bus interfacing, cloud visibility, secure API connectivity, and accessories aligned with OEM branded programs. Alongside product supply, 3Gen products supports sourcing, documentation, compliance, warranty coordination, and the after-sales processes customers need once technology is in the field.

What sets 3Gen products apart is the combination of technical depth and commercial practicality. Customers are not simply choosing a catalogue of devices; they are choosing a partner that understands fleet safety, in-vehicle user experience, asset visibility, and the importance of seamless brand alignment. The result is a more dependable path to connected vehicle technology for businesses that need quality, compatibility, and ongoing support.

Our Services

3Gen products supplies connected vehicle systems and support services for automotive programs, dealers, and fleets that need telematics, tracking, infotainment, safety monitoring, and OEM-compatible integration.

e.things Cloud Platform

The e.things platform brings fragmented vehicle and device data into one secure environment so operators can work from a clearer picture of activity, utilisation, and exceptions. It is designed for businesses that need actionable visibility, predictive analytics, and integration with third-party systems through secure APIs.

Telematics + IoT Devices

These OEM-certified devices are built for vehicle environments where reliable data capture matters. With CAN bus integration, real-time fleet visibility, and predictive diagnostics, they help customers move from reactive maintenance and guesswork to informed decision-making.

Multimedia + Infotainment Systems

3Gen products supplies integrated infotainment platforms in multiple screen sizes for modern in-vehicle experiences. With navigation powered by HERE Maps, Bluetooth, app integration, CAN interfacing, and support for multiple camera displays, these systems improve usability without compromising vehicle integration.

Advanced Vehicle Cameras

Connected camera solutions help fleets capture critical events such as collisions, duress incidents, and risky driver behaviour with far more context than a basic recording system. The value for customers is faster incident review, better safety oversight, and clearer evidence when it matters most.

Asset Tracking Solutions

Battery-powered, solar, hardwired, and IP67-rated trackers provide dependable monitoring for trailers, containers, and high-value mobile assets. These systems are suited to customers who need location accuracy, better asset utilisation, and stronger control over non-powered or remote equipment.

Vehicle Accessories

The accessories range includes products such as wireless charge mats, intelligent talking alarms, and reverse camera systems. Each item is selected to improve safety, convenience, and the overall in-vehicle experience while supporting cleaner installation and stronger compatibility outcomes.

Safety and Personal Security Devices

Safety pendants and duress buttons are designed for situations where lone-worker protection and rapid location awareness are essential. Features such as real-time GPS tracking and automatic fall detection help customers support staff safety with immediate, practical tools.

OEM Integration & Sourcing

3Gen products sources and delivers visual and accessory systems that align with OEM branded programs and vehicle requirements. This service is especially valuable for customers who need brand-consistent fitment, documented compatibility, and a smoother route from sourcing through after-sales support.
