About 3Gen products

3Gen products supplies connected vehicle technologies and integration-focused automotive solutions for customers who need more than an off-the-shelf accessory. As the South African authorized reseller of Directed Technologies, the business brings OEM-grade platforms and hardware to automotive programs, fleet operators and channel partners looking for dependable fit, compatibility and support. The product range spans cloud software, telematics devices, in-vehicle multimedia, safety cameras, asset trackers and personal security devices, giving buyers a practical route to connected mobility.

The company approach is built around integration, not isolated components. That means helping customers align hardware, data, CAN bus communication, sourcing and distribution requirements so the end result works properly inside real vehicles and real operating environments. Whether a client needs infotainment for an OEM-branded program, telematics for fleet visibility or tracking for valuable mobile assets, 3Gen products focuses on solutions that fit operational demands and support long-term use.

Customers choose 3Gen products when they need technical clarity, product alignment and after-sales support that matches the complexity of the automotive sector. The business is grounded in OEM-grade standards, careful product selection and a clear understanding of compliance, documentation, warranty handling and service continuity. That promise is simple: connected vehicle technology should deliver visibility, safety and convenience without creating new problems downstream.

Our Services

Explore connected vehicle technology solutions designed for fleet visibility, OEM integration, driver safety, in-vehicle convenience and reliable asset monitoring across South Africa.

e.things Cloud Platform

This OEM-grade cloud platform brings fragmented vehicle and device data into one connected environment that is easier to monitor, interpret and act on. It supports actionable reporting, predictive analytics and secure API integrations so businesses can connect vehicle data to wider operational systems without rebuilding everything from scratch.

Telematics + IoT Devices

These telematics and IoT devices are designed for automotive-grade deployment with CAN bus integration, diagnostic visibility and real-time tracking capability. They suit fleet operators and mobility programs that need accurate vehicle insight, stronger reporting and a better foundation for maintenance, usage analysis and live fleet oversight.

Multimedia + Infotainment Systems

3Gen products supplies integrated infotainment platforms in multiple screen sizes with navigation, Bluetooth, app integration, CAN interfacing and support for multi-camera display layouts. These systems are well suited to OEM-aligned projects and vehicle programs where the in-cabin experience needs to feel built in rather than added on later.

Advanced Vehicle Cameras

Connected camera solutions help capture and upload safety-critical events so operators can review incidents faster and understand behavioural patterns across their vehicles. They are valuable for fleets that need better context around collisions, duress events and driver-related risk instead of relying on incomplete reports after the fact.

Asset Tracking Solutions

Asset trackers are available in battery-powered, solar, hardwired and ruggedised formats to suit trailers, containers and other high-value mobile assets. The result is more reliable visibility over asset position and movement, even where traditional vehicle-only tracking tools are not the right fit.

Vehicle Accessories

The accessory range includes products such as wireless charge mats, intelligent talking alarms and reverse camera systems that improve convenience and day-to-day safety. These additions are selected to complement modern vehicles while supporting practical user needs in consumer, fleet and dealer environments.

Safety and Personal Security Devices

Solutions such as the TEQ-Secure Safety Pendant and duress buttons support lone-worker protection with real-time GPS tracking and automatic fall detection. They are ideal where personal safety, rapid response visibility and location awareness matter as much as vehicle data.

OEM Integration & Sourcing

3Gen products sources visual and accessory systems intended to fit smoothly within OEM-branded programs, preserving compatibility and brand alignment. This service is particularly useful for automotive businesses that need coordinated sourcing, integration thinking and a cleaner path from specification through delivery and support.
