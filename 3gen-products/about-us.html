About Us

Automotive technology supplied with an OEM-grade mindset, practical integration focus and support-led approach.

Built around connected vehicle capability

3Gen products operates in the automotive technology and vehicle accessories space with a strong emphasis on connected systems that serve real operational needs. As the South African authorized reseller of Directed Technologies, the business makes advanced solutions available to customers who need proven telematics, infotainment, camera, tracking and safety products. The core audience is made up of automotive and fleet-related buyers who value compatibility, performance and structured support rather than off-the-shelf gadgetry.

The business is shaped by an understanding that vehicle technology is rarely just about a single device. Customers often need systems to align with OEM programs, fit within compliance expectations, connect to CAN bus environments or remain supportable after deployment. That is why 3Gen products positions itself around sourcing, integration and after-sales readiness as much as product supply itself.

The offering reflects a broad but connected technology ecosystem: cloud-based data platforms, telematics hardware, in-vehicle multimedia, event-driven camera systems, asset trackers, accessories and personal security products. Each category contributes to a bigger picture of safer vehicles, better information flow and more useful operational control. For customers, that means the conversation starts with outcomes and ends with a more coherent technology solution.

What makes 3Gen products worth choosing

Authorized reseller position

3Gen products represents Directed Technologies in South Africa, giving customers a clearer route to OEM-grade connected vehicle solutions. That matters when buyers need confidence in provenance, specification alignment and the broader support context around supplied systems.

Integration-minded supply

The business does not treat automotive technology as isolated parts. Its focus on OEM integration, sourcing and compatibility support helps customers move from selection to implementation with fewer disconnects between product choice and real vehicle application.

Operational support awareness

Documentation, warranty coordination and reverse logistics all form part of the broader support picture referenced in the source profile. This makes the offering more practical for organisations that need accountability and continuity after supply.
