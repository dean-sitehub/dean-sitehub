About Us

3Gen products connects the South African automotive market to OEM-grade vehicle technology designed for visibility, safety and integration.

Who we are

3Gen products operates in the automotive technology and vehicle accessories space with a clear technical focus. The business is positioned as the authorized reseller of Directed Technologies in South Africa, giving local customers access to connected vehicle systems developed for OEM-grade use. That means the offer is centred on equipment and platforms intended to integrate cleanly with vehicles and support practical operational outcomes.

The company’s approach is shaped by the needs of automotive buyers who cannot afford guesswork around compatibility, support or long-term usability. Instead of treating telematics, infotainment, tracking, accessories and safety devices as separate one-off products, 3Gen products presents them as part of a wider connected vehicle ecosystem. This is important for fleets, dealer channels and OEM-linked programmes that need a more organised supply and support relationship.

What makes 3Gen products worth choosing is the combination of product access and operational thinking. The business supports sourcing, OEM integration, documentation, distribution considerations, warranty processes and after-sales service alongside the technology itself. For customers, that creates a more dependable path from enquiry to deployment, with less friction between the product selected and the vehicle environment it must work in.

What drives the business

Quality-first product selection

3Gen products is built around OEM-grade standards rather than novelty hardware. The focus is on systems that can support vehicle integration, practical field use and credible long-term deployment.

Reliability in the real world

Automotive and fleet buyers need solutions that continue to make sense after delivery. That is why the business pairs products with support areas such as sourcing, warranty handling and service management.

Technology with operational purpose

The product range is tied to real outcomes: visibility across fleets, safer driving environments, better in-cabin functionality, clearer asset monitoring and stronger personal security capability.
