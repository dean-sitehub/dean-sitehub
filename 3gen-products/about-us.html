About Us

Built around connected vehicle technology that fits real automotive requirements

3Gen products focuses on supplying systems that work within OEM expectations, fleet operations and long-term service realities.

The business behind the solutions

3Gen products positions itself as the South African authorized reseller of Directed Technologies, supplying OEM-grade connected vehicle technologies and related integration services to the local market. Its role is not limited to offering standalone devices; it brings together cloud platforms, telematics, infotainment, camera systems, tracking products, safety devices and accessories in a way that makes sense for automotive use. This gives customers a single specialist point of contact when evaluating connected vehicle technology in a demanding environment.

What drives the business is the need for technology that does more than look good on a brochure. Automotive and fleet customers need systems that interface correctly, fit operational processes, and continue to make sense after installation or delivery. That is why 3Gen products places so much emphasis on integration, sourcing, compliance support, technical documentation, warranty handling and after-sales structure alongside the product range itself.

The value of 3Gen products lies in how it helps customers move from interest to implementation with fewer unknowns. Whether the need is a connected infotainment platform, a fleet camera setup, asset tracking for high-value equipment, or a personal safety device for mobile workers, the business approaches the requirement through the lens of fit, compatibility and practical support. That solutions-focused approach helps customers make more confident purchasing decisions.

What makes 3Gen products worth choosing

The business combines product access with operational thinking. Customers are not left to connect the dots alone between hardware, software, documentation and service pathways. That is particularly valuable where OEM standards, fleet risk, or compatibility requirements influence the buying decision.
