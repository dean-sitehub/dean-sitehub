About Us

A business focused on OEM-grade vehicle technology, integration-minded sourcing and practical support for the South African automotive market.

The role 3Gen products plays

3Gen products operates in the connected vehicle technology space, supplying solutions that help vehicles, fleets and mobile assets become more visible, more informed and more useful in day-to-day operations. As the South African authorized reseller of Directed Technologies, the company acts as a route to OEM-grade platforms and hardware for customers who need reliable automotive technology rather than loosely matched aftermarket parts. Its offering spans cloud software, telematics, infotainment, camera systems, asset tracking, vehicle accessories and personal safety devices.

What makes the business relevant is the way it bridges products and practical deployment. Automotive technology rarely works well when it is treated as a simple box to install and forget, especially where CAN bus communication, OEM alignment, channel distribution or support obligations are involved. 3Gen products is positioned around that reality, helping customers think through sourcing, fit, distribution and after-sales continuity so the technology serves the vehicle program instead of complicating it.

The company’s work is grounded in OEM-grade expectations: compatibility matters, documentation matters and support after delivery matters. This is why the business profile highlights areas such as warranty handling, reverse logistics and technical support alongside the product range itself. For customers, that translates into a more considered buying experience and a stronger sense that the solution has been chosen with the whole lifecycle in mind.

What drives the business

OEM-grade standards

Products and services are positioned around quality, compatibility and long-term usability inside real automotive programs. That matters when the requirement goes beyond a standalone gadget and into integrated vehicle systems.

Integration thinking

The business is not only about supplying devices. It focuses on how cloud platforms, hardware, distribution and after-sales processes connect into one workable solution for the customer.

Trust and reliability

Automotive buyers need dependable support, clear technical direction and products that align with operational reality. The business positioning reflects that need for stable, credible execution.

Innovation with purpose

Connected vehicle technology should improve visibility, safety, convenience and decision-making. 3Gen products brings innovation into areas where it can create measurable usefulness for fleets, programs and drivers.
