The 3Gen products approach

3Gen products operates in the space where vehicles, software, electronics and operational demands all need to meet cleanly. The business is positioned as the South African authorized reseller of Directed Technologies, making OEM-grade connected vehicle systems available to customers who need dependable solutions rather than consumer-level accessories. Its work is relevant to fleet-focused environments, dealer channels and OEM-aligned programs where technical fit, data integrity and ongoing support carry real weight.

What makes the business distinct is the way it combines product access with integration thinking. Instead of treating telematics, infotainment, cameras, trackers and safety devices as separate purchases, 3Gen products frames them as part of a connected vehicle ecosystem that should work coherently across hardware, software and support processes. That includes attention to CAN BUS compatibility, documentation, warranty pathways, reverse logistics and after-sales service, which are often the details that determine whether a deployment succeeds long term.

The company’s values are visible in the kind of solutions it brings to market: OEM-grade quality, trust, practical reliability and an emphasis on innovation that serves real operational needs. Customers looking for cloud-connected insight, safer driver monitoring, improved in-cabin systems or better asset visibility need technology that stands up to daily use and aligns with established automotive standards. 3Gen products exists to make that process clearer and more workable for the South African market.

Why customers consider 3Gen products

Authorized access

Customers gain access to Directed Technologies solutions through an authorized South African reseller. That matters when product credibility, continuity and support context are important to the decision.

Integration mindset

The business focuses on how systems fit into vehicles, programs and operational workflows. This helps customers avoid disconnected technology choices that create extra complexity later.

Operational support

Warranty handling, technical documentation and after-sales considerations are part of the picture. That makes the offering more useful for customers who need dependable rollout and support continuity.
