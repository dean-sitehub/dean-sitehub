About Us

Focused on OEM-grade vehicle technology, practical integration, and support that extends beyond the point of sale.

Built around connected vehicle capability

3Gen products operates in the South African automotive technology space as the authorized reseller of Directed Technologies. Its role is to make proven connected vehicle systems more accessible to local automotive and fleet customers who need telematics, infotainment, tracking, camera systems, safety devices, and integrated accessories. The business speaks to customers who value fit, compatibility, and dependable support as much as the product itself.

The company’s story is defined by a practical view of how vehicle technology is actually deployed. In many environments, buyers do not need isolated devices; they need systems that can connect to vehicle architecture, support compliance and documentation requirements, and align with broader operational processes. That is why 3Gen products combines sourcing and product supply with integration thinking, distribution support, and after-sales considerations.

Its values come through in the way the offering is positioned: quality first, trust and reliability, innovation, and OEM-grade standards. Rather than chasing broad consumer appeal, the business is focused on customers who need a more disciplined technology stack for vehicles and assets. That promise is ultimately about giving buyers a cleaner path to connected solutions that work properly in the field and remain supportable over time.

Why businesses choose 3Gen products

Authorised channel

3Gen products is positioned as the South African authorized reseller of Directed Technologies, giving customers a clearer route to the brand’s connected vehicle portfolio.

Integration-minded supply

The business focuses on systems designed to work within vehicle programs and OEM contexts, rather than disconnected add-ons that create support problems later.

Operational follow-through

Support areas such as documentation, warranty handling, sourcing, and reverse logistics are part of the value discussion, not an afterthought once products ship.
