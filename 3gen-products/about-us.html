About Us

A South African route to OEM-grade connected vehicle technology

3Gen products connects automotive customers with technology portfolios that are engineered for compatibility, cleaner integration and long-term operational use.

Who we are

3Gen products is positioned as the South African authorized reseller of Directed Technologies, supplying OEM-grade connected vehicle technologies and related integration support to the local automotive market. The focus is not only on product availability, but on selecting solutions that work properly within vehicle systems, dealer environments and fleet operations. That matters when technology needs to do more than simply switch on after installation.

The business sits at the point where automotive hardware, connected data and practical deployment meet. Customers may need a telematics device with CAN integration, an infotainment platform suited to branded vehicle programs, a connected camera system for safety review, or asset tracking hardware for non-vehicle equipment. By covering these related categories together, 3Gen products gives buyers a way to think about connected mobility as a system rather than a collection of isolated purchases.

The approach is grounded in OEM-grade standards, compatibility and supportability. That means paying attention to sourcing, program fit, technical documentation, compliance considerations and after-sales requirements that often get overlooked when products are sold as standalone accessories. For customers who need a more structured and reliable route into vehicle technology, that difference is significant.

What drives the business

At the centre of the business is the idea that connected vehicle technology should be useful, properly integrated and commercially sensible for the customer adopting it. That is why the offer extends beyond devices alone into sourcing, OEM alignment, distribution pathways and support processes that help products succeed after delivery.

Rather than using broad marketing language, 3Gen products speaks to practical concerns: data quality, vehicle compatibility, fitment context, visibility, safety and customer support. This solutions-first mindset makes the business relevant to buyers who need technology to support actual operations, not just product lists.

Why customers choose 3Gen products

Customers looking at 3Gen products are often balancing technical requirements with operational realities. They may need systems that fit OEM branded programs, improve fleet visibility, support incident review, protect lone workers or add meaningful in-cabin functionality without compromising vehicle integration.

That combination of OEM-grade product access, integration awareness and after-sales thinking is what makes the business worth considering. It is especially valuable for buyers who want a supplier that understands how products behave inside larger automotive and fleet ecosystems.
