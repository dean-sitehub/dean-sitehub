About the business

About Us

3Gen products serves the South African automotive market with OEM-grade connected vehicle technology, accessory integration and support shaped around practical deployment.

Get In Touch

A focused automotive technology partner

3Gen products is positioned as the South African authorised reseller of Directed Technologies, bringing OEM-grade connected vehicle systems into a local market that needs reliability, compatibility and operational follow-through. The business offering spans telematics, IoT devices, multimedia systems, advanced cameras, asset tracking, vehicle accessories and personal safety technology. That combination makes the company relevant to fleet operators, dealers, automotive programs and other buyers who need connected systems to work across real vehicles and real support environments.

The business story is defined less by broad claims and more by technical fit. Rather than treating each product category as an isolated item, 3Gen products presents them as part of a wider vehicle ecosystem that may include cloud platforms, CAN interfacing, distribution pathways, warranty requirements and brand alignment. This approach is useful for customers who need systems to integrate neatly with vehicle platforms and program requirements instead of simply being installed and left to the operator to figure out alone.

At its core, 3Gen products reflects a quality-first mindset. The emphasis on OEM-grade standards, sourcing discipline, documentation and after-sales considerations shows a business built for long-term usability rather than short-term supply. For customers, that means dealing with a company that understands the pressure points around compatibility, field support, visibility and safety, and works to make the technology easier to deploy and support with confidence.

What makes 3Gen products worth choosing

Authorised reseller position

The business is presented as the authorised reseller of Directed Technologies in South Africa. That matters for customers who want a clear line between the technology source, the local market offering and the support context around deployment.

Integration-led thinking

3Gen products goes beyond supplying individual components by addressing CAN bus integration, OEM alignment and visual system sourcing. This is valuable where the technology must fit a broader vehicle or fleet environment without creating avoidable compatibility issues.

Operational support awareness

Distribution models, warranty handling, technical documentation and reverse logistics are part of the conversation. That makes the offer more practical for businesses planning ongoing serviceability after the first order is delivered.

Visibility and safety focus

With telematics, cameras, tracking and security devices in the range, the company is well aligned to customers prioritising fleet oversight, incident evidence, worker safety and better operational awareness.
