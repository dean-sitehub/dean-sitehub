The Business Behind the Systems

3Gen products operates in the space where vehicles, data, safety and integration meet. The business serves the South African automotive market with OEM-grade connected vehicle technologies, supplying solutions that range from telematics and IoT devices to multimedia systems, camera platforms, asset tracking and personal safety devices. Its customer value comes from understanding that the right technology choice is not only about features, but also about fit, reliability and ongoing support.

A defining part of the company’s position is its role as the authorized reseller of Directed Technologies in South Africa. Directed Technologies is recognised as Australia’s leading designer and manufacturer of OEM-grade connected vehicle technologies, and 3Gen products brings that capability into the local market through a more accessible commercial and support relationship. This gives customers a path to high-standard technology while still engaging with a supplier focused on local implementation needs, program compatibility and communication.

The business approach is shaped by practical execution. In addition to supplying products, 3Gen products supports OEM integration and sourcing, distribution pathways, technical documentation, warranty processes and after-sales coordination. For customers, that means fewer handoffs between disconnected vendors and a better chance of getting a vehicle technology solution that can be evaluated, deployed and supported with clarity.

What makes 3Gen products worth choosing is not a flashy promise, but a disciplined focus on quality, trust and technical relevance. The company works in a field where compatibility matters, compliance matters and poor integration decisions can create expensive complications later. By keeping attention on OEM-grade standards and customer-specific use cases, 3Gen products helps organisations move forward with more confidence in the systems they install and support.

What Guides the Work

Quality First

The product mix and service approach are built around OEM-grade expectations. That keeps the focus on systems that are engineered for dependable use, not just short-term convenience.

Trust and Reliability

Customers need clear answers on compatibility, rollout and support. 3Gen products aims to be straightforward about scope and suitable solution pathways, especially when integration decisions carry long-term consequences.

Innovation That Serves a Purpose

The technologies offered are modern and connected, but the end goal is always practical value. Better visibility, better safety, better usability and better alignment with operational needs are what matter.
