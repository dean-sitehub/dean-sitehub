OEM-grade connected vehicle focus

About Us

3Gen products exists to help South African automotive and fleet buyers access connected technology that is built for integration, usability and operational reliability.

The business behind the systems

3Gen products positions itself as the South African authorized reseller of Directed Technologies, bringing OEM-grade connected vehicle systems into a market that increasingly depends on visibility, compatibility and usable data. The business focuses on practical automotive technology categories including telematics, multimedia platforms, advanced cameras, asset tracking and safety devices. Its customer base is likely to include automotive channels, fleet operators and organisations that need products to perform in real operating environments rather than in isolated demos.

The strength of the business lies in combining product access with a more structured support approach. Instead of treating each requirement as a simple box-sale, 3Gen products also addresses the surrounding realities of OEM alignment, sourcing, integration pathways, documentation and after-sales management. That makes it especially relevant where a customer needs a clearer route from requirement to deployment, with attention paid to how systems fit into the vehicle, the program or the operational workflow.

At the centre of the offering is a quality-first view of connected vehicle technology. 3Gen products is built around reliable supply, technical relevance and solutions that help customers gain better control of data, safety or in-vehicle functionality. Whether the need is a cloud platform that turns vehicle data into insight, an infotainment system that integrates with existing controls, or a safety device that protects lone workers in the field, the business is geared toward useful technology with credible automotive application.

What makes 3Gen products worth choosing

Authorized product access

3Gen products is positioned as the authorized reseller of Directed Technologies in South Africa, giving customers access to a portfolio developed around connected vehicle requirements and OEM-grade standards.

Integration-minded approach

The business does not separate technology from deployment reality. Products are considered in the context of vehicle compatibility, branded programs, operational use and downstream support requirements.

Support beyond supply

Evidence indicates support can extend into warranty handling, service processes, reverse logistics and technical documentation. This makes the relationship more useful for customers who need continuity after implementation.

Clear connected vehicle focus

Everything in the portfolio ties back to visibility, safety, in-vehicle experience or asset awareness. That clear focus helps customers evaluate solutions around practical outcomes rather than generic electronics sourcing.
