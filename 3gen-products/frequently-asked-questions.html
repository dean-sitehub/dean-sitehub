Frequently Asked Questions

These answers are designed to help buyers understand how 3Gen products handles enquiries, project fit, pricing and support around OEM-grade connected vehicle technologies.

How do I start an enquiry with 3Gen products?

The easiest route is to send an email through the contact details on the site or use the contact form on the home page. It helps to describe the type of vehicles, fleet or program you are working with, the technologies you need, and whether the requirement is for supply, integration guidance or an OEM-aligned accessory rollout.

What kinds of solutions can I ask about?

You can enquire about the e.things Cloud Platform, telematics and IoT devices, multimedia and infotainment systems, connected cameras, asset tracking, vehicle accessories, safety devices and OEM integration services. If your requirement spans more than one category, 3Gen products can help structure the conversation around the full deployment rather than isolated products.

Who are these solutions for?

The offering is relevant to automotive businesses, fleet operators, OEM programs, dealers and organisations managing mobile workforces or high-value assets. Customers usually approach 3Gen products when they need stronger compatibility, better visibility, or a more supportable technology choice than generic aftermarket alternatives.

What is included beyond the hardware itself?

3Gen products is positioned around more than supply alone. Depending on the project, support can include sourcing, integration guidance, documentation, distribution model alignment, warranty handling and after-sales support considerations so the solution continues to make operational sense after rollout.

How is pricing handled?

Pricing will depend on the exact product mix, vehicle environment, integration requirements and rollout model. Because these are OEM-grade technologies and not one-size-fits-all retail items, the best way to get accurate pricing is to share the application, quantities and support needs so the response can match the scope.

How long does supply or implementation take?

Lead times vary according to product type, sourcing requirements, volume and whether the project involves integration or distribution planning. A straightforward product enquiry may move differently from an OEM accessory program or fleet technology deployment, so timelines are normally confirmed once the application is understood.

Can 3Gen products support OEM-branded programs?

Yes, OEM integration and sourcing is one of the core service areas described by the business. The focus is on delivering visual and accessory systems that align with OEM requirements and maintaining compatibility, brand fit and operational practicality across the program.

What happens after installation or delivery?

Support remains an important part of the offer. 3Gen products references warranty, documentation, technical support and reverse-logistics considerations, which is valuable for customers who need long-term serviceability rather than a handover with no follow-through.

How do I get support if I am unsure which product is suitable?

Start with the operational problem you need to solve instead of choosing a product first. Whether the issue is fleet visibility, in-cab user experience, incident evidence, asset movement or lone-worker safety, 3Gen products can guide the conversation toward the category that best fits the use case.
