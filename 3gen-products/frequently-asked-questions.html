Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions buyers often ask when evaluating connected vehicle technology, accessories, and OEM-ready support.

How do I start an enquiry with 3Gen products?

You can begin by using the contact form on the home page or by emailing deankann@gmail.com. The most useful enquiries include the type of vehicles or assets involved, the category of solution you need, and whether the requirement is for fleet use, dealer supply, or an OEM-branded program.

What kinds of solutions does 3Gen products provide?

The business supplies e.things cloud platform services, telematics and IoT devices, multimedia and infotainment systems, advanced vehicle cameras, asset tracking, vehicle accessories, safety devices, and OEM integration support. It also supports related operational requirements such as sourcing, documentation, warranty coordination, and distribution models.

Does 3Gen products only sell hardware?

No. The offering goes beyond devices by combining hardware with cloud visibility, integration support, and after-sales considerations. That matters for customers who need an end-to-end solution rather than simply buying standalone products.

Are the products suitable for fleet use?

Yes, the range is especially relevant to fleets that need visibility, safety monitoring, event capture, predictive diagnostics, and asset tracking. Several solutions are designed to support operational oversight where consistency and durable field performance matter.

Can 3Gen products support OEM or dealer programs?

Yes. OEM integration and sourcing is one of the core value areas highlighted in the business profile. The focus is on systems that align with branded programs, compatible fitment, and the support framework needed for cleaner rollout and after-sales handling.

How is pricing handled?

Pricing is typically scope-based because the right solution depends on the vehicle environment, number of units, integration complexity, and support requirements. It is best to request a tailored discussion rather than expecting a one-size-fits-all price list.

How long does a project take?

Turnaround depends on the product category, sourcing requirements, and whether the enquiry involves integration planning, distribution considerations, or after-sales support processes. The best way to understand timing is to share your specific use case so the requirement can be assessed properly.

Is support available after supply or deployment?

Yes. The business profile specifically references warranty and service management, reverse logistics, documentation, and technical support-related capabilities. That means customers can engage 3Gen products beyond the initial supply stage.

What if I need both safety and driver experience solutions?

That is a common fit for the range. A customer may combine vehicle cameras, telematics, infotainment, accessories, and personal safety devices within one broader connected vehicle strategy, depending on the use case and vehicle program goals.
