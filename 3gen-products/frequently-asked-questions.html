Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about enquiries, solution scope, pricing approach, rollout expectations and after-sales support.

How do I get started with 3Gen products?

The simplest starting point is to send an email outlining your vehicle, fleet or program requirement. Include what type of solution you are considering, such as telematics, infotainment, asset tracking, cameras or safety devices, and any technical or deployment constraints you already know. From there, the discussion can focus on fit, integration considerations and the most suitable route forward.

What is included when I enquire about a solution?

An enquiry can lead into product guidance around the available technology categories, likely compatibility needs and the broader operational context behind the request. Depending on the requirement, this may include discussion around CAN bus integration, OEM alignment, distribution pathways and after-sales considerations. The emphasis is on matching the right product ecosystem to the intended use case.

Do you publish fixed pricing?

No fixed public pricing was evident in the available business information, and that makes sense for solutions that vary by vehicle type, integration level and deployment model. Products such as infotainment, trackers, cameras and accessories can differ significantly depending on scope and channel. The most accurate way to understand cost is to request a solution-specific quotation.

Who are these services for?

3Gen products is best suited to automotive buyers that need OEM-grade connected vehicle technology rather than generic consumer electronics. That can include fleet operators, OEM-linked programs, dealerships and businesses that need better asset visibility, in-vehicle systems or safety tools. The offer is especially relevant where technical fit and operational support are important.

What kinds of products can be supplied?

The known range includes the e.things Cloud Platform, telematics and IoT devices, multimedia and infotainment systems, advanced vehicle cameras, asset trackers, vehicle accessories, safety devices and OEM integration support. Distribution, warranty and service management also form part of the broader capability. This means customers can engage around both product supply and supporting operational processes.

How long does a project or rollout take?

Turnaround depends on what is being sourced or integrated, how many vehicles or assets are involved and whether the requirement sits inside an OEM or dealer program. A simple accessory requirement and a structured multi-channel rollout are very different jobs. Once the scope is defined, a more realistic timeline can be discussed.

Do you support OEM-branded programs?

Yes, OEM integration and sourcing is one of the identified service areas. That includes helping source systems intended to align with OEM-branded programs and supporting compatibility and brand fit. For customers, this reduces the risk of using products that feel disconnected from the vehicle program they are meant to support.

What happens after supply or deployment?

The business profile indicates support around warranty, reverse logistics and technical documentation, which are important parts of the ownership experience. That means the relationship does not stop at product delivery. Customers can expect a more structured after-sales path than a simple one-time transaction.

How do I get support?

Email is the clearest confirmed contact method in the available information. If you need product guidance, support clarification or want to discuss a potential deployment, use the contact option on the website to start the conversation. Providing clear information up front usually speeds up the response and helps move the enquiry in the right direction.
