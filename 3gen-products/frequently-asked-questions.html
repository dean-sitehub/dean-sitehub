Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers for buyers evaluating connected vehicle technology

These answers cover the questions customers commonly ask before discussing telematics, infotainment, tracking, safety devices and OEM-aligned sourcing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started with 3Gen products?

The simplest way to start is by sending an enquiry through the contact form or by email. Share the type of vehicles, fleet or program you are working with, the category of solution you need and any integration or branding requirements you already know about. That gives 3Gen products a practical starting point for the conversation.

What kinds of products do you supply?

3Gen products focuses on OEM-grade connected vehicle technologies and accessories. The main categories include the e.things Cloud Platform, telematics and IoT devices, multimedia and infotainment systems, advanced vehicle cameras, asset tracking, vehicle accessories, safety and personal security devices, and OEM integration and sourcing support.

Who are these solutions for?

The offering is well suited to automotive businesses, dealer programs, fleet operations and buyers who need more structured vehicle technology than generic aftermarket accessories usually provide. If you need integration support, cleaner data capture or better alignment with branded programs, the product mix is designed around those needs.

Is installation included?

The source material supports sourcing, integration and after-sales support, but it does not specify a universal installation model for every product. The best approach is to ask about your exact requirement so the right delivery path, fitment expectations and support scope can be discussed before any decision is made.

How is pricing handled?

No fixed public pricing is presented in the available business information, which is sensible for solutions that vary by product category, fleet size, vehicle type and integration needs. Pricing is typically shaped by the solution required, the complexity of the deployment and any associated support or sourcing requirements.

How long does turnaround usually take?

Turnaround depends on the solution category, sourcing pathway and technical requirements. A straightforward accessory enquiry is naturally different from a broader OEM integration or connected fleet project, so timelines are best confirmed once the application and requirements are known.

What support is available after supply?

3Gen products positions itself around more than product supply alone. The business also supports areas such as documentation, warranty-related processes, reverse logistics and technical guidance, which is valuable for customers who need continuity after the initial product decision.

Do you offer solutions beyond vehicle tracking?

Yes. Tracking is only one part of the portfolio. The wider offering also covers infotainment, connected cameras, accessories, cloud data platforms and personal security devices, which means customers can address multiple vehicle technology requirements through one supplier relationship.

Can these products align with OEM branded programs?

Yes, that is one of the more important differentiators in the business profile. 3Gen products supports sourcing and delivery of visual and accessory systems that integrate with OEM branded programs, helping customers avoid a disconnected or mismatched product approach.

How do I ask for technical guidance?

The best option is to outline your vehicle environment, operational goal and any known constraints in your enquiry. That allows the discussion to move quickly toward the right category, likely integration points and the support needed to evaluate fit, compatibility and rollout expectations.
