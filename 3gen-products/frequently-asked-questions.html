Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for automotive and fleet buyers evaluating connected vehicle technology

This page explains how to enquire, what is typically included, how pricing is approached, and what support looks like after supply.

How do I start an enquiry with 3Gen products?

You can start by emailing the business through the contact section on the website. The most helpful first step is to describe the vehicle type, fleet or asset requirement, the technology category you are considering, and whether you need OEM program alignment or broader operational support.

What kinds of solutions does 3Gen products provide?

The business supplies e.things Cloud Platform services, telematics and IoT devices, multimedia and infotainment systems, advanced vehicle cameras, asset tracking solutions, vehicle accessories, safety and personal security devices, and OEM integration and sourcing support. These solutions are intended for automotive and fleet use rather than general consumer electronics supply.

Who are these solutions for?

They are suited to automotive businesses, OEM-aligned programs, fleets, dealer networks, and organisations managing connected vehicles or high-value assets. They are especially relevant where compatibility, operational visibility, safety monitoring, and structured after-sales support matter.

What is included besides the product itself?

Depending on the requirement, support may include sourcing, integration guidance, compliance-related consideration, technical documentation, warranty handling, reverse logistics and service management. 3Gen products positions itself around the full delivery path, not only a once-off hardware transaction.

How is pricing structured?

No public fixed pricing is presented in the available business information. Pricing is likely to depend on solution type, quantity, integration complexity, hardware selection, software scope, and whether additional sourcing or support services are required. The best route is to request a tailored discussion based on your application.

How long does implementation take?

Turnaround time depends on what you need. A straightforward accessory or device supply requirement may differ greatly from an OEM integration or multi-component connected vehicle project. 3Gen products can clarify likely lead times once your use case and technical scope are known.

Can 3Gen products support OEM-branded programs?

Yes. One of the core differentiators in the source material is OEM integration and sourcing support, including compatibility, brand alignment and delivery pathways such as line fit, port fit and direct-to-dealer distribution. This makes the business relevant for structured automotive programs rather than only standalone aftermarket requests.

Do you offer support after supply?

Yes, after-sales support is part of the business positioning. Source material references warranty management, technical documentation, operational support and reverse logistics, which are important for customers who need continuity after deployment rather than a simple handover.

How do I get help choosing the right solution?

The best approach is to explain the vehicle environment, data requirement, safety concern, or integration target you are trying to solve. This allows 3Gen products to guide you toward the most suitable platform, device, camera, accessory, or security configuration instead of forcing a generic package.

Still need clarity?

If your requirement involves telematics, infotainment, cameras, tracking or OEM-aligned sourcing, contact 3Gen products with your application details.
