Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about engagement, scope, pricing, timing and support for connected vehicle technology projects.

How do I get started with 3Gen products?

The easiest starting point is to contact the business by email or through the enquiry form on the home page. Share the type of vehicles, assets or safety requirement you are dealing with, and outline whether you need telematics, infotainment, cameras, tracking or broader OEM integration support. That makes it easier to guide the discussion toward the right product category and implementation route.

What is included when I enquire about a solution?

An enquiry can cover product suitability, integration considerations and support expectations. Depending on the requirement, the conversation may include compatibility, CAN bus considerations, sourcing, distribution pathway and after-sales needs, so the recommendation is based on how the system will actually be used.

Do you only supply products, or do you help with integration too?

3Gen products is not limited to simple product supply. The business also supports OEM integration and sourcing, plus surrounding needs such as documentation, warranty handling, reverse logistics and program-aligned delivery support where relevant.

Who are these solutions best suited to?

The offering is most relevant to automotive stakeholders, fleets, dealer-related programs and organisations that need connected vehicle technologies to operate reliably at scale. If your requirement depends on better data, safer driving oversight, cleaner in-cabin systems or stronger compatibility with branded vehicle programs, the service fit is likely worth exploring.

How is pricing structured?

Pricing depends on the solution category, the type of device or system required, the level of integration involved and the broader scope of support needed. Because offerings range from standalone accessories to cloud-connected platforms and OEM-aligned hardware programs, a tailored quotation is more accurate than a fixed public price list.

How long does turnaround usually take?

Turnaround varies according to product availability, integration complexity and whether the request involves sourcing, distribution or support elements beyond supply. The best approach is to outline the use case early so timing expectations can be discussed in context rather than guessed upfront.

Can you help with fleet visibility and driver safety together?

Yes, the portfolio is designed to support connected use cases across visibility, diagnostics and safety. For example, telematics devices, cloud reporting and vehicle camera solutions can work together to create a more complete view of vehicle performance, incident history and driver behaviour.

What if we need tracking for assets rather than vehicles?

Asset tracking solutions are available for trailers, containers and other high-value mobile assets. Different power and durability options make it easier to match the tracker to the environment in which that asset operates, whether continuous vehicle power is available or not.

Do you offer support after the product is supplied?

Yes, after-sales support forms part of the wider value proposition. Depending on the project, this can include warranty coordination, service management, technical documentation and support processes that help customers manage the solution after deployment.

How do I request support?

You can email 3Gen products directly with details of the product category, issue or support need. Providing a concise summary of the vehicle program, device type or operational problem helps the business respond more effectively and route the request appropriately.
