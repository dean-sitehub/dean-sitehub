Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about product selection, rollout planning, pricing, support and the best way to start an enquiry.

Common customer questions

How do I start an enquiry with 3Gen products?

You can start by sending an email through the contact section on the home page. The most useful enquiries explain the vehicle application, the type of technology needed and whether the requirement relates to fleet use, dealer distribution, OEM programme alignment or a specific accessory category.

What kinds of solutions does 3Gen products supply?

The offering includes the e.things Cloud Platform, telematics and IoT devices, multimedia and infotainment systems, advanced vehicle cameras, asset tracking solutions, vehicle accessories, safety devices and OEM integration support. The business is focused on connected vehicle technology rather than general automotive servicing.

Who are these products for?

These solutions are suitable for automotive programmes, fleet operators, dealer channels and businesses that need integrated vehicle technology with a more structured support model. They are especially relevant where compatibility, data visibility, safety and after-sales support are important.

Is installation included?

The source material strongly supports product supply, sourcing, integration and after-sales support, but does not clearly define a universal installation model for every solution. The best approach is to enquire with your vehicle or programme requirements so 3Gen products can clarify the appropriate supply and implementation path.

How is pricing handled?

Pricing depends on the solution category, vehicle compatibility, quantity, integration scope and support requirements. Because these are OEM-grade and programme-sensitive technologies, customers should expect pricing to follow the application and technical brief rather than a one-price-fits-all list.

What is included beyond the hardware itself?

3Gen products also supports areas such as OEM integration, sourcing, documentation, compliance-related considerations, warranty handling, reverse logistics and after-sales service management. This matters for customers who need a cleaner operational process rather than a simple product handoff.

How long does rollout or supply take?

Turnaround time depends on the type of product, the level of integration needed and the intended distribution model, such as line fit, port fit or direct-to-dealer supply. Once the application is understood, 3Gen products can advise on the next steps and likely delivery path.

Can 3Gen products help with fleet visibility and safety?

Yes, this is one of the most relevant areas of the portfolio. Telematics, connected cameras, tracking devices and personal security products all contribute to stronger visibility, better event reporting and improved awareness across vehicles, assets and personnel.

How do I get support after supply?

The business positions after-sales support as part of its offer, alongside warranty and service management. Customers should use the contact channel to explain the product category and support need so the request can be directed correctly.
