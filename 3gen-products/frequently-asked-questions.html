Frequently Asked Questions

If you are assessing telematics, camera systems, infotainment or OEM-aligned accessory solutions, these are the common questions buyers tend to ask before starting the conversation.

How do I enquire about a product or project?

You can contact 3Gen products by email or through the contact form on the website. The most helpful starting point is to describe the vehicle type, the intended use case and whether you need a single product, a broader solution or OEM integration support.

What kinds of solutions does 3Gen products provide?

The business focuses on connected vehicle technology and related accessories, including the e.things Cloud Platform, telematics and IoT devices, multimedia systems, advanced vehicle cameras, asset trackers, security devices and OEM integration support. The offering is especially relevant where compatibility, data visibility and operational use all matter.

Who are these solutions suitable for?

The solutions are suited to automotive and fleet-related buyers in South Africa who need OEM-grade hardware and connected systems. That can include vehicle programs, commercial fleets, dealer-related distribution channels and organisations managing mobile assets or field safety requirements.

Does 3Gen products only supply hardware?

No. The value of the business is not limited to supplying products. It also includes sourcing, OEM-aligned integration thinking, support around compatibility and access to after-sales processes such as warranty, documentation and service management where applicable.

How is pricing handled?

Pricing depends on the product category, installation or integration requirements, project scope and the quantity involved. Because requirements differ across vehicles and programs, the best approach is to request a tailored quote based on your intended application rather than expecting a one-size-fits-all price list.

What is included when I request a quote?

Quotes typically start with identifying the solution category and the intended operational outcome. Depending on the requirement, this may include product selection, compatibility guidance, sourcing, and discussion around deployment or support needs.

How long does the process take?

Turnaround time depends on the complexity of the requirement. A straightforward product enquiry can move quickly, while OEM-oriented programs or multi-part solutions may require additional review to confirm fit, scope and support requirements.

Can 3Gen products assist with support after supply?

Yes. Source information indicates support can extend to after-sales areas such as warranty handling, service management, reverse logistics and technical documentation. That matters for customers who want continuity after deployment rather than a handoff at the point of sale.

Are the solutions intended for OEM integration?

Yes, that is a core part of the positioning. 3Gen products supports systems designed to integrate with OEM branded programs and provides sourcing and compatibility support for customers who need a more structured, aligned implementation path.

How do I get ongoing updates or contact details?

You can use the website contact form, email directly, or follow the linked social pages for business updates. For project-specific communication, email is the clearest route because it allows you to share technical requirements from the start.
