Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about engaging 3Gen products for connected vehicle technology, sourcing, integration and ongoing support.

How do I get started with 3Gen products?

The easiest way to begin is to send an email through the contact section and outline what type of vehicles, assets or safety requirements you need to support. A good enquiry usually includes the operating environment, the type of product needed and whether you require OEM integration, sourcing or after-sales guidance.

What is included when I enquire about a solution?

Enquiries can cover product suitability, integration requirements, deployment considerations and the support elements around the technology. Depending on the need, discussions may include telematics hardware, cloud connectivity, infotainment options, cameras, trackers, accessories and documentation-related requirements.

Do you only supply hardware?

No. 3Gen products also supports OEM integration and sourcing, along with broader operational elements such as documentation, warranty pathways and after-sales service. That is especially useful when a customer needs a solution to fit into an existing automotive program rather than simply purchasing a standalone unit.

Who are these solutions for?

The offering is relevant to fleet operators, dealer programs, OEM-related channels and businesses that need connected technology in or around vehicles and mobile assets. It also suits organisations that want stronger safety visibility for drivers, field staff or lone workers.

How is pricing handled?

Pricing depends on the combination of products, integration requirements, deployment model and support scope. Because needs can vary significantly between telematics, infotainment, tracking, cameras and safety devices, the best approach is to request a tailored response based on your use case.

How long does delivery or rollout take?

Lead times depend on the product category, sourcing requirements and whether the solution needs OEM-aligned integration or distribution coordination. Once your requirements are clear, 3Gen products can indicate what is involved and what to expect for the next steps.

Can you help with CAN BUS and vehicle integration considerations?

Yes. CAN BUS and compatibility support form part of the business focus, particularly where technology needs to interface with existing vehicle systems. This helps reduce mismatches between the chosen hardware and the vehicle environment it needs to serve.

Do you provide support after supply?

Yes. 3Gen products positions its offering around more than initial supply by including support themes such as warranty management, technical documentation and after-sales service. That makes the solution more sustainable for customers who need continuity after rollout.

Can I get help choosing between tracking, cameras and telematics?

Yes. These systems serve different purposes, and the right combination depends on whether your priority is asset visibility, driver behaviour insight, vehicle data, in-cabin functionality or worker safety. A clear consultation helps match the technology to the operational outcome you want.

How do I request support?

The most reliable route shown for this business is email. Send your enquiry to deankann@gmail.com with your project or support requirement so the request can be reviewed and directed appropriately.
