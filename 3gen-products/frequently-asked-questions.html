Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers about enquiries, pricing, support and what to expect when sourcing connected vehicle technology through 3Gen products.

Common questions

How do I enquire about a product or solution?

You can contact 3Gen products by email using the contact details on the site or by submitting the contact form. The best starting point is to explain the type of vehicles or assets involved, the technology you need and whether the requirement is for a fleet, dealer program, OEM-aligned rollout or a specific safety use case.

What is included when working with 3Gen products?

The business offers more than standalone devices. Depending on the requirement, support may involve product guidance, OEM integration alignment, sourcing, documentation, distribution pathway support, warranty coordination and after-sales service context around the supplied technology.

Do you only supply telematics?

No. 3Gen products supplies a broader connected vehicle portfolio that includes the e.things Cloud Platform, telematics and IoT devices, multimedia and infotainment systems, advanced vehicle cameras, asset tracking products, accessories and personal security devices.

How is pricing handled?

Pricing depends on the specific product category, the number of units, the integration requirements and the support scope around the project. Because the solutions are often tied to operational or OEM requirements, it is best to request a tailored quotation rather than expect fixed public pricing across every category.

How long does a project or order take?

Turnaround time varies according to the solution selected, stock pathway, integration needs and the complexity of the rollout. A single accessory requirement is very different from a broader telematics or OEM-branded program, so timelines are usually confirmed once the requirement is properly scoped.

Can you support OEM-branded or dealer-linked programs?

Yes, OEM integration and sourcing is part of the business focus. 3Gen products supports visual and accessory systems intended to align with OEM branded programs, helping customers manage compatibility, presentation and supply expectations more effectively.

Do your solutions support fleet safety?

Yes. Several categories directly support safer fleet operations, including advanced vehicle cameras, telematics devices, duress-focused safety products and asset tracking solutions. These tools help customers capture events, monitor conditions and respond more quickly to incidents or exceptions.

What kind of support is available after supply?

The business profile indicates after-sales support considerations such as warranty handling, reverse logistics and technical documentation. That structure is valuable for customers who need technology backed by a support process rather than a once-off transaction.

Can 3Gen products help with asset tracking outside standard vehicles?

Yes. Asset tracking solutions are intended for items such as trailers, containers and other high-value assets, not only passenger or commercial vehicles. Different power and environmental options are available depending on how and where the asset is used.

How do I get technical guidance on which solution fits my needs?

The best route is to describe your operational goal clearly—whether it is driver visibility, infotainment integration, lone-worker safety or asset monitoring. That allows 3Gen products to direct you toward the most suitable category and clarify what level of integration or support may be needed.

Still need help?

If your question is specific to a vehicle program, fleet rollout or product category, send an enquiry and we will help you narrow down the right next step.
