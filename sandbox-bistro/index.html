About Sandbox Bistro

Sandbox Bistro is a cozy, welcoming restaurant located in the heart of Cape Town. We serve fresh and locally sourced meals, tailored to satisfy the taste buds of our community and visitors alike. Whether you're looking for a hearty breakfast to start your day or a delightful lunch to energize you, we have you covered.

Our journey began with a simple idea: to create a space where people can enjoy delicious meals made from fresh ingredients while catching up with friends and family. Our chefs are passionate about preparing dishes that not only taste great but also bring people together. Each dish is crafted with care and attention to detail, ensuring a memorable dining experience.

At Sandbox Bistro, we believe in supporting local farmers and producers. Our commitment to sustainability and quality is reflected in every dish we serve. We strive to create a friendly and inclusive atmosphere where everyone feels at home. Join us for a meal and become a part of our community.
