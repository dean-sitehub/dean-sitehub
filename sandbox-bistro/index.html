About Sandbox Bistro

Sandbox Bistro is your go-to spot for delicious, locally-sourced meals in Cape Town. We cater to both the early risers and the lunch crowd, offering a variety of dishes that highlight the best seasonal ingredients.

Our story begins with a simple idea: to create a welcoming space where our community can enjoy fresh and flavorful meals. With a menu that changes with the seasons, we take pride in supporting local farmers and artisans.

At Sandbox Bistro, our promise is to provide a delightful dining experience with a focus on quality and sustainability. We believe in honest cooking and warm hospitality, ensuring that every visit feels like coming home.
