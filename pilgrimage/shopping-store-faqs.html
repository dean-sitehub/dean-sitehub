Shopping & Store FAQs

Answers to common questions about ordering handcrafted furniture and décor from Pilgrimage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I order online?

Yes. Pilgrimage is presented as an online store, so customers can browse and start purchases digitally. If you need advice before ordering, use the enquiry page or contact Pilgrimage directly.

Do you offer delivery?

Yes, delivery support is an important part of the customer experience. Review feedback highlights careful packaging, including for an order sent to Johannesburg, so customers outside Cape Town can enquire about delivery arrangements with confidence.

What kind of products do you sell?

Pilgrimage focuses on handcrafted furniture, textiles, homeware and decorative pieces. The range has an elegant, artisan-made feel with a distinctive Asian-infused style.

Can I ask about a specific item before buying?

Absolutely. If you want more detail about a furniture piece, homeware item or decorative accent, contact Pilgrimage first. This is especially useful if you need practical guidance on size, styling or delivery.

What is the return policy?

Return handling should be confirmed directly with Pilgrimage before sending anything back. If there is an issue with an order, contact the business promptly so the best next step can be discussed.

Can I reserve an item?

If you are seriously interested in a piece, the best approach is to enquire directly and ask about availability. Reservation arrangements, if offered, would need to be confirmed by Pilgrimage on a case-by-case basis.

Do you stock a specific brand?

The strongest evidence points to Pilgrimage as a curated artisan-focused furniture and homeware store rather than a standard multi-brand retailer. If you are searching for a particular style or product type, send an enquiry and describe what you need.
