How to Order, Pay & Return

Clear buying guidance helps you shop Pilgrimage with confidence, especially when choosing handcrafted furniture and home décor online.

Ordering Step by Step

1. Browse or enquire

You can start by visiting the shop page or by sending an enquiry if you want help with a specific item or style direction. This is useful when you want more clarity before placing an order.

2. Confirm the piece

Once you know what interests you, contact Pilgrimage to confirm the item and discuss any practical details related to delivery or handling. For larger furniture pieces, this step helps make sure the purchase fits your space and expectations.

3. Arrange payment

Live checkout and payment for shop items are handled through the hosted shop experience. If you are ordering through direct enquiry, confirm payment instructions with Pilgrimage before sending funds.

4. Delivery or collection planning

Pilgrimage is known for careful packaging, which is especially important for decorative and handcrafted items. If you are outside Cape Town, include your location early so delivery arrangements can be discussed clearly.

Payment Guidance

Use the hosted shop for live cart and checkout where available. For direct purchases arranged through enquiry, always wait for confirmed payment details from Pilgrimage. If you are unsure which route applies, call or email first so the process stays straightforward.

Delivery & Collection

Delivery options depend on the item and destination. Because furniture and homeware need proper handling, delivery costs and timelines should be confirmed before the order is finalised. If collection is available for your order, arrange it directly with Pilgrimage.

Returns & Exchanges

Because Pilgrimage sells handcrafted furniture and decorative items, customers should inspect their purchase as soon as it arrives. If there is a problem with the condition of an item or you need help with a return-related question, contact Pilgrimage promptly with your order details and photographs where relevant. Return acceptance, exchanges and any transport arrangements should be confirmed directly with the business before sending an item back.

Tip for Smoother Orders

Include the item name, your city, and any delivery access details when you enquire. That makes it easier to advise you accurately and helps reduce delays once you are ready to buy.
