Privacy Notice

This notice explains what information may be collected through the Pilgrimage website and how it may be used in practice.

Your Privacy

Information that may be collected

Pilgrimage may collect information you choose to provide when you contact the business by form, email or phone. This may include your name, email address, telephone number, location, and details about the furniture or homeware enquiry you submit. Basic technical data may also be collected through standard website use, such as browser type, device data and page visits.

How information may be used

Your information may be used to respond to enquiries, assist with product questions, discuss delivery arrangements, manage customer communication, and improve website performance. If you contact Pilgrimage about a purchase, your details may also be used to support ordering, delivery coordination and after-sales assistance.

Analytics and normal website use

Like most websites, this site may use basic analytics or hosting tools to understand traffic patterns and improve usability. This helps Pilgrimage see which pages are useful to visitors and where the site experience can be improved.

How long information may be kept

Enquiry and customer communication records may be retained for a reasonable period so Pilgrimage can respond to follow-up questions, assist with orders, and keep accurate business records. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of enquiry and any related transaction.

When information may be shared

Your information may be shared with trusted service providers where needed to operate the website, process forms, support hosted shop functionality, or coordinate delivery and communication. Information is not intended to be sold casually to third parties for unrelated marketing purposes.

Your choices and requests

If you want to ask what information Pilgrimage may hold about you, request a correction, or ask for help with your data, contact the business directly. The team will do its best to respond reasonably and practically to privacy-related requests.
