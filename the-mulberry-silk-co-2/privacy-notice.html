Privacy Notice

How customer information may be used

This notice explains the types of information that may be collected through the website and how The Mulberry Silk Co may use it in practice.

Privacy Information

1. Purpose of this notice

This privacy notice explains, in practical terms, how The Mulberry Silk Co may collect, use, store, and share personal information through normal website use, contact forms, email communication, linked tools, and hosted shopping systems. It is written for a South African business website and is intended as general guidance rather than legal advice. By using the site or contacting the business, you acknowledge that your information may be handled in line with this notice.

2. Information that may be collected

The business may receive information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, and the contents of any message sent through a form or by email. Standard website usage information may also be collected automatically, including browser type, device information, IP-related technical data, pages viewed, referral sources, and general session activity through analytics or similar tools. If you continue to a hosted shop or other third-party service, additional information may be collected there under that platform’s own privacy practices.

3. How information may be used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, help customers choose between products such as comforters, linen sets, covers, and sleep sets, improve website usability, monitor site performance, and support product-related communication. It may also be used to maintain records of conversations or requests so that follow-up support is more accurate and consistent. The business may use contact details to answer questions about browsing, ordering pathways, or support requests that you initiate.

4. How long information may be kept

Enquiry records, messages, and related communication may be retained for a reasonable period so the business can manage customer service, track previous discussions, and maintain ordinary business records. Technical website information may also be retained through analytics systems according to those tools’ standard retention settings. Information that is no longer reasonably needed may be deleted, anonymised, or securely removed where practical.

5. When information may be shared

The Mulberry Silk Co may share information with service providers that help operate the website or customer communication process, such as form providers, analytics services, hosting partners, map integrations, or ecommerce/checkout platforms. Information will generally be shared only where needed for those functions or where disclosure is required by law. The business does not treat casual website enquiries as public content and does not intend to publish personal contact details you submit privately.

6. Cookies, analytics, and normal browsing

Like most websites, this site may use cookies or similar technologies to support basic functionality, understand how visitors use the site, and improve the browsing experience. These tools can help identify which pages are used most often and whether navigation or contact pathways are working effectively. You can usually control cookie behaviour through your browser settings, although disabling some tools may affect how parts of the site perform.

7. Your choices and requests

If you would like help with the personal information you have shared, you can contact the business to ask about correction, update, or deletion requests where appropriate. You may also ask for help if you believe information is inaccurate or if you want clarity about how a previous enquiry was handled. Requests can be sent to martin@themulberrysilkco.com.

8. Third-party platforms

This website may connect to third-party platforms such as embedded forms, maps, social media, and a hosted shop. Those services operate independently and may collect data under their own policies once you interact with them. Customers should review those privacy notices separately when using external services linked from this site.

9. Updates to this notice

This privacy notice may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in business operations, website tools, or legal expectations. The latest version published on the site will apply from the date it appears. Continued use of the site after an update indicates acceptance of the revised notice.
