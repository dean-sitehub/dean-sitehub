Privacy Notice

How website information may be used

This notice explains the practical ways customer information may be collected and used when you browse this website, make contact, or follow links to connected services.

Information We May Collect

The Mulberry Silk Co website may collect information that you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, and the contents of a message sent through a contact form or by email. Standard website usage information may also be collected automatically, including browser type, device information, IP address, referral source, and general page activity. If you interact with an embedded form or third-party shopping page, additional data may be handled through those connected services.

How Information Is Used

Customer information may be used to respond to enquiries, provide product information, assist with support requests, improve website performance, and understand how visitors use the site. Information may also help the business manage communications related to products such as the Mulberry Silk Comforter, Silky Sleep Set, or other listed collections. Data is used for practical business purposes rather than for unrelated marketing claims.

Email, Forms, and Normal Site Use

If you contact the business by email at martin@themulberrysilkco.com or through the website form, your message and contact details may be retained so the business can respond and keep a record of the enquiry. Normal site use may also involve cookies, traffic measurement tools, or platform analytics that help monitor page visits and site functionality. These tools generally collect technical and behavioural information rather than detailed private correspondence.

How Long Information May Be Kept

Enquiry records and email communications may be kept for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, provide follow-up support, maintain business records, or meet operational and legal obligations. Technical analytics information may be retained according to the default settings of the platforms or service providers involved. Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the enquiry and the systems used.

When Information May Be Shared

Information may be shared with trusted service providers who help operate the website, forms, hosting, analytics, email tools, mapping tools, or the connected shop platform. Such sharing is limited to what is reasonably necessary for those services to function. The business may also disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect its website, users, or operations.

Your Choices and Requests

If you want help with your personal information, would like to update contact details, or want to ask how your enquiry has been handled, you can contact The Mulberry Silk Co directly. The business can be reached at martin@themulberrysilkco.com. This notice is provided as practical website guidance and should not be taken as legal advice.
