About The Mulberry Silk Co

The Mulberry Silk Co focuses on sleep comfort products for people who want their bedroom essentials to feel calm, practical, and easy to order online. The range centres on mulberry silk comforters, linen sets, pillow and comforter combinations, sleep sets, and silk covers that suit customers shopping for everyday rest as well as gift-worthy bedding. Everything is presented with a straightforward product-led approach so visitors can quickly understand what is available and choose what fits their sleep routine.

For more than two decades, the business has built its identity around shipping sleep comfort direct to customers’ doors. That direct-to-door model shapes the entire experience, from the way the products are grouped into featured and most-loved collections to the simple online paths that help customers move from browsing to buying. The result is a retail brand that feels focused rather than crowded, with attention kept on the products that define the business.

What sets The Mulberry Silk Co apart is its clear promise: make quality sleep comfort easier to access through a curated silk bedding offering and practical customer contact points. The brand voice is uncomplicated, warm, and useful, because customers are not looking for noise when they shop for bedding; they want confidence in what they are choosing. That is why the business keeps the emphasis on recognisable product categories, direct communication, and a more comfortable end result in the bedroom.
