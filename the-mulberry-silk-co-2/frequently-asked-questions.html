Frequently Asked Questions

Here are clear answers to the questions customers most often ask about ordering, product selection, pricing visibility, delivery timing, and support from The Mulberry Silk Co.

How do I order from The Mulberry Silk Co?

You can browse the product range through the online shop showcase and continue through to the hosted shop for live ordering. This is the easiest way to view items such as the Mulberry Silk Comforter, Mulberry Silk Linen Set, Silky Sleep Set, and Mulberry Silk Cover in one place. If you have questions before ordering, you can also email the business directly for guidance.

What products are included in the range?

The current range includes Our Featured Products, Mulberry Silk Comforter, Mulberry Silk Linen Set, Mulberry Silk Pillow and Comforter Set, Silky Sleep Set, Sleep In, Most Loved Products, and Mulberry Silk Cover. The catalogue is built around sleep comfort and coordinated bedding choices rather than unrelated home goods. That makes it easier to shop with a clear focus on silk-based sleep essentials.

Is pricing shown on this website?

This website acts as a business showcase and product introduction, while live ordering takes place through the hosted shop. Product snapshots may appear here, but the most relevant purchase journey is the linked shop experience. If you need clarification before placing an order, email support and mention the specific product name you are asking about.

How do I know what is included in a set?

Set names are grouped to help you shop by sleep need and bedroom setup. For example, the Mulberry Silk Pillow and Comforter Set is presented as a coordinated pairing, while options such as Silky Sleep Set and Mulberry Silk Linen Set are suited to customers wanting a more matched feel across their bedding. If you need exact buying guidance, contacting the business directly is the best next step.

How long does delivery take?

The source information confirms that The Mulberry Silk Co ships sleep comfort direct to your door, but it does not publish a fixed turnaround time on this website. Delivery timing may depend on the product selected and the order process in the hosted shop. For the most accurate update on availability or dispatch expectations, contact the business by email before or after placing your order.

Can I get help choosing the right product?

Yes. If you are deciding between a single item and a coordinated bundle, it helps to think about whether you want to refresh one layer of your bed or build a more complete silk sleep setup. Customers often compare the comforter, cover, linen set, and bundled pillow-and-comforter options when narrowing down the best fit.

What is the difference between featured and most loved products?

Featured products are presented as highlighted choices within the broader range, while most loved products point shoppers toward favourites that naturally attract attention. Both sections help simplify browsing. They are especially useful if you are new to the range and want a shorter list of starting points.

How do I get support after I enquire or order?

The clearest support channel published for this business is email. You can contact martin@themulberrysilkco.com with product questions, order follow-ups, or help selecting from the available mulberry silk bedding options. Including the product name in your message will make it easier for the business to assist you quickly.
