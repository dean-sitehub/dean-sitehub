Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers before you shop

Use this page to understand how The Mulberry Silk Co works, what the product range includes, and how to get support when you need it.

Common Questions

How do I place an order?

You can start by visiting the Online Shop page and choosing the collection or product that best matches your sleep setup. Each product card links through to the hosted shop, where live product viewing and checkout handling take place. If you would rather ask a question first, you can also contact the business by email before ordering.

What products does The Mulberry Silk Co offer?

The current range includes Our Featured Products, Mulberry Silk Comforter, Mulberry Silk Linen Set, Mulberry Silk Pillow and Comforter Set, Silky Sleep Set, Sleep In, Most Loved Products, and Mulberry Silk Cover. These product names reflect the core categories customers browse when looking for mulberry silk sleep comfort items. The catalogue is focused on bedding and coordinated sleep products rather than unrelated general merchandise.

What is included in a set or bundle?

The product names indicate coordinated combinations where relevant, such as the Mulberry Silk Pillow and Comforter Set or the Silky Sleep Set. For exact inclusions on a live item, customers should use the hosted shop listing or contact the business directly for confirmation. This helps ensure you review the specific product details for the item you are considering.

How is pricing shown?

Pricing is best checked on the hosted shop pages, where product-specific details are kept current. The showcase website is designed to help you explore the available range and move into the correct buying flow, rather than replace the shop system itself. If you need clarity on a particular item before purchase, email support is the most direct option.

How long does delivery or turnaround usually take?

The business positions itself around shipping sleep comfort direct to your door, but no fixed delivery timeline is published in the supplied information. For the most accurate turnaround expectations, customers should review the hosted shop details or contact the business directly before ordering. This is especially useful when buying a coordinated set for a planned room refresh or gift.

Can I get help choosing between products?

Yes. If you are not sure whether you need a comforter, a full linen set, a bundled pillow-and-comforter option, or a silk cover, you can contact The Mulberry Silk Co by email. A clear question about your preferred setup will make it easier to point you toward the most suitable product category.

How do I get support after browsing the site?

The simplest support route is to use the contact section on the home page or email martin@themulberrysilkco.com directly. You can also visit the official website if you want to browse further before reaching out. Social pages may also help you stay connected with the brand’s latest presentation and updates.

Still need help?

If your question is about a specific comforter, linen set, cover, or coordinated sleep bundle, the fastest next step is to contact the business directly or head to the hosted shop.
