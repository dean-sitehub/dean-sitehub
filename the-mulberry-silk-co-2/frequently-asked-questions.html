Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers before you shop

If you are comparing silk bedding options, these common questions explain how to browse the range, what the collections include, and how to contact The Mulberry Silk Co.

How do I order from The Mulberry Silk Co?

You can begin by browsing the shop showcase on this website and then follow the Shop Now links to the hosted online shop. From there, you can review the available product pages and continue with the live buying process. If you would rather ask a question before ordering, you can also contact the business by email.

What products are included in the range?

The current source-backed range includes Our Featured Products, Mulberry Silk Comforter, Mulberry Silk Linen Set, Mulberry Silk Pillow and Comforter Set, Silky Sleep Set, Sleep In, Most Loved Products, and Mulberry Silk Cover. These products are presented as a focused sleep comfort collection rather than a broad general catalogue.

What is included in a set or bundle?

The exact components depend on the product page you choose. Some items are single bedding pieces, while others are coordinated sets designed to simplify the shopping decision and create a more complete silk bedding arrangement. For the most accurate item detail, use the shop links to open the individual product page.

How is pricing shown?

Pricing is displayed in the hosted shop environment linked from this website. The showcase page here is a marketing snapshot that helps you browse the range before moving to the live shop for product-level detail. This keeps ordering and checkout in the correct place.

How long does delivery or turnaround usually take?

The supplied business profile confirms that The Mulberry Silk Co is focused on shipping sleep comfort direct to your door, but no fixed turnaround period was provided in the evidence supplied for this website build. If you need current delivery timing, the best option is to enquire directly before ordering. That allows the business to confirm the most up-to-date shipping information for your purchase.

Can I get help choosing between products?

Yes. If you are deciding between a comforter, a full linen set, a pillow and comforter set, or a cover, you can contact the business for guidance. This is especially useful if you are balancing bedroom styling, gifting, or starting with a first silk bedding purchase.

How do I get support after browsing?

You can email martin@themulberrysilkco.com or use the contact form on the home page. Social channels on Facebook and Instagram also provide an additional way to keep up with the brand and make contact if needed.
