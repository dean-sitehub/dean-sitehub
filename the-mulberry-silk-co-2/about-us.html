About Us

The story behind The Mulberry Silk Co

A sleep comfort brand built around mulberry silk essentials, straightforward online shopping, and products designed to feel good night after night.

Who We Are

The Mulberry Silk Co is an online retail business dedicated to sleep comfort products centred on mulberry silk. It serves customers who want their bedding choices to feel softer, more considered, and easier to buy without spending time searching across countless unrelated items. The range is intentionally focused, with products such as comforters, linen sets, coordinated pillow-and-comforter combinations, silky sleep sets, and covers that support a calmer bedtime environment.

The business presents itself with a practical, direct approach because bedding should not be difficult to shop for. Customers can browse featured ranges, look at most-loved selections, or head straight to the specific product format they need for their room. That clarity has helped shape the brand’s identity over time: sleep comfort first, simple product navigation second, and direct-to-door fulfilment as the final part of the experience.

What drives The Mulberry Silk Co is the belief that a good night’s rest begins with what touches the bed and surrounds the sleeper. The company’s promise is not about cluttering the experience with unnecessary claims; it is about offering a product range that feels aligned, useful, and comfortable from first click to first use. For customers, that means a more confident way to shop for silk bedding and a brand that keeps the focus where it belongs: on better sleep comfort at home.

What Makes The Brand Worth Choosing

Direct-to-door convenience

The business is built around the idea of shipping sleep comfort directly to customers. That makes the shopping experience especially appealing for people who want quality bedding without adding store visits or extra steps.

Focused silk bedding range

Instead of trying to be everything to everyone, The Mulberry Silk Co keeps attention on mulberry silk comfort products. This focused catalogue helps customers compare options more clearly and shop with more certainty.

Comfort-led product selection

Each named collection supports the same overall goal: a bedroom that feels softer, calmer, and more complete. Whether a customer chooses a single cover or a coordinated set, the emphasis remains on sleep comfort.
