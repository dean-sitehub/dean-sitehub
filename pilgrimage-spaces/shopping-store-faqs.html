Frequently Asked Questions

Can I order online?

Pilgrimage Spaces has an online shop pathway available through the linked shop page on this website. If you prefer guidance before proceeding, you can also enquire directly by phone or email about a specific furniture, decor, homeware, or textile item.

Do you offer delivery?

Delivery-related questions are best confirmed directly with the store, especially for larger handcrafted furniture pieces or orders going beyond Cape Town. Available review evidence indicates that an item was carefully packaged and delivered to Johannesburg, which suggests that distance orders can be discussed on enquiry.

What is the return policy?

A formal published return policy was not included in the available business details. If you want clarity on returns, exchanges, or conditions before purchasing, contact Pilgrimage Spaces directly so you can get the most accurate guidance for the item you are considering.

Do you stock a specific brand or product line?

The business is positioned around curated handcrafted artisan work rather than a broad list of named mass-market brands. The strongest confirmed categories are handcrafted furniture, decor and homeware, and textiles and soft furnishings, so it is best to ask directly if you are searching for a particular type of piece.

Can I reserve an item?

Reservation details have not been formally published in the source material. If you have found something you do not want to miss, contact the store as soon as possible and ask about the best way to secure or proceed with that item.

How do I know what will suit my space?

Pilgrimage Spaces is especially useful for customers who want interiors with soul, layered detail, and story-rich pieces. If you are unsure what category or style is right for your room, send an enquiry describing the space, your needs, and the overall feel you want to create. That makes it easier to receive focused help rather than making a rushed choice.

Still Need Help?

If your question is not covered here, the fastest route is to contact Pilgrimage Spaces directly. Whether you are choosing between categories, asking about delivery direction, or looking for a certain kind of handcrafted piece, a direct enquiry will help you move forward with confidence.
