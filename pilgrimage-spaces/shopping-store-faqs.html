Shopping & Store FAQs

Helpful answers for customers exploring handcrafted furniture, decor, homeware and textiles through Pilgrimage Spaces.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I order online?

Yes. Pilgrimage Spaces has an online shop page where you can continue to the hosted store for live shopping. If you would like help before ordering, the enquiry page and direct contact details are also available.

Do you offer delivery?

Customers can contact Pilgrimage Spaces directly to ask about delivery for a specific piece or order. Because furniture, decor and textiles can vary in size and handling needs, it is best to confirm delivery details during your enquiry.

What is the return policy?

No detailed return policy was supplied in the available business information. The best approach is to contact Pilgrimage Spaces before purchase if you need clarity on returns, exchanges or item suitability for your space.

Do you stock a specific brand or product line?

The business is positioned around handcrafted artisan work, story-rich pieces and curated living spaces rather than a generic multi-brand catalogue. If you are searching for a specific style within furniture, homeware or textiles, contact the store and describe what you need.

Can I reserve an item?

If there is a particular item you do not want to miss, get in touch as soon as possible. Reservation terms were not supplied in the business profile, so it is sensible to confirm directly with Pilgrimage Spaces.

How do I choose the right piece for my home?

Start by thinking about the room's role, the feeling you want to create and whether you need a statement furniture piece or softer finishing layers. Pilgrimage Spaces is especially suited to customers who want character, tactile detail and a more curated, collected look.
