Shopping & Store FAQs

Helpful answers for customers exploring handcrafted furniture, decor, homeware, and textiles from Pilgrimage Spaces.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I order online?

Yes. Pilgrimage Spaces has an online shop page that points customers to the hosted shop experience for browsing the current catalogue. If you want extra guidance before buying, you can also send an enquiry directly.

Do you offer delivery?

Delivery-related questions are best handled directly so the store can respond according to the item and destination. If you are considering a purchase, contact Pilgrimage Spaces with the product category you have in mind and your location for the most useful next step.

What is the return policy?

No detailed returns policy has been published on this site, so the best approach is to ask before purchase if you need clarity on a specific item. This is especially helpful for handcrafted furniture, decor, and textiles where care, suitability, and selection matter.

Do you stock a specific brand or product?

Pilgrimage Spaces is positioned around curated artisan work rather than a broad list of advertised brands. The store focuses on handcrafted furniture, decor and homeware, and textiles and soft furnishings selected for character, story, and interior warmth.

Can I reserve an item?

If there is a piece you are interested in, contact the store directly to discuss availability and the best way to proceed. Because the collection is curated, it is sensible to enquire promptly when something feels right for your space.

What kind of customer is Pilgrimage Spaces best for?

This store is especially suited to people who want interiors with depth, tactility, and a collected feel. If you are looking for handcrafted pieces with soul and a stronger sense of provenance than standard mass retail, you are in the right place.

Practical Shopping Guidance

When shopping for handcrafted interiors, it helps to think in layers rather than isolated items. Start with the furniture that sets the tone of the room, then refine the space with decor and homeware, and finally soften the environment with textiles. This approach makes it easier to create a balanced room where every piece feels connected.

If you are unsure which category to start with, browse the online shop for an overview and then enquire about anything that stands out. A direct message is often the best way to move from inspiration to a purchase decision with confidence.
