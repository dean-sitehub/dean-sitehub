How to Enquire

If you have seen a piece you love, want help selecting from the collection, or need guidance on combining furniture with decor and textiles, the best next step is to contact us directly. Pilgrimage Spaces is built around curated pieces with individual character, so enquiries are a practical way to ask about availability, finish, size, or styling direction.

You can email, call, or use the contact form on our home page to begin your enquiry. When you reach out, it helps to include the kind of piece you are looking for, where it will be used, and whether you are shopping for furniture, homeware, or soft furnishings. That gives us a clearer starting point and helps us respond with relevant guidance.

What to Include in Your Message

To make your enquiry more useful, mention which collection interests you most: handcrafted furniture, decor and homeware, or textiles and soft furnishings. You can also describe the room you are furnishing, the style you are drawn to, or the mood you want to create. That helps us keep the conversation focused on pieces that belong in your space rather than offering broad, unhelpful suggestions.
