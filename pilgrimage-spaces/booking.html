How enquiries work

Because the collection is curated around handcrafted furniture, decor, homeware, and textiles, the best starting point is a clear enquiry. You can get in touch if you are interested in a specific piece, want guidance on selecting complementary items, or need help understanding which category best suits your space. This page is designed as an enquiry point rather than a live calendar, so you can reach the business directly and receive a more considered response.

Tell us what caught your eye

Share the type of piece you are looking for, such as handcrafted furniture, decor accents, or soft furnishings. If you are building a room around texture, wood tones, or a story-rich statement item, mention that too so your enquiry is easier to guide.

Add room and sizing details

Practical information helps narrow the conversation quickly. Include approximate dimensions, the room you are styling, and whether you are looking for one anchor piece or a more layered combination of furniture and homeware.

Choose your preferred contact method

You can email, call, or send a message through the enquiry form. That makes it easy to begin in the way that feels most comfortable, while keeping the enquiry focused on real products and real guidance.
